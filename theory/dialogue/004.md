## The theory program

### Minimal definitions

1. **Raw presented world**

   A world consists initially of presentation fibers \(Z_p\), indexed by presentations \(p\), rather than one already-quotiented \(X\).

2. **Response laws**

   Each admissible probe \(c\) supplies a response law

   \[
   r_{p,c}:Z_p\rightarrow O_c,
   \]

   where \(O_c\) may be a finite observation or an outcome distribution. Neighborhoods can later be derived from equality or declared divergence of these laws.

3. **Typed legal moves**

   A primitive move \(a\) is a total or partial map between specified fibers. A move word \(w=a_k\cdots a_1\) is legal only when the types compose. Undefined execution is recorded as an outcome rather than silently discarded.

4. **Future observational signature**

   For horizon \(h\),

   \[
   \Theta_h(x)=
   \left(r_c(w x)\right)_{\substack{|w|\le h\\c\in C}},
   \]

   including the empty word.

5. **Operational place at horizon \(h\)**

   \[
   x\equiv_h y
   \iff
   \Theta_h(x)=\Theta_h(y).
   \]

   The quotient is \(Q_h=Z/{\equiv_h}\). This is exact theory; learned continuous systems later receive tolerance-based congruence certificates, not threshold relations falsely treated as exact equivalences.

6. **Passive presentation morphism versus active rebinding**

   A passive presentation morphism transports states, probes, and action names while preserving response laws and move composition. Such morphisms form a small groupoid of presentations. An operation that changes \(\delta(p,t)\) while holding the symbol fixed is an active rebinding move, not automatically a symmetry.

### First theorem: finite-horizon identity and action descent

**Theorem.** Let \(Z\) be a finite deterministic latent transition system with a finite set \(A\) of total primitive moves and a finite response family \(C\). Define \(\equiv_h\) by equality of all responses after all action words of length at most \(h\). Then:

1. \(\equiv_{h+1}\) refines \(\equiv_h\).
2. Every primitive action \(a\) induces a well-defined map
   \[
   \bar a_h:Q_{h+1}\rightarrow Q_h,
   \qquad
   [x]_{h+1}\mapsto[ax]_h.
   \]
3. All primitive actions descend as endomorphisms \(Q_h\to Q_h\) if and only if
   \[
   \equiv_h=\equiv_{h+1}.
   \]
4. Because \(Z\) is finite, the refinement sequence stabilizes after at most \(|Z|-1\) strict refinements. The stable quotient is the coarsest response-preserving congruence for the action system.

**Proof strategy.**

- Refinement is immediate because every word of length at most \(h\) also occurs at horizon \(h+1\).
- If \(x\equiv_{h+1}y\), then for every continuation \(w\) of length at most \(h\), the words \(wa\) have length at most \(h+1\). Hence \(r_c(wax)=r_c(way)\), so \(ax\equiv_h ay\).
- If \(\equiv_h=\equiv_{h+1}\), the preceding implication makes each action well-defined on \(Q_h\).
- Conversely, if every action respects \(\equiv_h\), equality through horizon \(h\) propagates through every first action and then every continuation of length \(h\), giving equality at horizon \(h+1\).
- A strict refinement increases the number of equivalence classes; a finite carrier permits at most \(|Z|-1\) such increases.
- Any response-preserving action congruence implies agreement after every action word, so it refines the stable relation.

This is recognizable as a finite Nerode/bisimulation theorem; novelty is not claimed. Its value here is foundational: it derives the denizen’s notion of place and legal quotient moves without coordinates.

**Assumptions.** Finite carrier; deterministic, totalized action table; declared response family; exact response equality.

**What would falsify it.** A finite counterexample satisfying those assumptions in which \(x\equiv_{h+1}y\) but \(ax\not\equiv_h ay\), or in which \(\equiv_h=\equiv_{h+1}\) yet some action is not well-defined on \(Q_h\). Existing toy failures do not falsify the theorem; they show that the chosen shallow quotient had not stabilized.

**So what.** *An inhabitant can know when two hidden situations are truly the same place: they remain indistinguishable after every move the inhabitant can still make.*

The proof is pencil-and-paper. Existing Round 36/37 response tables can illustrate the refinement filtration, but no new model run is required.

### Second theorem target: presentation covariance or rebinding

**Statement.** If a passive presentation morphism \(g\) preserves response laws and commutes with primitive moves, then for every \(h\),

\[
x\equiv_h y
\iff
gx\equiv_h gy,
\]

and \(g\) induces an isomorphism \(Q_h^p\cong Q_h^{gp}\) conjugating all descended moves.

**Proof strategy.** Induct on action-word length using response naturality and move equivariance.

**Assumptions.** An explicitly declared bijection of fibers, probes, outcomes, and action names satisfying the two commutation laws.

**Falsifier.** One declared passive presentation pair for which a response or descended action fails to commute. That would either reject L5 for that map or show that the proposed pair was not actually presentation-equivalent.

**So what.** *Changing how a place is written should not change where it is; changing what the writing means is a genuine move.*

The paired preflight features can serve as a finite example of the distinction between passive relabeling and active rebinding, again without a model run.

### Immediate corollary program

Once the first theorem is established:

- **Move:** a primitive operation becomes a move on places only when it descends.
- **Map:** the induced \(\bar a:Q\to Q\) is the native map of that move.
- **Law:** an equation among induced maps—commutation, involution, conjugacy, or composition—is a native law when it holds on the quotient and is preserved by presentation morphisms.
- **Cost:** defer. Only after executable move programs and quotient maps exist should cost be assigned to programs. A directed endpoint cost may then be derived by minimizing over programs, but a norm, geodesic, or symmetric distance must not be assumed.

For learned continuous systems, do not turn an \(\varepsilon\)-similarity relation directly into a quotient: threshold similarity is generally non-transitive. The next mathematical object should instead be an **approximate congruence certificate for a proposed partition**, reporting within-place response spread, move leakage, between-place margins, horizon, and presentation covariance separately.

\sup_{w,c}D_c(r_c(wx),r_c(wy)).
\]

1. \(d_\infty\) is a pseudometric.
2. Every legal move is \(1\)-Lipschitz.
3. \(x\sim y\iff d_\infty(x,y)=0\) is an equivalence relation.
4. The quotient \(Q=Z/{\sim}\) carries the metric
   \[
   \bar d([x],[y])=d_\infty(x,y),
   \]
   and every legal move descends to a nonexpansive map on \(Q\).
5. More strongly, \(d_\infty\) is the **least pseudometric dominating immediate response discrepancy for which every legal move is nonexpansive**.

The proof of minimality is short. If \(p\geq\rho\) and every action is nonexpansive for \(p\), then for every word \(w\),

\[
\rho(wx,wy)\leq p(wx,wy)\leq p(x,y).
\]

Taking the supremum gives \(d_\infty(x,y)\leq p(x,y)\).

So I accept \(d_\infty\) as the foundational object. There is no finite-deterministic reason it loses: in that setting,

\[
d_h(x,y)=0\iff \Theta_h(x)=\Theta_h(y),
\]

so Round 31’s signature quotients are exactly the zero-kernels of the truncated metrics. On a finite \(N\)-state deterministic system, \(d_h=d_\infty\) after the synchronously reachable pair graph has been exhausted—at worst \(N^2-1\) steps. The earlier \(N-1\) bound concerns strict partition refinements, not stabilization of all metric values.

The corrections:

- **Discounting is not automatically nonexpansive.** For
  \[
  d_\gamma(x,y)=\sup_w\gamma^{|w|}\rho(wx,wy),
  \]
  one gets
  \[
  d_\gamma(ax,ay)\leq\gamma^{-1}d_\gamma(x,y),
  \]
  not \(d_\gamma(ax,ay)\leq d_\gamma(x,y)\). Its zero-kernel is still the same as \(d_\infty\), because every word receives positive weight, so descent survives but \(1\)-Lipschitzness does not.

- **Finite resources are not only resolution.** Statistical limitations produce an \(\varepsilon\); temporal or energetic limitations can also restrict executable word length. Thus \(d_\infty\) is the exact ontic object, while \(d_h\) and \(d_\gamma\) remain resource-indexed approximants unless the world itself supplies a canonical discount.

- **Move leakage is not always zero operationally.** Metric-ball diameter cannot increase under exact \(d_\infty\), but a proposed partition cell need not be a metric ball, and truncated or estimated distances need not be closed under moves. Cell leakage can therefore remain even when the theorem’s Lipschitz defect is zero.

Finally, \(d_\infty\) is **response distinguishability, not effort**. It supports quotient maps and laws, but latent cost still requires separate resource weights on executable programs.

What breaks: partial actions require common domains or observable failure-state totalization; genuinely stochastic state transitions require a distributional/Kantorovich lifting rather than treating \(ax\) as a point.

**Accept.**

\[

\qquad

\[

so

\[
r_c(T_wx)-r_c(T_wy)
=

\]

Therefore

\[
d(x,y)=p(x-y),
\qquad
p(v)=\sup_{w,c}\alpha_w\lVert W_cA_wv\rVert_c.
\]

When finite, \(p\) is a seminorm. Its kernel is

\[
K=\bigcap_{w,c}\ker(W_cA_w),
\]

and the operational places are precisely the affine cosets \(x+K\). Intrinsically, the native linear object is the normed quotient \(V/K\).

After choosing an ambient inner product,

\[
\mathcal O
=
K^\perp
=
\operatorname{span}_{w,c}
\operatorname{range}(A_w^\top W_c^\top),
\]

and for the orthogonal projector \(P_{\mathcal O}\),

\[
p(v)=p(P_{\mathcal O}v).
\]

But the projector is chart-dependent; the kernel and quotient are the intrinsic objects.

The important correction is that the supremum generally produces an **arbitrary seminorm**, not a Euclidean seminorm. A discounted \(\ell_2\) aggregate,

\[
p_{\gamma,2}(v)^2
=
\sum_{w,c}\beta_{w,c}^2
\lVert W_cA_wv\rVert_2^2,
\]

when convergent, gives

\[
p_{\gamma,2}(v)^2=v^\top Gv,
\qquad
G=\sum_{w,c}\beta_{w,c}^2
A_w^\top W_c^\top W_cA_w.
\]

That is a Hilbert seminorm.

A \(P\)-adapted chart merely separates observable and unobservable directions. Ambient Euclidean distance agrees with the Gramian seminorm up to scale only when

\[
G=\lambda P_{\mathcal O}.
\]

Cosine requires still more: normalization plus that isotropy condition. Therefore the theorem does **not** precisely explain the DINOv2 result. It offers a possible mechanism—training may have approximately aligned or whitened a response-observable chart—but that remains an empirical hypothesis. The measured DINOv2 moves and readouts were not established as an affine-linear observability system.

For nonlinear moves, \(T_w(x)-T_w(y)\) is no longer a function only of \(x-y\); observable directions become orbit- and basepoint-dependent.

**Accept.**

1. primitives are defined through denizen-accessible actions and response laws; and
