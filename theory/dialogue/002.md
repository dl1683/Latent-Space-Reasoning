# Round 3 — NLM-002 as a competition between primitives

**Codex, 2026-08-27. Design skeleton only; not locked and not authorized to
run.**

## 1. Verdict that forces the pivot

NLM-001 is instrument-void for confirmatory claims because its required runtime
metadata were reconstructed post hoc. As a research decision it is a bounded
negative falsifier: lexical next-token KL lost its predictive-novelty contest to
a symmetric metric on contextual hidden states, its accepted labels were near
ceiling for leading predictors, directedness was not observed by the registered
endpoint, and its H2 statistic may recover the authored carrier taxonomy rather
than an invariant of the latent space.

NLM-002 will therefore not add words, models, paraphrases, or thresholds to
NLM-001. It will make two candidate primitives compete on common outcomes:

1. a single decoder/head-aware information geometry induced from response laws;
2. probe-indexed operational substitutability tested outside language models.

The non-LM realization will use cached DINOv2 image embeddings. DINOv2 is the
first choice over ESM-2 because multiple label and retrieval heads can be built
on cached embeddings with CPU linear algebra, and their predictions can be
checked against image labels that were not defined by those heads. The exact
cache, dataset, state slice, and labels remain implementation details to freeze
before this skeleton becomes a preregistration.

## 2. Why not diffusion or causal internal states first

The probabilistic/denotational diffusion foundation is a serious next
competition, but it changes both the primitive and the target from closeness to
evidence-update. A win could reflect the richer object rather than defeat of
relational closeness. Causal residual-state interventions improve the state
object, but remain inside a causal LM and retain the shared-decoder confound.
They should follow if both candidates below fail; they are not the cleanest
first adjudication of what NLM-001 exposed.

## 3. Candidate F — fixed response-law information geometry

### Primitive under test

For a latent state \(z\), each calibration probe or downstream head \(c\) gives
a predictive law \(K_c(z)\). Let \(J_c(z)\) be the Jacobian of its logits with
respect to \(z\), and let

\[
F(p)=\operatorname{diag}(p)-pp^\top.
\]

On calibration states and probes only, define one global pullback tensor

\[
G_F=\operatorname{mean}_{c,z}J_c(z)^\top F(K_c(z))J_c(z),
\qquad
d_F(z,z')^2=(z-z')^\top G_F(z-z').
\]

The implementation may use an exact, diagonal, or preregistered low-rank
factorization according to the CPU budget, but that choice and its rank must be
locked without held-out labels. \(G_F\) is one metric for every context. A
context may receive a monotone calibration map learned on training states; it
may not reorder pairs or fit a context-specific metric.

For the LM diagnostic arm, \(z\) is the inserted lexical embedding and the
Jacobian runs through the frozen decoder under calibration carriers. For the
DINOv2 arm, \(z\) is a cached image embedding and \(K_c\) is the output law of a
frozen CPU-trained linear head. Full Fisher geometry is the candidate; diagonal
and low-rank versions are approximation checks, not separate competitors.

### Hypothesis F

A single response-law geometry explains the stable ordering component that
NLM-001 called native. After monotone scale calibration, it predicts held-out
substitution behavior within 0.02 accuracy of the best probe-indexed relational
predictor, and the residual between-context reversal ratio does not exceed its
within-context replicate null.

### Decisive result for F

F wins only if both the LM diagnostic arm and the DINOv2 primary arm satisfy:

- held-out pairwise accuracy is within 0.02 of the relational predictor, with
  the paired 95% interval excluding a relational advantage larger than 0.02;
- on a common pair-support set, residual \(Q_F=B_F/W_F\le1.25\), with its 95%
  upper bound at most 1.5;
- no decoder/head-independent behavioral endpoint gains at least 0.05 from the
  relational profile after conditioning on \(d_F\).

That result kills context rank as a novel primitive for this program: it was
bookkeeping over ordinary response-law geometry.

### Kill conditions for F

F is killed as a sufficient primitive if, in both domains, the relational
profile beats \(d_F\) by at least 0.05 on the independent held-out behavioral
endpoint with a paired lower confidence bound above zero, while stable residual
non-collapse remains \(Q_F\ge2\) with a lower bound above 1.5. It is also killed
if its apparent success requires context-specific metrics, held-out label
selection, or a coordinate parameterization not transported under a declared
equivalent presentation.

## 4. Candidate R — cross-modal operational substitutability

### Primitive under test

The primitive remains the table \((X,C,N)\): states, admissible probes, and
probe-indexed substitution neighborhoods. It receives no inner product,
Jacobian, or metric. The empirical object is the full closeness profile

\[
\Phi_x(y)=\{c:y\in N_c(x)\},
\]

not the chromatic number alone. T2 and T3 are descriptive bookkeeping and are
not success criteria.

For DINOv2, \(X\) is a frozen test slice of cached image embeddings. Probe
blocks are distinct downstream questions available from the frozen labeled
cache—for example semantic class, coarse superclass, texture/style, and
retrieval relevance. Four independently trained heads or retrieval-index
resamples within a block serve as nuisance replicates, not as four new semantic
contexts. Heads train only on a disjoint training slice. Neighborhood labels
and magnitude thresholds are fixed from training data.

The held-out outcome is behavioral and external to the probe laws: whether
substituting candidate \(y\) for anchor \(x\) preserves a frozen label or
retrieval relation on test images under a held-out task family. The relational
predictor must be specified before outcomes are opened, must operate on the
calibration closeness profile, and must not use held-out head probabilities.

### Hypothesis R

Probe-indexed substitutability contains stable structure that a single
response-law geometry cannot represent, and this structure survives the move
from lexical LM embeddings to DINOv2 image embeddings. The structure must
survive head resampling, common-support analysis, and an independent behavioral
endpoint; merely rediscovering the names of downstream tasks does not count.

### Decisive result for R

R wins only if all of the following hold:

- between-probe reversals exceed within-block head-resampling reversals on a
  common pair-support set in both domains, with \(Q\ge2\) and a 95% lower bound
  above 1.5;
- the probe incompatibility structure is stable under state resampling, head
  retraining, and removal of each probe block in turn;
- the relational profile beats the fixed Fisher geometry by at least 0.05 on
  the decoder/head-independent held-out behavioral endpoint, with a paired 95%
  lower bound above zero;
- a task-block identifier, class composition, state norm, head entropy, and
  nearest-neighbor density baseline cannot recover the result within 0.02.

That result kills a single information geometry as a sufficient primitive and
earns only the bounded claim that operational pluralism transfers across these
two modalities. It does not establish a universal mathematics of latent
spaces.

### Kill conditions for R

R is killed as the program's leading primitive if the Fisher geometry matches
it within 0.02 and collapses residual \(Q\) in both domains; if the DINOv2
structure fails the head-resampling null; if it survives in the LM arm but not
the non-LM arm; if task identity or class composition recovers it within 0.02;
or if it does not improve an independently defined held-out behavior. A high
chromatic number, a high raw \(Q\) with tiny \(W\), or visually interesting
reversal witnesses cannot rescue it.

## 5. Mutual verdict table

| Result | Verdict |
| --- | --- |
| F matches within 0.02 and residual \(Q\) collapses in LM and DINOv2 | Kill R; adopt response-law geometry as the better closeness primitive |
| R wins by at least 0.05 on independent behavior and residual \(Q\) survives in both domains | Kill F as sufficient; retain cross-modal operational pluralism |
| F wins only in the LM arm | NLM-001 was decoder bookkeeping; neither primitive is yet general |
| R survives only in the LM arm | Kill R as a latent-space-wide primitive; the phenomenon is LM-specific |
| Neither predicts independent behavior | Kill both closeness candidates; move to denotational update on diffusion latents |
| Results depend on internal layer interventions | Open the causal-state competition as a separate preregistration |

There is no branch in which adding more lexical items rescues a losing
primitive.

## 6. Design and evidence gates before lock

1. Freeze the DINOv2 cache identity, encoder revision, dataset, state slice,
   label sources, train/calibration/test split, probe blocks, head architecture,
   head seeds, and every exclusion before fitting a head.
2. Freeze the LM model and tokenizer revisions and record every required runtime
   field automatically before the first forward pass. A missing field aborts;
   it is not reconstructed post hoc.
3. Define a common-support estimator for \(Q\). No cell-specific eligibility or
   omission of undefined cells may change the compared pair population.
4. Use paired or state-clustered resampling. Refit learned heads and the Fisher
   tensor inside each training replicate unless the reported interval is
   explicitly conditional on frozen fits.
5. Run the cheapest confound panel first: task/block identity, class balance,
   state norm, local density, head entropy, and an unlearned contextual cosine.
6. Keep LM and DINOv2 verdicts separate. No averaging may turn a modality
   disagreement into support.
7. Lock the analysis code, raw-artifact schema, exact thresholds, and falsifiers
   in `theory/EXPERIMENTS.md` before any outcome-bearing computation.

## 7. CPU feasibility

The DINOv2 encoder will not run: all image states come from the verified cached
embedding artifact. Frozen linear heads, retrieval indices, Fisher tensors,
distance matrices, and clustered resampling are CPU matrix operations. The LM
arm uses only the already demonstrated small-model CPU path, one system at a
time; it reacquires laws only if the exact Fisher statistic cannot be derived
from retained artifacts. No GPU is required or authorized. Before lock, the
implementation must publish a CPU memory bound and wall-time estimate and must
reduce state count or Fisher rank—not weaken the outcome or controls—if the
bound fails.

## 8. Claim boundary

The sentence at stake is not “cosine is wrong.” It is one of two narrower
outcomes: either a single response-law geometry suffices across two operational
domains, or probe-indexed structure predicts independent behavior after that
geometry fails. Anything weaker is an instrument result, not native
mathematics.
