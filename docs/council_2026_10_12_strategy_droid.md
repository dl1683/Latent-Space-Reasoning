## LSR strategy council, 2026-10-12

1. **Keep the central bet, change the unit of evidence.** LSR’s consequential claim is that one latent state can preserve several *independently selectable futures*, survive a selective edit and further updates, and do something a storage-only control cannot. The registered flagship says exactly that; the high-dimensional interference theorem predicts a crossing only for its specified random-feature readout, **not** LLM answer capacity ([LATENT_BRANCH_CAPACITY.md:8–9,47–68](theory/LATENT_BRANCH_CAPACITY.md#L8-L68); [HPE_AGGREGATE_INTERFERENCE.md:241–257](theory/HPE_AGGREGATE_INTERFERENCE.md#L241-L257)). By **distinct study question since the frozen-model step-back**, I count **one direct attempt** at state-only transfer, Q, versus **seven** local-patch, readout, reader-control or ablation questions. That is approximately **7:1 indirect-to-direct work**, not a measured code-line ratio. Q’s direct interface lacked a passing positive control, so the number of successful direct tests is **zero**. Earlier, the gate and P2 did test updates more directly and missed their registered criteria ([EXPERIMENTS.md:20–105](experiments/EXPERIMENTS.md#L20-L105); [ledger.jsonl:1115–1129](experiments/ledger.jsonl#L1115-L1129)). The answer is not more elaborate probing. It is to make *future behavior after source removal* the primary observable again.

2. **Correct the live reading before choosing a model.** The closing state supports **slot-indexed colour recovery**, including after rename and reorder; name-query readers were inadequate. That does **not** establish that name-keyed information is absent. The value-token intervention is causal but its effect is explained by fitted colour-pair means, not a contextual edit dimension ([EXPERIMENTS.md:8–40](experiments/EXPERIMENTS.md#L8-L40); [codex_bindq_smoke_hunt.md:1–7](docs/council_2026_10_11/codex_bindq_smoke_hunt.md#L1-L7); [codex_slotctl_hunt.md:1–7](docs/council_2026_10_11/codex_slotctl_hunt.md#L1-L7)). The initial name ablation met its 0.10 k=4 magnitude bar only in W2, with different selected layers ([codex_nameabl_hunt.md:1–7](docs/council_2026_10_11/codex_nameabl_hunt.md#L1-L7)). **Newer, untracked fixed-layer output is already present:** at layer 18 the k=4 drops are 0.069 and 0.080, both below 0.10; at layer 9 they are 0.084 and 0.102. All four reported intervals exclude zero, but this output has not passed the required fresh result hunt and is **provisional**, not a new verdict ([nameabl_8b_fixedlayer_diff.txt:1–8](experiments/results/cloud/nameabl_8b_fixedlayer_diff.txt#L1-L8); [AGENTS.md:8–11](AGENTS.md#L8-L11)). The Stateful product’s Chinese-origin exclusion need **not automatically govern research subjects**: keep pinned Qwen as a diagnostic comparator, but require the decisive result on a non-Chinese model before arguing portability or product relevance. Start with **Mistral**, already in LSR’s model panel; consider **Gemma 4, Nemotron and Muse Glimmer 30B** after verifying the exact checkpoint, provenance, licence and hardware fit. **gpt-oss**, and Gemma under the existing wording, require an explicit change to the frozen program’s model exclusions before a new registered run ([LATENT_BRANCH_CAPACITY.md:11–13,72–75](theory/LATENT_BRANCH_CAPACITY.md#L11-L75)). None of these recommendations puts Qwen in Stateful.

3. **Radical direction A, a *future-equivalence atlas* rather than a colour probe.** **Hypothesis:** two histories that look identical now can retain different *sets of answerable future questions*, and an intervention can selectively remove one future without disturbing the others. That is a native behavioral quotient, not a cosine or PCA claim ([CONTINUATION_REFINEMENT.md:50–52,70–96,98–132](theory/CONTINUATION_REFINEMENT.md#L50-L132)). Combine Genome’s whole-state readout and drift dissection with matrix-native composition of actions, then test the quotient in a real reasoner ([CROSS_PROJECT_INSIGHTS.md:64–80](theory/CROSS_PROJECT_INSIGHTS.md#L64-L80)). **Smallest decisive test:** on one frozen 8B-class model, form paired *unresolved* constraint states; discard source text and donor KV; choose one of four future constraints only afterward. Compare single-position, declared multi-position, shuffled-state, explicit-cache and recomputation receivers. A separate smoke must first make k=1 state-only retrieval work. Then test selective substitution at k=4 on held-out compositions, not merely held-out names. **Compute ceiling:** $700, with roughly $75 for admission before confirmation or a second architecture. **Success:** four futures remain answerable from the declared state, with targeted changes, low collateral and a lead over explicit storage at matched resources. **Failure:** if k=1 cannot cross the interface, pivot the *seat or receiver*; if only multiple positions work, map a distributed-state quotient instead. Q’s failed closing-position transplant and successful value-token hook make that distinction essential ([native.py:47–66,193–229,350–402](experiments/cloud/lsr_jobs/native.py#L47-L402); [EXPERIMENTS.md:50–64](experiments/EXPERIMENTS.md#L50-L64)).

4. **Radical direction B, a behavioral width–precision phase diagram.** **Hypothesis:** future-answer capacity depends on *both* available state channels and numerical precision, with a crossover that an ordinary “number of dimensions” story misses. Matrix Native Math explicitly treats operation cost and precision as separate axes; Genome warns that apparent state spread can be the isotropic null ([MATRIX_NATIVE_INFORMATION_THEORY.md:46–59](../matrix-native-math/research/MATRIX_NATIVE_INFORMATION_THEORY.md#L46-L59); [CROSS_PROJECT_INSIGHTS.md:16–35](theory/CROSS_PROJECT_INSIGHTS.md#L16-L35)). **Smallest decisive test:** only after A has a competent state-only interface, preregister $C(r,b,\varepsilon,T)$, the number of future branches meeting an accuracy tolerance after $T$ updates. Vary controlled width and 2/3/4/8-bit precision within one backbone. Compare rotated and unrotated quantization, equal nominal bit budgets, explicit packing, clipping and batch-shape nulls; measure *answers*, not PR alone. **Compute ceiling:** $600. **Success:** a replicated action-dependent crossover, with a bound or constructive counterexample explaining why equal-bit states support different future operations. **Failure:** if explicit packing or clipping explains the curve, publish the scoped constraint and use it to redesign the state, not to infer an LLM capacity law. The current 3-bit observation and low PR do not identify the limiter ([STATE.md:28–29](STATE.md#L28-L29); [CROSS_PROJECT_INSIGHTS.md:82–85,139–142](theory/CROSS_PROJECT_INSIGHTS.md#L82-L85)).

5. **Radical direction C, the algebra of updates.** **Hypothesis:** the decisive obstruction is not storing a fact but keeping the *same future distinctions* after actions compose. Genome’s dissection distinguishes formation collisions from established-read transport; Matrix Native Math suggests asking whether a family of composed operators preserves structure or accumulates disorder. Neither result transfers to LSR without intervention ([THE_LAWS_V3.md:26–53](../AI%20Moonshots/moonshot-llm-genome/THE_LAWS_V3.md#L26-L53); [CROSS_PROJECT_INSIGHTS.md:75–80](theory/CROSS_PROJECT_INSIGHTS.md#L75-L80)). **Smallest decisive test:** from a competent state, apply two independent edits in both orders, an edit then its undo, and matched sham passages. Query *all* affected and unaffected futures after each step. Compare the resulting behavioral commutator and closure defect with an explicit key-value store and identity-plus-shared-displacement null. Require that each edit changes its own answer first; a constant-answer system cannot pass by commuting trivially. **Compute ceiling:** $800. **Success:** reproducible, approximately commuting selective updates with a bounded defect across new compositions, yielding a falsifiable action law. **Failure:** identify the shortest separating future and the first layer or position where closure breaks; use that diagnosis to design a different update path. P2’s lower geometric defect without adequate answerability is the warning against another MSE-only result ([EXPERIMENTS.md:81–90](experiments/EXPERIMENTS.md#L81-L90); [CONTINUATION_REFINEMENT.md:119–148](theory/CONTINUATION_REFINEMENT.md#L119-L148)).

6. **Radical direction D, build the missing substrate, but force it to beat a cache.** **Hypothesis:** a *structured, query-addressable update operator* can turn the present slot-readable but name-query-inadequate traces into an update-closed reasoning state. Use Genome’s formation-versus-transport dissection to choose the intervention seat, Matrix Native Math’s composition/precision accounting to specify the operator, and LSR’s future-equivalence tests as the loss and verdict, not a prettier linear probe ([CROSS_PROJECT_INSIGHTS.md:49–80,95–100](theory/CROSS_PROJECT_INSIGHTS.md#L49-L100); [bindq.py:76–127,250–294](experiments/cloud/lsr_jobs/bindq.py#L76-L294)). **Smallest decisive test:** train a small adapter with *late, unseen entity queries* and two sequential edits; compare a structured operator, a plain adapter, an equally budgeted explicit cache/GRU, and delayed text readout. First smoke own-write versus counterfactual on training items, then change **one** difficulty at a time through novel names and compositions. **Compute ceiling:** $1,350, conditional on A or C supplying a working causal seat; three seeds only after the proximal smoke. **Success:** selective edit/undo/advance at k≥4 on held-out compositions, without source access, and an advantage over the best storage-only control at accounted training and inference cost. That could support a substantial result. **Failure:** if the cache matches it, the result is an honest architectural baseline; use the failing future and Genome-style transport diagnosis to redesign, rather than calling ordinary activation engineering native mathematics ([LATENT_BRANCH_CAPACITY.md:42–51,85–89](theory/LATENT_BRANCH_CAPACITY.md#L42-L89); [AGENTS.md:145–174](AGENTS.md#L145-L174)).

7. **What is going wrong:** the program keeps converting a failed causal interface into a better measurement of that interface. R’s rank curve, N’s reader adequacy, the slot control and the name-count re-score were justified *diagnostics*, but another layer/name/model sweep would extend the **7:1** ratio without testing selectable future computation. A probe can read slot colours at the closing token while a single closing-token additive patch changes no answers; these are compatible observations, not a paradox to solve by probe selection ([EXPERIMENTS.md:8–41](experiments/EXPERIMENTS.md#L8-L41); [readout.py:60–107](experiments/cloud/lsr_jobs/readout.py#L60-L107)). **Change the decision order:** define the future-action theorem or counterexample first, smoke a behavior-changing intervention, then spend on its held-out test. Make name/slot probes secondary localization tools. Do not revive vanilla latent ES, frozen looping, or the original graph curriculum without a new mechanism: their registered comparisons missed, and formation F’s positive control failed ([EXPERIMENTS.md:66–145](experiments/EXPERIMENTS.md#L66-L145)). A negative should name the replacement mechanism. It must not end the mission.
