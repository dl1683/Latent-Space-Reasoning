# Structured Negative: What Two Days of Audited Latent-Space Experiments Localized

## Abstract

Across 2026-08-29–30, the program moved from toy certification to causal intervention in a real model and ended with a structured negative, not a no-go theorem. The Rounds 36–37 toy quotient program closed because no learned artifact passed the complete exact reducer, only the oracle fixture did, and the carrier comparison showed no architectural win; its durable lesson was that exact certificates are diagnostics for learned continuous systems, not verdicts, and toy results do not establish properties of real residual streams. The frozen-residual line—`coordinate_v1–v3`, `interchange_v1–v2`, `state_bus_v1r1`, and `control_cost_v1`—encountered invalid instruments, late lexical steering, a fixed-site null, a repeatedly injected supervised controller, and a failed fixed control-cost construction. It therefore stopped as an allocation pivot rather than a scientific conclusion. The positive-control staircase then localized the one-write failure: `onewrite_recall_v1` failed held-out recall on a valid direct-copy instrument; rung 1 failed proximal control on training entities; source localization found weak tag information at the generic source anchor; the oracle-code actuator failed balanced eight-way control even without an encoder; the site oracle found construction-bound HESK/VORN sensitivity but not a balanced channel; and `reachability_v1` found no slot-specific geometry relative to position and prompt controls. No native latent mathematics or transferable causal state was demonstrated, but the sequence produced concrete requirements that a future substrate must satisfy before stronger claims are tested.

## Findings that survive audit

- “Under the current apparatus budget, this sequence of frozen residual-stream constructions is not yielding a native-mathematics artifact, so open-ended layer, task, and decision-rule repair stops here. This is an allocation pivot, not evidence that frozen pretrained residual streams lack usable native structure.”

- `state_bus_v1r1` licensed “repeatedly maintained supervised response control with donor-verbalizer-specific lexical/semantic steering,” not autonomous persistence, abstraction, general interchangeability, or native latent mathematics.

- `onewrite_recall_v1` closes “the fixed model/layer/source-state/16-dimensional encoder/injector/norm-cap/slot/objective/400-step/prompt construction—not one-write memory, persistence, block-12 capacity, or persistent state in real models generally.”

- For the rung-0/site-oracle pair: “At this exact site and prompt, finite strict-choice response is strongly tag- and context-dependent. Removing the learned shared J does not remove the concentration of effects, so J optimization was not its sole source; nevertheless, the concentration remains inseparable from baseline logit gaps, cross-name gradient averaging, local-to-finite extrapolation, the fixed norm, and the first-token greedy readout.” `reachability_v1` then classified the measured response as **NO SLOT-SPECIFIC GEOMETRY CONCLUSION** because the slot spectrum was not distinguished from generic-position nulls and changed under tag-order prompt controls.

The strongest unified explanation is correspondingly local and plural. Several frozen-model effects are parsimoniously explained by cue-redundant lexical-semantic response geometry rather than persistent state. Small base models could not support the more demanding rule-application instruments, although direct visible recall was valid. The chosen generic source anchor carried only weak tag information. Finally, the actuator’s apparent anisotropy was construction-bound and was not isolated from prompt, label-order, baseline-gap, or output-boundary geometry. These are four constraints, not one universal latent-space hole.

## Methodological result and cost

The positive-control staircase was the decisive process improvement. It prevented held-out names, unseen wording, and long delays from concealing a proximal failure, and it separated instrument validity, source extraction, actuation, propagation, and readout instead of naming whichever downstream component was most convenient after a failure.

Three other governance rules mattered:

- Exact certificates remained diagnostics; primary judgments used tolerances, effect sizes, controls, and seed spread.
- One fresh audit governed each result and corrected overclaims without spawning sentence-by-sentence repair runs.
- Position, prompt, visible-copy, cue, counterfactual, random, and zero-hook controls were treated as parts of the scientific object rather than post-hoc explanations.

The cost was high. With the single audit attached to `reachability_v1`, the declared closeout accounting is 15 measurement or governance rounds to five artifact-building rounds, or 3.0:1. That exceeds the warning threshold. The correct response is this consolidation and closure, not another site, layer, model-size, or actuator repair.

## Requirements for a future substrate

A successor must satisfy these requirements in order:

1. Its behavioral readout must be valid on the unmodified base: visible information must produce the required behavior with adequate completion, while the cue-only arm must not.
2. The chosen source representation must demonstrably contain the fact under held-out source wording before an encoder is credited with extracting it.
3. A fixed oracle code must pass bounded, proximal behavioral control against wrong-code, random-code, cue, and zero-hook arms before source encoding is introduced.
4. Exactly one difficulty—delay, names, wording, or consequence family—may change at each staircase rung.
5. Primary evidence must be actual decoded behavior or another independently valid behavioral outcome, not likelihood uplift alone.
6. Site-specific geometry requires matched position and prompt/output controls. Neither an effective rank nor favored decoded labels may be called intrinsic to a site when generic positions or tag-order permutations reproduce the effect.
7. Norms, cap activation, causal distance, and pre/post intervention telemetry must be recorded so optimization, dose, site reachability, and retrieval are not conflated.

## What remains open

The mission still asks whether a denizen of a real latent space has stable notions of place, move, effort, composition, prediction, and causal state. These experiments do not determine whether such structure is distributed across positions or trajectories, exists at another layer or site, appears in another model family or scale, requires a context-conditioned actuator, or must be introduced by training. The synthetic navigator also remains an unrun calibration control, not evidence about pretrained language models.

Never say:

- Frozen pretrained residual streams lack usable native structure or native mathematics.
- Persistent state must be built rather than discovered.
- The toy and real-model programs exposed the same latent-space hole.
- Block 12 cannot support memory, reachability, or an eight-way channel.
- HESK and VORN define two reachable dimensions or an intrinsic subspace.
- The intervention wrote a fact that was later forgotten.
- The state bus demonstrated persistence, abstraction, or general interchangeability.
- `coordinate_v3` demonstrated a grammatical coordinate.
- `reachability_v1` established a low-dimensional slot or a general early-position geometry.
- More steps, another site, a larger model, or a different actuator would either succeed or fail.
