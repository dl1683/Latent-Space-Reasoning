# Train the maintenance swarm to invent its own operations

27 November 2026. Design decision / necessary mathematical foundation, based on `fa4f57d2`. Public numerical audit executed; learning proposal not run. This is a parallel research direction, not an amendment of the frozen ordinary or operation gate.

## Decision: make the world an evolving library, not a fixed predictor

The new [system design](LATENT_WORLD_SYSTEM_DESIGN.md) establishes a common WorldPin/WorldView and treats the revision-conditioned predictive candidate as a third reasoning mechanism. I accept that architecture and the ordinary-reader prerequisite for its registered comparison. The failure ledger rules out another tiny head trained to execute already-supplied arithmetic, and the native addressing failures rule out assuming that a known operation key automatically encodes its operands. The still-open design choice is how the workers **invent** useful operations and determine which interactions are worth retaining before a task exists.

I recommend a fourth, deliberately distinct implementation strategy within the predictive candidate: a population of locally adaptable, typed latent transition modules, with a learned proposal/challenge policy and a counterexample-driven library induction objective. Do not put all changing corpus knowledge into global backbone weights; do not fix the operation inventory in advance. Train a shared proposal/transport policy across corpora; let it generate and revise compact local operator parameters and an applicability/role-binding interface during each corpus's lifetime. A local operator may be a small generated residual block, low-rank transition or slot-to-slot message function, not necessarily a linear map. Its identity, originating evidence, training examples, version, uncertainty and dependents are separately tracked. Keep the same ordinary optional-memory call and WorldPin. A module is a reusable **computation**, not a string of extracted facts or a textual tool script. A symbolic implementation is an explicit competing arm.

Why this matters: learning globally on every edit is expensive, catastrophic interference is difficult to audit, and the original whole-corpus idea demands continuing changes on edge hardware. Locally generated modules make selective replacement, dependency-aware requalification and exact same-pin ablations natural. They also risk proliferation, bad routing and latent coordinate drift; the experiment should test those risks rather than assume the design solves them.

## Real external anchor: joint edit consequences are not additive

The [2026 Universal Credit repeal Act explanatory notes](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2026/13/notes/division/3/index.htm) set the relevant start of assessment periods at 6 April 2026. The [DWP April 2026 claimant statistics, Table 2 and accompanying text](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/universal-credit-claimants-statistics-on-the-two-child-limit-policy-april-2026/universal-credit-claimants-statistics-on-the-two-child-limit-policy-april-2026) separately report 478,120 households missing some child element under the old policy, 5,930 expected not to gain because of transitional protection, 35,530 because of the benefit cap, and 120 in both categories. These are published, rounded estimates conditional on unchanged circumstances, not observed post-repeal payments or household microdata.

Treat the displayed numbers as integers for this diagnostic. Let N=478120, A=5930, B=35530 and J=120. The two marginal counts do not determine the intersection. Fréchet bounds give max(0,A+B-N) <= J <= min(A,B), so the same marginals admit 5,931 integer joint tables and a range of 436,660 to 442,590 for N-A-B+J. The report's additional intersection count yields 436,780, matching its published expected gain count. A deliberately naive independent-factor approximation gives J=A*B/N=440.669 and a count of 437,100.669 (approximately 321 above the reported value). Neither independence nor zero overlap is licensed by the marginal observations. No LSR model was evaluated; these are logical consequences of the displayed aggregates, not estimates of model error or causal effects of removing either protection.

Executed in V8: enumerated every integer J in [0,5930], reconstructed the four nonnegative cells and verified both marginals and total for all 5,931; checked the published intersection and independent estimate. Reproduction:

```js
const N=478120,A=5930,B=35530,J=120;
let count=0;
for(let j=Math.max(0,A+B-N);j<=Math.min(A,B);j++){
  const cells=[j,A-j,B-j,N-A-B+j];
  if(cells.some(x=>x<0)||cells.reduce((a,b)=>a+b,0)!==N||
     cells[0]+cells[1]!==A||cells[0]+cells[2]!==B) throw Error("bad");
  count++;
}
console.log({count, reported:N-A-B+J,
  lo:N-A-B+Math.max(0,A+B-N), hi:N-Math.max(A,B),
  independent:N-A-B+A*B/N});
```

The previous [retention theorem](REVISION_CONDITIONED_PREDICTIVE_STATE.md) identifies loss of distributional distinctions. This example adds an independently specified, real joint dependency that must be represented, challenged or refused. Neither supplying its intersection nor a hand-coded union formula demonstrates discovery. The next test is whether a worker discovers **which predicates interact** and what observation distinguishes their competing joint states, without being supplied the predicates or the answer by the evaluator.

More generally, for a scoped scalar consequence F on a fixed opening source world, define the two-edit interaction
`I(a,b) = F({a,b}) - F({a}) - F({b}) + F({})`.
An additive single-edit predictor has I=0 by construction and cannot fit a nonzero interaction even if all three opening/single-edit outcomes are exact. There is no general implication from success on single edits to success on compositions. With two edits the interaction can be any value consistent with the domain of F. For k binary edits, Boolean-lattice Möbius inversion gives F(S)=sum over T subset S of I(T), where I(T)=sum over R subset T of (-1)^(|T|-|R|) F(R). Retaining all possible interactions is exponential in k; sparse interactions are a workload hypothesis, not a theorem about arbitrary corpora. This identity is standard mathematics and not LSR novelty. Only define unordered edit sets for semantically order-independent changes; otherwise use ordered event sequences and separate final semantic equivalence from historical provenance. Never enforce commutativity of conflicting edits.

## How to train without hand-designing the semantics

Use three roles over the same versioned world during corpus construction and maintenance:

1. **Inventor:** samples candidate latent roles, transformations and combinations from source/revision encodings and prior library modules. Produces a module draft and prospective predictions, not a textual explanation that the grader executes. Candidate inputs and outputs use typed role bindings internally, with source occurrence handles. The inventor cannot read later task text.
2. **Adversary:** produces candidate source edits, role substitutions, paired/composed edits or alternative bindings that separate current rival predictions. The query objective is expected reduction of *consequential* ambiguity or held-world loss per total cost, not generic embedding novelty or entropy. It must actively seek nonzero interactions and false invariances. Penalize redundant/easy queries and record failed challenges.
3. **Witness:** for development-only executable domains, an independent pinned interpreter provides a bounded outcome for an explicit intervention; for other domains, reopen authoritative evidence or seek a human adjudication. Unchecked imagined consequences are hypotheses, never new source facts. The witness is separate from both trainable policies. A positive result may be narrow, not a proof of universality.

Train the shared proposer and local module generator on developmental corpora with a frozen or tiny backbone plus adapters. First learn single-event transitions; then specifically train on counterexamples to composition and role-binding. Contrastive examples preserve the same current answer and change a material conjunction or source role. Require the predicted result and its applicability, not latent-state reconstruction. Sample source deletions and restorations; protect unrelated verified outcomes. Train a routing policy to select a module from the semantic task or source event, not an explicit module ID. Evaluate coordinate consistency across re-encoding and source updates without requiring identical numeric latent coordinates. Local modules with different coordinate versions must be requalified or transported, not silently mixed.

For a development probe pool D with witnessed outcomes y and a declared loss ell (wrong answers and false closure penalized; proper scoring for probabilistic predictions), a module proposal earns admission only if its net value on a separate public validation fold is positive relative to the current library: sum of loss reductions on D minus calibrated costs of build, extra storage, routing, challenges, source reads, and updates. Units of loss and resource cost must be set before comparison. Use the incremental value relative to the library, not relative to no model, to reject duplicate modules. A fixed per-world capacity and a versioned residual/recovery path prevent the library from becoming an unbounded cache. Train the adversary on past development outcomes; it never sees held test task identities, grader or its rewards. Admission, not merely a low training loss, determines publication. Report forward error, cross-edit error, unrelated regression, and causal remove/restore at the same source pin. The shared module generator is itself frozen before the held corpus, while bounded query-blind maintenance can still acquire corpus-specific modules.

This is a proposed learning mechanism, not just another deterministic receiver. The strongest prior art must be built into the comparison: [DreamCoder (PLDI 2021)](https://pldi21.sigplan.org/details/pldi-2021-papers/55/DreamCoder-Bootstrapping-Inductive-Program-Synthesis-with-Wake-Sleep-Library-Learnin) learns a symbolic library and a search policy together; [counterexample-guided synthesis](https://www2.eecs.berkeley.edu/Pubs/TechRpts/2008/EECS-2008-176.html) already alternates proposals and counterexamples; [Angluin's query-learning work](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00116828) formalizes learning from explicit query types. No claim that library learning or active querying is new. The possible LSR advance is *latent* module invention, provenance-preserving continual revision and causal reuse across unseen semantic edit families, at lower lifecycle cost than symbolic library induction, an incremental compiler and competent text reasoning. In open text, an oracle cannot generally certify equivalence; bounded checked predictions and uncertainty are essential.

## A more useful development environment than another planted operator

[OpenFisca's parameter documentation](https://openfisca.org/doc/coding-the-legislation/legislation_parameters.html) records dated parameters and legislative references; its [reform interface](https://openfisca.org/doc/coding-the-legislation/reforms.html) can replace variables/parameters without mutating the reference system; its [YAML tests](https://openfisca.org/doc/coding-the-legislation/writing_yaml_tests.html) support boundary cases and combined reforms. [PolicyEngine UK](https://github.com/PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk) provides a public tax/benefit model with formulas, parameters and reforms. These are mature executable alternatives, not evidence of an unmet demand for numerical simulation. They offer a real independently specified environment for discovering compositional semantics and a strong exact comparator. They are models of legislation with coverage/implementation limitations, not authoritative law. Confirm particular rules against official sources; report model-law disagreements separately. The UK two-child repeal and other examples above are public development candidates, not held tasks.

Proposed **development-only** acquisition:
- Select chronological public rule changes in more than one tax/benefit subsystem, with accessible original guidance and testable code at pinned versions. Do not use private claimant data. Start with synthetic households and public code tests. Separate a source-facing corpus (legislative/guidance text, factual case records) from the evaluator's formulas, test answers and exact execution traces. Independently verify which branches the model covers; do not pretend that omitted real exceptions are known negatives. For the cheapest pilot, a deterministic harness can call a pinned simulator, but learned policies may query only permitted inputs and outcomes with charged oracle access. Disallow code/variable names as side channels in the text-only semantic arm.
- In the public development fold, train the inventor/adversary on one set of rule families. In the unseen fold, choose disjoint rule families and chronology, plus private synthetic boundary cases, paired edits and questions after the world is frozen. The same external compiler gives outcome labels, and an independent reviewer checks source applicability. Assess source links and semantic roles independently of numerical output. Renaming, reordered evidence and decoy clauses should not change findings. Public repositories may have been in pretraining; novel composite interventions and private case combinations mitigate but do not prove absence of memorization.
- Compare (i) static predictive state, (ii) predictive state with the evolving latent library but no adversarial challenges, (iii) the full method, (iv) a symbolic library/CEGIS policy with same permitted source and oracle access, (v) strong text-to-rule/code compilation plus execution, and (vi) a warm exact incremental engine with the full compiled semantics as an explicit privileged-ceiling arm. Separate the cost of semantic extraction from executing the exact oracle. Give learned and symbolic policies identical budgets for oracle queries, generation, storage and validation. If exact code is already available in a deployment, direct execution is the incumbent; there is no claim LSR should replace it.
- Hold the serving reasoner and task bytes fixed. Remove/restore exactly one invented latent module and its contribution, not its raw sources or the entire world; include wrong-revision and no-memory variants. An output that becomes correct only after decoding raw clauses or sending generated code to the evaluator is not a demonstrated latent computation. Record both pre-fallback and final accuracy. Require reuse on multiple new operands and a revised rule, not just a previously witnessed value.
- Proposed decision: authorize a small public pilot separately only after data/licence and resource preflight; no checkpoint choice, model download or alteration of the frozen reader gate is authorized here. First compare public exact simulator outputs against its own tests and source coverage. Then run matched learning budgets on the host when approved. Kill the latent library line if the symbolic compiler matches it at lower full cost, if routing fails on disjoint roles, if challenge work costs more than it saves, or if held paired edits expose uncalibrated false closure. Scale only if repeated-use benefit survives fresh-world and same-pin interventions. The current four-case gate remains untouched.

## Open decisions for the main engine

The large architectural swing is to make operator creation and interrogation learnable and persistent, rather than hand-coding a new transition whenever a known diagnostic fails. Can the existing NativeOperatorInventoryModel be treated as a control, not a restriction on the learned module's shape? Is a simulator-backed semantic discovery environment preferable to more supplied coordinates after the ordinary gate? Keep the strict frozen comparison and consider this independently scoped learning line. The strongest unresolved concern is that symbolic compilation may dominate and that a small model cannot infer roles from source text at all; neither is resolved by the mathematical example.

No held input/grader read or transfer, pretrained execution, or implementation change in this contribution.
