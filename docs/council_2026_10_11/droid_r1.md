# Round 1: Droid advisor

1. **Verdict.** Make *causal, selectable latent branching* the flagship, not “more answers can be decoded from a vector.” The repository’s exact quadratic result predicts aggregate interference near $k\sim\sqrt{\mathrm{PR}(\Sigma)}$ **for its specified random-feature readout**. It does not predict how many hypotheses a language model can reason over. The flagship should test that bridge, including the possibility that the bridge fails while the theorem remains sound.

2. **Attack the four bets.**
   - **Latent-state search:** Test-time activation or soft-prompt optimisation is not itself new. A verifier can do all the useful work by selecting among many attempts. Compare against text best-of-$N$ and self-consistency with identical verifier calls *and* measured inference-plus-search FLOPs. A win on a leaked or easily verified answer is inconsequential; a reproducible win on held-out problems at matched cost would matter.
   - **Superposed hypotheses:** COCONUT already motivates latent branching; its latent-BFS evidence does not establish simultaneous, causally usable branches or a $\sqrt d$ law. A probe decoding several answers can find readable traces, not computation. Bigger models also differ in training, not just width. Require one shared state from which a *later, unseen selector* retrieves each correct branch, with the original prompt and KV cache unavailable after that state. Vary effective width within one backbone, then replicate across backbones. This is the highest-upside bet.
   - **Frozen recurrent depth:** Huginn and recurrent/looped transformers make additional depth established territory; looping ordinary pretrained middle layers may simply move activations off-distribution. Compare with extra-token chain-of-thought and an ordinary repeated-forward-pass control at equal FLOPs. A small gain alone is a useful inference trick, not a new account of latent reasoning.
   - **Cross-model geometry:** Representation alignment and model stitching are substantial prior art. A mapper could transmit the answer, prompt identity, or a generally helpful activation rather than a reasoning state. The repository’s cumulative-capability line established no artifact. Require answer- and prompt-scrubbed transfers that beat same-model, shuffled, and direct-answer controls on held-out problem families. Do **not** put this high-integration-risk bet on the five-day critical path.

3. **Two sharper alternatives.** First, make the flagship’s observable a **counterfactual branching capacity**: how many distinct futures remain independently actionable from one state when the future question is chosen afterward? That is closer to native state equivalence than cosine distance or PCA. Second, test **causal compression rather than visible branch count**: can a fixed-size latent state support several later questions more accurately per FLOP than a text scratchpad or independent states? These strengthen Bet 2 rather than add another training program.

   For the flagship, define capacity before training as the largest tested $k\in\{1,2,4,8,16,32\}$ with **≥90% per-branch correctness**, and report joint correctness separately so its mechanical decline with $k$ cannot masquerade as interference. Measure task-conditioned covariance participation ratio and fourth-moment/localisation diagnostics; preregister $\sqrt{\mathrm{PR}}$ as a **conditional prediction**, not an assumed language-model law. A width mask must not grant more decoding FLOPs or an answer-bearing side channel. Record training and inference FLOPs, not just token counts.

5. **Baselines that decide meaning.** Across probes: answer-in-prompt and shuffled-label checks, fixed-input numerical-noise runs on the same hardware, matched-position sham patches, independent serial states, and held-out compositions rather than new names on familiar structures. For the flagship specifically, compare a parameter- and compute-matched text scratchpad, independently computed branches, a memory-only adapter, and an unchanged-state/identity-plus-shared-displacement null. Selective state replacement must affect the targeted future without merely making every answer worse. Raw covariance geometry is $R^n$ scaffolding; the claim-bearing quantity is which *future questions remain answerable after a causal intervention*.

7. **Flagship so-what.** *If one hidden state can keep several possible solutions alive until a question chooses among them, we could measure a genuine unit of parallel thought rather than just generate more text.*
