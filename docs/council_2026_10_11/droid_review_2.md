## Adversarial verdict

**Continue the program, but do not call either probe a capability result yet.** The most consequential result by October 16 would be a custody-valid, selectively editable state that survives further updates and a matched storage-only control. The current dev screens do not establish that result.

1. **Claims and custody.** The record now correctly calls the gate contrast a “DEV observation (not a verdict)” ([EXPERIMENTS.md, line 23](experiments/EXPERIMENTS.md#L23)). Probe B’s best-looking Qwen3-8B dev cell is 0.164 for latent ES **and 0.164 for matched random perturbations**, despite exceeding the sampled-text arm ([search_dev_summary.txt, lines 64–66](experiments/results/cloud/search_dev_summary.txt#L64-L66)). That is not evidence for verifier-guided *direction* search. Probe C’s apparent graph gain, 0.586, remains below the 0.617 constant-answer baseline ([EXPERIMENTS.md, lines 16–22](experiments/EXPERIMENTS.md#L16-L22)). Many CoT and some direct cells fail the 95% termination gate ([recur_partial_summary.txt, lines 35–47](experiments/results/cloud/recur_partial_summary.txt#L35-L47)). Its logged 128-item `test` reads are screening, not the registered 512-new-item confirmation ([recur_sweep_s.json, lines 62–64](experiments/cloud/configs/recur_sweep_s.json#L62-L64)); repeated method decisions based on those reads would consume that holdout. Spend is an estimate at a dated snapshot, **$425.67 with 14 live VMs at $55.2/hour**, not a final bill ([SPEND.md, lines 3–22](experiments/cloud/SPEND.md#L3-L22)).

2. **Code threats.**
   - **Probe B, budget 32:** `search_confirm.json` sets `"skip_repair": true` throughout its 32-attempt shards ([lines 136–145](experiments/cloud/configs/search_confirm.json#L136-L145)). `search.py:text_arms` consequently makes **zero repair attempts** ([lines 74–93](experiments/cloud/lsr_jobs/search.py#L74-L93)). A win at that budget cannot meet the frozen “strongest matched text arm” comparison ([LATENT_BRANCH_CAPACITY.md, line 20](theory/LATENT_BRANCH_CAPACITY.md#L20)).
   - **Matched compute remains unverified:** `search.py:latent_arms` runs an additional hidden-state forward pass to size perturbations ([lines 101–105](experiments/cloud/lsr_jobs/search.py#L101-L105)); neither it nor `recur.py` records paired actual FLOPs. The recurrence control substitutes filler tokens, rounded sample counts, or a larger *maximum* token budget ([recur.py, lines 129–146](experiments/cloud/lsr_jobs/recur.py#L129-L146)). Early stopping makes those poor guarantees of the registered ±5% match.
   - **Gate interpretation:** `branch.py:forward_loss` samples queried branches without excluding addressed-update targets ([lines 177–185](experiments/cloud/lsr_jobs/branch.py#L177-L185)). This is a **documented training deviation**, not repaired by evaluation alone ([LATENT_BRANCH_CAPACITY.md, lines 143–153](theory/LATENT_BRANCH_CAPACITY.md#L143-L153)). The evaluator now records selector accuracy, but its unaddressed primary accuracy is pooled across selectors ([branch.py, lines 241–253](experiments/cloud/lsr_jobs/branch.py#L241-L253)); do not substitute it for a per-selector unaddressed claim. `common.load` records a model commit hash but not a tokenizer revision or chat-template version ([common.py, lines 39–53](experiments/cloud/lsr_jobs/common.py#L39-L53)), leaving a reproducibility gap.

3. **Strategy.** Review 1’s wording, ES-norm and selector-accounting findings were substantially handled; FLOP matching and the irreversible pilot-v1 overwrite were not ([droid_review_1.md, lines 5–15](docs/council_2026_10_11/droid_review_1.md#L5-L15); [ledger, review entry](experiments/ledger.jsonl#L1093)). Do not scale the checkpoint panel or claim B’s running confirmation as decisive until the missing budget-32 text arm and measured-compute comparison are addressed. Prioritize finishing the gate, checking edits and advancement against custody controls, then running the mandatory storage-only comparator if the gate passes. Keep C as a bounded instrument investigation, not a headline. PR is useful scaffolding; the native mathematical target is **retained, selectively editable, update-closed answerability**, not an $R^n$ spread statistic.

4. **Continue? Yes. Highest leverage?** The flagship’s causal comparison is the highest-leverage bet **if its gate passes**. Broadening B/C before their controls support interpretation is not.
