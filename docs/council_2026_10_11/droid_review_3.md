**Pre-launch verdict: continue P2, but do not launch P1-prime’s full 35-run grid until its capacity evaluator and synchronization recovery are fixed.** Neither pivot rescues the failed admission gate.

1. **Claims and custody.** The ledger says, “Seed 11 ignores global updates entirely at k=1 (main == sham).” Equal accuracies do not establish equal predictions. It also says the edited result “tracks the UNEDITED graph”; the reported 0.41 was not paired-scored against that graph. The later [evidence audit](docs/council_2026_10_11/codex_evidence1.md) correctly withdraws both inferences, but the original ledger entries remain. B and C miss their registered success criteria, not all possible latent-search or recurrence mechanisms: verifier calls and FLOPs were not fully matched, and some termination counts include empty answers. The raw checkpoints remain in cloud storage; committed summaries alone do not establish revision and selection custody.

2. **Code threats before launch.**
   - [`branch.py:546–551`](experiments/cloud/lsr_jobs/branch.py) brackets on powers of two, then tests integers only between the last passing power and first failing power. It can declare a `C90` despite an untested smaller integer failing, contrary to the registered definition. Test **every integer through the frozen endpoint**, including when all coarse-grid points pass.
   - [`branch.py:447–451,473–485`](experiments/cloud/lsr_jobs/branch.py) resumes from an optimizer checkpoint but reconstructs `kmax` from the *latest* append-only mastery result. After a preemption between checkpoint saves, it can replay earlier steps under a future curriculum; existing step posts are skipped. [`sync_post_and_wait`](experiments/cloud/lsr_jobs/branch.py#L409) also accepts existing GCS decisions without checking the checkpoint or curriculum state. That breaks synchronized, equal-exposure comparisons. Resume must recover the curriculum, decision history, and random state at the checkpointed step.
   - [`p2.py:184–188`](experiments/cloud/lsr_jobs/p2.py) reads **test** re-encoding competence before training. Keep that diagnostic from influencing any training, continuation, or arm choice; otherwise the held-out set becomes a decision set. [`p2.py:194–200`](experiments/cloud/lsr_jobs/p2.py) spends roughly one-third of presentations on zero-update examples, which give the updater no training signal. State the effective update-bearing exposure rather than treating 192,000 as updater training examples. The answer-only, consistency, and shuffled arms also need measured FLOPs before a “matched” claim.

3. **Allocation through October 16.** About **$725** is recorded spent, with **$38.6/hour** live burn at the ledger snapshot ([SPEND.md](experiments/cloud/SPEND.md)); money is not yet the binding constraint. Validity and the short window are. Stop widening B/C and do not escalate the 1.7B flagship. Prioritize P2’s frozen-reader competence, paired update/undo tests, and cheap identity-displacement and sham controls. Run only a corrected, recoverable P1-prime seed-11 pilot first; scale the remaining seeds only if it clears the registered stop rule. An explicit bit-packing competitor is essential: PR and width are useful $R^n$ scaffolding, not native mathematics. The consequential question is whether a state supports **selective edits that remain answerable after further updates**, beyond ordinary storage and readout.

4. **Continue?** Yes. P2 is the highest-leverage current test of that question, conditional on the safeguards above. P1-prime is subordinate. A failure of these constructions calls for a different mechanism, not abandonment of the high-dimensional-mathematics mission.
