## Adversarial verdict

**Continue the program, but pause interpretation of P2 and do not scale P1-prime yet.** The current work can test whether a compressed state supports selective edits through further updates. It does not yet establish native mathematics of high-dimensional spaces.

1. **Claims and custody.** The P1 record says “**stop rule FIRED**” and “**no capacity claim**” ([EXPERIMENTS.md:3–10](experiments/EXPERIMENTS.md#L3-L10)). That is the right scope: the reported 0.603/0.624 values are pooled *dev* bit accuracy, not test capacity. The implementation uses 48 collections with 16 selectors at $k=1$, and 1,562 batches of 64 give 99,968 presentations, not exactly 100,000 ([branch.py:239–298](experiments/cloud/lsr_jobs/branch.py#L239-L298), [branch.py:458](experiments/cloud/lsr_jobs/branch.py#L458)). The fresh hunt could not independently read the raw endpoint, although the operator reports doing so ([codex_hunt_p1.md:9–13](docs/council_2026_10_11/codex_hunt_p1.md#L9-L13)). Keep that custody distinction. The licensed B/C sentences are suitably narrow: B’s verifier calls and FLOPs were not exactly matched, and C has termination and empty-answer confounds ([EXPERIMENTS.md:20–40](experiments/EXPERIMENTS.md#L20-L40); [recur.py:133–149](experiments/cloud/lsr_jobs/recur.py#L133-L149)). Neither running pivot has a result yet.

2. **Fix validity threats now.**
   - **P2 cannot deliver its registered paired interval from its output.** `p2.py` writes aggregate accuracy and defect cells, while `acc_cell` discards collection-level paired outcomes; the aggregator has no P2 paired analysis ([p2.py:213–227](experiments/cloud/lsr_jobs/p2.py#L213-L227), [branch.py:239–298](experiments/cloud/lsr_jobs/branch.py#L239-L298), [aggregate.py:112–169](experiments/cloud/aggregate.py#L112-L169)). The registration requires a positive Holm-adjusted *collection-paired* interval ([LATENT_BRANCH_CAPACITY.md:187–198](theory/LATENT_BRANCH_CAPACITY.md#L187-L198)). Log matched collection predictions and identifiers, then rerun affected evaluations. P2 also saves no trained updater checkpoint and has no training-resume path; after preemption, persistent result keys could combine different training timelines ([p2.py:189–227](experiments/cloud/lsr_jobs/p2.py#L189-L227), [common.py:14–33](experiments/cloud/lsr_jobs/common.py#L14-L33), [vm_startup.sh:18–19](experiments/cloud/vm_startup.sh#L18-L19)).
   - **Check updater gradients before spending further.** `P2Model.step` switches to the updater adapter but switches back to default *before* `loss.backward()`; `updater_params()` explicitly re-enables updater parameters only later during gradient clipping ([p2.py:42–63](experiments/cloud/lsr_jobs/p2.py#L42-L63), [p2.py:198–203](experiments/cloud/lsr_jobs/p2.py#L198-L203)). Verify nonzero updater-LoRA gradients and freeze the default adapter/reader. Otherwise an apparent consistency effect may come only from `Eu`/`Du`, not the intended updater.
   - **P1-prime’s best-checkpoint choice breaks on resume.** `best` resets to −1 after loading a checkpoint, so the first eligible post-resume dev score can overwrite an earlier, better `best.pt` ([branch.py:447–451](experiments/cloud/lsr_jobs/branch.py#L447-L451), [branch.py:465–466](experiments/cloud/lsr_jobs/branch.py#L465-L466), [branch.py:528–544](experiments/cloud/lsr_jobs/branch.py#L528-L544)). Persist and restore the best score. Synchronization also accepts old GCS posts without checkpoint identity ([branch.py:409–428](experiments/cloud/lsr_jobs/branch.py#L409-L428)); audit recovery before comparing widths.
   - **Do not call post-advance edits causal successes without the post-advance contrast.** Reachability edits are chosen to flip the *initial* graph, but the two subsequent global toggles can erase that difference; the reported advance accuracies do not require the edited and unedited futures to differ ([branch.py:312–317](experiments/cloud/lsr_jobs/branch.py#L312-L317), [branch.py:348–355](experiments/cloud/lsr_jobs/branch.py#L348-L355)). Report paired future counterfactuals, alongside shams.

3. **Allocation through October 16.** The $775.98 spend snapshot predates these launches, so its $4.4/hour live rate is not their burn rate ([SPEND.md:3–29](experiments/cloud/SPEND.md#L3-L29); [launches.jsonl:279–295](experiments/cloud/launches.jsonl#L279-L295)). **Pause or checkpoint P2 until gradients, custody and paired logging are sound.** Let P1-prime remain a seed-11 pilot, but do not launch the other seeds before its stop rule and resume fix ([p1b_s11.json:108–227](experiments/cloud/configs/p1b_s11.json#L108-L227); [LATENT_BRANCH_CAPACITY.md:207–220](theory/LATENT_BRANCH_CAPACITY.md#L207-L220)). Do not restart B/C or escalate the failed gate. The better near-term question is whether update-closed, selectively editable answerability beats ordinary storage and readout, with an explicit packing competitor. PR and orthogonal coordinates remain useful $R^n$ scaffolding, not the native-math result ([CROSS_PROJECT_INSIGHTS.md:16–35](theory/CROSS_PROJECT_INSIGHTS.md#L16-L35), [AGENTS.md:145–174](AGENTS.md#L145-L174)).

**Highest leverage:** yes, *if* these corrections precede claims and scaling. A failed construction should change the mechanism under test, not close the mission.
