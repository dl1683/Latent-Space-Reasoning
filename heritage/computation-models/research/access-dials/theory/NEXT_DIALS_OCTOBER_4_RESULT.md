## NEXT DIRECTIONS

**HEURISTIC ranking: 1. endurance-limited persistent computation; 2. bounded-buffer network computation; 3. hard-deadline exact convolution.** This ranks questions for advisor/architect dialogue. It does not settle an architecture or replace the registered work.

### 1. Endurance-limited persistent RAM — NEXT-CANDIDATE

**Dial:** finite state-transition capacity, coupled to representation redundancy.

**Finite contract.** Store an arbitrary logical vector \(x\in\{0,1\}^k\) in \(N=k+r\) binary persistent cells. Each cell permits at most \(L\) bit changes. Commands are `FLIP(i)` and exact `QUERY(i)`.

Primitives are addressed bit READ/WRITE, Boolean operations, finite branches and bit emission. An addressed access costs its address bits plus one data bit; local bit operations cost one. The finite indexed-memory interface is explicit and shared by competitors; no physical wire-delay claim is implicit.

All persistent metadata occupies these same \(N\) cells. Scratch, addresses and PC use at most \(O(\log^2(NL))\) volatile bits, cleared between completed commands. Queries do not change persistent cells. The algorithm cannot read a hardware wear counter, retain a command number externally, or consult the original \(x\) after initialization.

**Useful task family.** Exact online mutable storage under arbitrary update sequences, including repeated attacks on one logical location.

**Charged costs.** Pay for the \(N\) allocated cells, initialization from raw \(x\), every initialization write, all metadata changes, scratch clearing, address formation, queries and outputs. Uniform code depends on parameters, never on future commands. Any wear history needed after a command must be encoded in the paid cells.

**Positive construction target — CONJECTURE.** For
\[
N=2k,\qquad \lceil\log_2^2N\rceil\le L\le N,
\]
construct a deterministic representation serving every sequence of at least
\[
H\ge c\,\frac{NL}{\log^2N}
\]
flips, with polylogarithmic worst-case command work and \(O(N\,\mathrm{polylog}(NL))\) initialization work. Initialization wear counts against \(L\).

The constructive approach is to encode remapping and generation information within the persistent representation while distributing its own updates. The hard part is preventing its metadata from becoming the first exhausted component.

**Universal CANNOT target — CONJECTURE.** In the same boundary-reset model, seek
\[
H^\star(N,k,L)\le
L\min\!\left\{N,\;2^{r+1}-1\right\}.
\]
The \(NL\) term is the elementary total-wear ceiling. The substantive target is the redundancy term, covering arbitrary nonlinear representations.

**Plausible mechanism.** Exact queries partition persistent configurations by logical contents. Averaging over adjacent logical vectors should expose a pair with few total representations. Alternating that logical bit confines completed states to that small set. A small set of physical coordinates distinguishes those states; each logical alternation must consume wear on one of those coordinates. This is a proposed counting route, not an admitted proof.

**Strongest occupied comparators.** Endurance-limited-memory codes already distinguish access to cell contents from access to programming counts. Wear-leveling constructions already charge mapping and small auxiliary memory. The more recent Min–Max model explicitly uses hardware wear feedback and endurance-unlimited auxiliary memory. None may be replaced by direct overwriting as the sole baseline. [ELM definitions](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2109.09932), [Wear Leveling Revisited](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/00lipics/lipics-vol181-isaac2020/LIPIcs.ISAAC.2020.65/LIPIcs.ISAAC.2020.65.pdf), [Min–Max model](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0166531625000562).

**Actual difference sought.** Joint lifetime, local execution and exact persistence when the wear-tracking representation must survive in the same limited cells. This is not a claim that wear leveling is new.

**First falsifiable gate.** After source translation and design review, register a tiny exact synthesis problem, initially \(N\le4,\ k\le2,\ L\le2\). Search arbitrary decoder partitions and stationary update policies; physical wear counts belong to the checker and remain invisible to the policy.

This admits a plausible finite combinatorial search. A complete unsatisfiability result in a carefully specified relaxation can establish a universal obstruction for that finite tuple. A satisfying policy still needs elementary compilation and cost admission. Incomplete search establishes neither.

**Narrative attack.** “This is wear leveling plus rewriting codes” is the strongest dismissal. It survives only if the complete construction beats or extends their matching contracts. If proved and instantiated, the useful implication is durable exact storage without a large trusted wear table. A counting bound alone would remain a component.

**LEAK trigger:** uncharged persistent cache, readable wear counters, externally supplied phase, or free replacement cells.

### 2. Bounded-buffer network computation — NEXT-CANDIDATE

**Dial:** communication topology and residence of information in local buffers.

**Finite contract.** A supplied directed acyclic graph has \(n\) vertices, bounded degree, three sources and three receivers; its underlying undirected graph has treewidth at most two. Sources receive arbitrary bit streams \(x_1,x_2,x_3\). Receivers must emit, in order,
\[
x_1\oplus x_2,\qquad x_2\oplus x_3,\qquad x_1\oplus x_3.
\]

An edge transports at most one bit per round. Each SEND, RECEIVE, copied bit, Boolean gate and controller transition is charged. Sending one bit along three edges costs three sends. Parallel processors, link buffers and local code are explicit installed resources. Round depth and total work are reported separately.

**Useful task family.** Repeated distributed linear summaries from several raw data sources, without gathering every source stream at every receiver.

**Charged costs.** Read the topology, compute its decomposition, generate and install local programs, initialize buffers, ingest every source bit, perform every transmission, maintain finite ring-buffer cursors and emit every answer. No decomposition, code coefficients, frame history or alignment buffer arrives free.

**Positive construction target — CONJECTURE.** Test whether the following necessary cut condition is sufficient on this graph family:
\[
\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb F_2}
M_{\text{receivers outside }U,\ \text{sources inside }U}
\le |\delta^+(U)|
\quad\text{for every }U.
\]

Seek a uniform compiler with polynomial preparation, sustained one source bit per round, delay \(O(h\log n)\), total installed mutable state \(O(n\log^2n)\), and \(O(n\,\mathrm{polylog}\,n)\) work per round, where \(h\) is the longest directed path.

The construction route is decomposition-based synthesis of local linear summaries, jointly assigning delays and buffers. Cut sufficiency and the buffer bound are both hypotheses.

**Universal CANNOT target — CONJECTURE for the exact transfer.** A violated cut-rank condition prevents sustained unit rate for every finite exact protocol, including nonlinear encodings and adaptive local control. Strengthen that limit with temporal cuts: information arriving early but required by later outputs must cross an available edge or remain in counted state.

**Plausible mechanism.** Fix outside-source values and count distinguishable demanded outputs across a cut over many frames. For buffer limits, use past inputs that require different outputs under a common future continuation. This addresses algorithms rather than merely linear coding schedules.

**Strongest occupied comparators.** Network function computation already has generalized cut bounds and tight single-receiver linear-function results. Multi-receiver computation already admits substantive coding gains and capacity theorems. Koetter–Médard also gives robust multicast constructions and demonstrates that general connections require more than cut conditions. [Network function computation](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/8sn7z02w), [Computation alignment](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~ngoela/MultiReceiverFunctionComputation_WebVersion_November2012.pdf), [Algebraic network coding](https://www.mit.edu/~medard/mpapers/networkcoding.pdf).

**Actual difference sought.** A charged, bounded-state, deadline-respecting compiler for the stated multi-receiver structural family. Coding versus forwarding alone is already occupied and cannot be the novelty claim.

**First falsifiable gate.** First translate existing constructions into the stated finite interfaces. Then select tiny cut-admissible graphs and solve exact bounded-horizon local-program feasibility. Include unequal path lengths: a short path cannot silently supply an unlimited queue while waiting for a long path.

A failure of scalar linear coding kills that construction class only. A universal finite obstruction requires admitting arbitrary Boolean local programs within the declared state and instruction budgets.

**Narrative attack.** “You rediscovered network coding” is decisive unless the buffer/deadline/compiler frontier differs under an actual matching comparison. The useful implication, if proved, is a deployable way to compute distributed summaries within bandwidth and memory budgets. It must include the installed programs and queues.

**LEAK trigger:** free broadcast, global aggregation, uncounted alignment queues, or a supplied successful code.

### 3. Exact convolution with a hard per-arrival budget — NEXT-CANDIDATE

**Dial:** causality and worst-case service time rather than amortized work.

**Finite contract.** Supply a raw length-\(n\) kernel \(a\) over \(\mathbb F_p\), with prime \(n\le p\le n^3\), and a finite stream of length at most \(n^3\). After each arriving symbol \(z_t\), emit
\[
y_t=\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}a_jz_{t-j}\pmod p
\]
before accepting the next symbol; missing initial symbols are zero.

Use a declared finite word RAM with \(w=\Theta(\log n)\): addressed word reads/writes and specified bounded-width arithmetic, Boolean and branch instructions have explicit word costs. Multiplication intermediates, modular reduction, all registers and controller fields count. Also provide an elementary bit expansion under an explicit addressed-bit meter. Neither ledger claims physical RAM latency.

**Useful task family.** Exact streaming correlations and finite algebraic computations that cannot postpone an answer until a large batch completes.

**Charged costs.** Read the raw kernel, generate any transforms and constants, verify the field parameters, allocate and initialize all storage, ingest and emit symbols, and execute every arithmetic/address/controller operation. The transformed kernel is produced by the algorithm. Past input exists only in counted memory.

**Positive construction target — CONJECTURE.** Obtain:

- \(O(n)\) stored words;
- \(O(n\log^2n)\) charged preprocessing word work;
- \(O(\log n)\) **worst-case** word work after every arrival;
- exact output with zero lookahead.

The constructive approach is reusable transforms with explicitly scheduled work whose operands are already available. The unresolved requirement is meeting every arrival boundary, including block-boundary arrivals.

**Universal CANNOT target.** Transfer the published online-convolution information-transfer lower bound to the exact machine: no admitted algorithm can guarantee \(o(\log n)\) work per arrival for every kernel and stream when symbol width and word width are comparable. This mechanism is a **published PROVED comparator**, not proposed novelty. [Theorem 1.2 and Corollary 1.7](https://theoryofcomputing.org/articles/v015a002/v015a002.pdf).

**Strongest occupied comparators.** That source already gives matching amortized cell-probe bounds; its upper allows free internal computation. Van der Hoeven’s relaxed and semi-relaxed multiplication algorithms explicitly reuse transforms and address online output. Both must be translated before asserting an elementary-runtime or deadline gap. [Relaxed multiplication](https://www.texmacs.org/joris/newrelax/newrelax.html), [later finite-ring treatment](https://www.texmacs.org/joris/fastrelax/fastrelax2.pdf).

**Actual difference sought.** The joint worst-case deadline, finite memory and charged elementary execution contract. Neither online convolution nor its logarithmic lower bound is new. A matching existing construction would make this direction **ABSORBED**.

**First falsifiable gate.** Translate the strongest published schedules first. If a construction obligation remains, freeze a finite schedule and symbolically check operand availability, exact outputs, peak storage and operations between successive arrivals. A block FFT requiring an unavailable future symbol or exceeding the boundary budget kills that schedule immediately. Schedule failure is not a universal computational lower bound.

**Narrative attack.** “This is a faster online FFT” is a fair dismissal of a merely incremental result. It matters beyond methodology only if an application needing exact answers gains a useful service-time guarantee after preparation and storage costs. That makes it third: it has a strong task and universal comparator, but the least assured new-model ceiling.

**LEAK trigger:** a free kernel transform, unexpanded field operations, oversized words, future inputs, or amortized work presented as a hard deadline.

**HEURISTIC next move:** take the endurance contract and its finite synthesis gate into the genuine advisor/architect dialogue first. It offers the clearest bounded route to distinguish a positive construction from an all-encoding obstruction. Keep the network hypothesis second and the convolution translation third. All three remain **NEXT-CANDIDATE**, with design/infinity-leak review before theorem investment and fresh independent proof review before any new **PROVED** label.
