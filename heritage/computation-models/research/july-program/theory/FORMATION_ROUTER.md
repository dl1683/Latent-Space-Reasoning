## 2. C0-C3 - unchanged Class Gate prefix

The following clauses are the unchanged Version-2 prefix. Their order and binding
tokens are **FROZEN**.

### C0 - finitary, uniform, and infinity-leak audit

Require finite descriptions, uniform family generation, finite charged
observations, finite precision/noise semantics where relevant, finite
advice/certificates, and ordinary finite-time access to every primitive. Return
`INFINITY-LEAK`, `UNBOUNDED-ADVICE`, `UNCOMPUTABLE-QUOTIENT`, or `ORACLE-FIAT` if
the corresponding power enters without being a finite charged input. A finite
computable subroutine merely declared free continues to C1-C3; it is not an
uncomputable oracle.

### C1 - relabelling test

Apply the uniform erasure `E`. If `E` is an isomorphism on computational objects
and morphisms, preserves observations and composition laws, and removes only
candidate-authored tags, colors, type names, or wrappers, return
`RELABELLING-FIAT`. A nontrivial task codomain alone does not escape when erasure
leaves all extensional behavior unchanged.

### C2 - resource-reindexing test

Ask whether one computable monotone map `f` takes an already admitted resource
preorder to the proposed preorder such that, uniformly over every instance and
machine, new cost is `f` of old cost and every proposed CAN/CANNOT is exactly the
pullback of an old statement through `f`. If so, return
`RESOURCE-REINDEXING-FIAT`. Mutual simulation with overhead is insufficient: C2
requires unchanged computational objects/morphisms and transport of the entire
theorem content through the same reindexing.

### C3 - antecedent-forcing test

Require all four conditions:

1. `A,O,P1,P2` were independently meaningful before the candidate boundary or
   ruler was named;
2. candidate-authored examples, benchmarks, applications, or narratives are not
   the sole evidence for `A`;
3. erasing the proposed admission/resource clause prevents uniform preservation
   of at least one frozen observation, composition law, or theorem statement
   across `P1,P2`; and
4. that failure is not explained solely by C1 or C2.

If any condition fails, return `BOUNDARY-FIAT`. Passing C3 says only that some
boundary is forced; it does not determine the formation, novelty, or theorem.

## 3. F0 - semantic formation determination

**FROZEN NOUN-ERASURE RULE.** Candidate names, field names, historical identities,
and words such as machine, protocol, stream, proof, or operator do not select the
route. Determine the primary formation from the frozen objects, morphisms,
observations, composition laws, and quantifier order.

**FROZEN PRIORITY.** Apply the tests below from F0.1 through F0.5 and choose the
first that matches. Exactly one primary formation is selected. A secondary aspect
is carried as part of the full contract but does not trigger a second native
differential.

### F0.1 - class operator

Select `CLASS-OPERATOR` when the native inputs or objects are uniform finite
presentations of computation classes or families, the primitive maps or combines
those classes, and the target theorem concerns closure, nonclosure,
composition, fixed points, or an operator hierarchy at that class level.

### F0.2 - proof-native

Select `PROOF-NATIVE` when native computational objects are derivations,
certificates, or proof terms; native morphisms are proof transformations such as
normalization, cut transformation, or proof composition; and the observations and
resource claims are invariant properties of those proof objects rather than an
ordinary machine computation with a proof attached.

### F0.3 - interaction

Select `INTERACTION` when the class contract has at least two separately
quantified roles or strategies, exchanged information can depend on prior
exchange, and the extensional observation quantifies over alternative behavior of
at least one role. Centralized feedback, an ordinary environment input, or
endogenous state transitions do not qualify merely because information moves.

### F0.4 - restriction/access

Select `RESTRICTION-ACCESS` when the defining clause removes or bounds an operation
otherwise available in the underlying computation: access order, revisit,
retention, copying/reuse, query pattern, locality, precision, or another admitted
access morphism. A charged increase within the restriction is not removal of the
restriction.

### F0.5 - added extension

Select `ADDED-EXTENSION` otherwise, when the proposed formation adds a primitive,
atom, permission, state component, transition, effect, or finite component to an
otherwise retained computational closure.

**AMBIGUITY RETURN.** If the frozen semantics are too incomplete to apply this
priority without relying on the candidate noun, C3 was not actually discharged;
return `BOUNDARY-FIAT`. Formation is not a registrar-selected label.

## 4. D0 - common native-comparator law

**FROZEN SAME-CONTRACT LAW.** Before the selected differential, freeze one native
comparator with the same task, interface, output, error, uniformity, finite
encoding, observation, and resource accounting rule. Both candidate and
comparator receive every genuinely common task-preserving operation. Acquisition,
preprocessing, advice, calibration, representation change, construction,
checking, output materialization, and reductions are charged symmetrically.

**FROZEN STATE LAW.** Full explicit finite state, metadata, logs, ordinary local
computation, and state expansion remain available symmetrically when they obey the
selected formation; their construction, storage, access, and use remain charged.
No candidate may hide a finite ledger from the comparator. Conversely, an
operation excluded by the frozen, antecedent-forced formation is not a common
operation and is not gifted to the comparator. A compiler that uses such an
operation does not establish transport.

**FROZEN TRANSPORT OBJECT.** A native transport is uniform and typed. It must
preserve the whole frozen task and observation contract, objects, morphisms,
composition/feedback, error, encoding equivalence, formation law, resource
preorder in the claimed region, and theorem quantifiers. Mere vocabulary overlap,
component occupation, or arbitrary-overhead simulation is not transport.

## 5. Formation-specific native differentials

Run exactly the differential selected by F0. Each differential asks whether the
formation changes the exact admissible closure or full theorem architecture after
D0 grants. Failure returns `FORMATION-NONDIFFERENTIAL` with the formation and exact
transport witness named.

### D-EXT - added extension differential

The comparator is the generated product of every complete admitted component,
including arbitrary finite common messages, logs, shared state, preprocessing,
ordinary composition, and reversible simulation, all symmetrically charged. The
candidate extension is not gifted unless it is independently common.

Pass only if an explicit finite same-contract witness or full-scope theorem shows
a closure, composition law, or resource boundary not reproduced by that generated
product. If a uniform product compiler preserves every load-bearing step and the
remaining proof is component theorems plus generic sum, maximum, serial, padding,
or bookkeeping accounting, return `FORMATION-NONDIFFERENTIAL`.

### D-RES - restriction/access differential

The comparator must obey the identical frozen restriction: the same order,
revisit, retention, copy/reuse, query, locality, precision, or access rule. It may
use arbitrary ordinary state expansion and genuinely common operations only
within that restriction and with their full symmetric charges. In particular, it
may not cache a forbidden complete input, add an undeclared revisit, create a
forbidden copy, use unrestricted random access, or erase a locality/precision
boundary and call that fair closure.

Pass only if the restriction changes admitted morphisms or quantified theorem
content and the CAN/CANNOT architecture uses that change. Reject if the same
restricted comparator already has the full closure and theorem packet, or if the
restriction is merely a tag, one cost reindex, or irrelevant to the load-bearing
proof. Comparing with an unrestricted machine is neither required nor sufficient.

### D-INT - interaction differential

The comparator receives the same local computation, local finite state,
randomness, preprocessing, and common output processing for every role, all
charged symmetrically. It is not granted cross-role messages, adaptive replies, or
strategy access that its frozen noninteractive contract excludes; granting those
would preinstall the formation being tested.

Pass only if exchange or adaptive strategy quantification is load-bearing for the
observation or theorem architecture. Reject if a uniform noninteractive
translation preserves role separation, every honest and alternative-strategy
quantifier, transcript/round resources, and the complete CAN/CANNOT content, or if
one established protocol/game model already owns that exact contract. Collapsing
an alternative strategy into nonuniform advice is not transport.

### D-PROOF - proof-native differential

The comparator uses the same proof-object interface and receives every genuinely
common inference, normalization, composition, representation, and checking
operation with construction and verification charged. It is not gifted a proof
transformation excluded by the frozen proof calculus.

Pass only if a normalization/canonicalization CAN and an internal strict boundary
or non-normalization CANNOT survive typed translation of derivations and their
resources. Reject if ordinary proof complexity, type theory, rewriting, or a
standard proof calculus transports the full derivation semantics and theorem
packet, or if the negative ranges only over artifacts accepted by one checker.

### D-OP - class-operator differential

The comparator acts on the same uniform finite class presentations and receives
every genuinely common reduction, closure, composition, encoding change, and
finite state expansion with symmetric charge. It is not gifted the candidate
operator or an operation excluded by the frozen class-level domain.

Pass only if a useful uniform positive operator theorem and a strict
nonclosure/noncomposition or operator-hierarchy boundary survive typed class-level
transport. Reject if a standard class operator already has the exact closure and
theorem packet, or if the hierarchy is only generic padding, diagonalization,
constructibility, word evaluation, or a resource reindex with no formation-specific
residue.

## 6. C4 - exact-scope occupation and full quantified theorem transport

Run the unchanged Version-2 occupation meanings after the native differential:

- `OCCUPIED-BOUNDARY`: one established model already states the exact
  antecedent-forced class contract, and a uniform definitionally conservative
  translation preserves objects, morphisms, observations, composition, resource
  preorder up to encoding equivalence, and theorem architecture at the claimed
  scope;
- `OCCUPIED-PRODUCT`: the candidate is a generated product of established models
  and every proof decomposes into existing component theorems plus generic
  accounting, with no load-bearing cross-component lemma;
- `OCCUPIED-CAN`: the exact automatic positive construction already exists at the
  frozen scope; and
- `OCCUPIED-CANNOT`: the exact non-tautological negative theorem already exists at
  the frozen scope.

`OCCUPIED-BOUNDARY` or `OCCUPIED-PRODUCT` rejects the new-class destination.
`OCCUPIED-CAN` or `OCCUPIED-CANNOT` rejects a novelty-dependent theorem packet and
requires a strict-delta rewrite before acceptance. Equality of extension sets,
robustness equivalence between non-isomorphic presentations, or simulation with
arbitrary overhead is insufficient. The calibration class itself is not counted
as its own occupying predecessor.

**FROZEN FULL-SCOPE RULE.** The occupation translator must preserve every outer
quantifier and typed interface in the literal theorem packet: all generated finite
members and sizes, all admitted uniform semantic procedures, all relevant honest
and alternative strategies, all allowed compositions and reductions, every error
or accuracy parameter, every encoding-equivalent presentation, and the native
resource charge. For a restriction formation, the translation must obey the same
restriction. For an interaction formation, it must preserve role separation and
strategy quantification. A theorem for one analyzer, compiler, type system, policy
family, presentation, finite example, or convenient factorization is not
full-scope transport. Whole-packet transport rejects even when the vocabulary is
different; failure to find a source never accepts.
