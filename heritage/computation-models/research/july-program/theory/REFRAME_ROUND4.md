## 0. Registration before analysis

**Binding mission.** Generate and adversarially adjudicate at least six candidate
classes of computation that are not substrate races and are not separation-seeking.
The class may change the type of problem, output, observation, context, obligation,
or reduction; it may not earn its identity from beating a symmetrically closed
competitor.

**Precommitted verdict tokens - HEURISTIC.**

- **`ROUND4-CONFIRM`:** at least six candidates receive a founding definition,
  first-class resource, candidate CANNOT shape, crisp famous question, named
  occupation risk, gossip one-liner, steelman, hostile attack, and ranked
  disposition; at least one survives as a precise Gate-0 target without making an

- **`ROUND4-KILL`:** every generated candidate is either an occupied reformulation,

- **`ROUND4-VOID`:** the round changes the corrected mission, reopens hardware or

  mandatory dossier fields after seeing adverse evidence.

**Permanent-law precommit - HEURISTIC.** A candidate can rank as Gate-0-live only if
its proposed computational object is not already compiled away by the same free
operations that define it. A separation-specific compiler is not required for a
non-separation model, but construction, representation, context selection,
certificates, and observers remain finite, uniform, and charged. Arbitrary reals,
unbounded advice, an infinite observer family supplied extensionally, or an
uncomputable quotient fires `INFINITY-LEAK`.

## 1. Candidate inventory

The inventory is deliberately wider than the Batch-7 seed. Names are provisional;
the objects, not the branding, are what the occupation attack adjudicates.

1. **Contextual Problem Transducers (CPT):** a problem is an open interface judged
   by a predeclared finite family of downstream contexts, including feedback.
2. **Observation-Costed Coinductive Computation (OCCC):** an output is a continuing
   process; finite observations, not termination, are the charged deliverables.
3. **Obligation-Carrying Computation (ObCC):** an output is a value plus a finite,
   composable future-work/correctness obligation that later consumers may discharge.
4. **Reduction-Producing Computation (RPC):** the delivered result is a verified,
   resource-graded reduction between problem families rather than an instance answer.
5. **Quotient-Native Computation (QNC):** outputs live natively in a finitely
   presented quotient, and the charged resource is the observer power needed to
   distinguish equivalence classes rather than the cost of choosing representatives.
6. **Context-Switching Strategy Computation (CSSC):** a problem asks for a strategy
   that remains valid as a finite environment changes modes; context switches and
   assumption revisions are first-class events.
7. **Counterfactual-Ready Computation (CRC):** an output is a finite state sufficient
   for a declared family of future interventions or updates, not merely the answer
   to the present instance.
8. **Refinement-Stream Computation (RSC):** an output is a certified monotone stream
   of improving finite approximants; observation latency and permissible retraction
   are part of the output type.

Listing is not survival. The dossiers below use checked primary-source statements
for occupation boundaries; they do not claim literature-final closure.

## 2. Candidate dossiers

### 2.1 Contextual Problem Transducers (CPT)

**Founding definition sketch - SPECULATION.** A contextual problem is a finite typed
input port, an open finite output/process port, a uniformly generated finite family
of downstream contexts, an observational loss at the final context boundary, and a
resource vector for producing and using the open port. A computation may return a
smaller interface (Z) instead of a full intermediate (Y) only when one uniform
compiler makes every predeclared context behave within tolerance. Context families,
side information, sequential composition, and feedback are part of the problem
type, not selected after the representation is seen.

- **First-class resource - SPECULATION:** contextual interface size plus the cost of
  constructing, storing, and reusing the interface under feedback.
- **Candidate CANNOT shape - CONJECTURE:** below a resource threshold, an explicit
  admitted context separates the interface from the full process; ideally the
  witness is dual to an automatic minimal quotient.
- **Crisp famous question - HEURISTIC:** the classical minimal-sufficient-statistic
  question becomes interactive and compositional: *what is the smallest state that
  preserves every declared future decision, including decisions that feed actions
  back into the process?*
- **Occupation risk - EMPIRICAL / CHECKED PRIMARY STATEMENTS:** extreme. Blackwell's
  comparison of experiments characterizes finite informativeness through decision
  problems and garblings; Weihrauch reducibility treats problems and uniform
  pre/post-processing as the ordered objects; probabilistic full abstraction makes
  equality coincide with contextual observational equivalence; interface automata
  provide automatic compatibility and compositional component interfaces; finite
  probabilistic bisimulation and minimization already construct behavior-preserving
  quotients. See [Blackwell 1953](https://doi.org/10.1214/aoms/1177729032),
  [Pauly-Steinberg 2016](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.03024),
  [Ehrhard-Pagani-Tasson 2015](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.01272),
  [de Alfaro-Henzinger 2001](https://doi.org/10.1145/503209.503226), and
  [Kiefer-Tang 2021](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.FSTTCS.2021.48).
- **Gossip one-liner - SPECULATION:** *This compiler throws away everything no
  future user can detect—and proves that throwing away one bit more breaks someone.*

**Steelman - HEURISTIC.** CPT does not claim a faster machine or a larger language
class. Its best possible delta is a *uniform construction theorem*: from a natural
interactive pipeline and context grammar, automatically synthesize a smallest open
interface that is preserved by composition and feedback, then obtain a matching
context witness for minimality. If the same construction spans statistics,
programming-language context, and controller feedback without an oracle, the object
could unify several notions that currently agree only pairwise.

**Hostile expert attack - HEURISTIC.** Every noun already has an owner, and the
frozen finite-state pipeline makes the attack sharper: behavioral equivalence,
probabilistic bisimulation, partition refinement, and full abstraction already say
which states no context distinguishes. If the context grammar is narrow, the answer
is forced by the grammar; if it is rich, identity-like contexts restore the full
state; if exact minimalization is hard, an alleged cheap compiler is false. The
CANNOT is tautological unless it proves a quantitative resource lower bound not
already supplied by state minimization or decision comparison.

### 2.2 Observation-Costed Coinductive Computation (OCCC)

**Founding definition sketch - SPECULATION.** An OCCC problem has a finite input and
a coinductive output object (stream, reactive process, or resumptive component).
Correctness is not termination; it is a uniform rule answering every finite,
adaptively chosen observation request. The cost of a computation is the function
mapping an observation schedule to the work, retained state, latency, and error
needed to answer it. Legal morphisms are causal finite-observation transducers;
composition and one guarded feedback operation must preserve an explicit modulus
from requested observation depth to consumed input/output depth.

- **First-class resource - SPECULATION:** observation complexity—the finite work and
  latency required per adaptive finite view of a continuing output—plus the modulus
  carried through feedback.
- **Candidate CANNOT shape - CONJECTURE:** no finite-observation machine computes a
  discontinuous stream/process functional; more sharply, a family with no uniform
  observation modulus cannot have bounded observation cost under any equivalent
  finite presentation.
- **Crisp famous question - HEURISTIC:** *what does it mean for an operating system,
  controller, or infinite stream program to have computed an answer when it is not
  supposed to halt?* The model would replace termination with costed observability.
- **Occupation risk - EMPIRICAL / CHECKED PRIMARY STATEMENTS:** very high. Universal
  coalgebra already treats infinite data and systems via final coalgebras and
  bisimulation; TTE computes finite prefixes/precision of infinite outputs in finite
  time; coalgebraic components already admit canonical feedback traces. See
  [Rutten 2000](https://ir.cwi.nl/pub/48/),
  [Weihrauch 1992](https://math.berkeley.edu/~pglutz/documents/Weihrauch_Degrees_Discontinuity_1992.pdf),
  and [Hasuo-Jacobs 2011](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0960129510000551).
- **Gossip one-liner - SPECULATION:** *A program that runs forever can still finish
  every question you ever ask it—and this theory prices each answer.*

**Steelman - HEURISTIC.** Coalgebra supplies objects and bisimulation, while TTE
supplies effective finite observation, but a model whose *complexity class* is the
observation-cost functional and whose feedback theorem preserves explicit moduli
could still be a strict delta. Its CAN would be a robust equivalence between machine
presentations of causal process computation; its CANNOT would be a quantitative
continuity barrier, not merely nontermination.

**Hostile expert attack - HEURISTIC.** This is likely TTE plus stream complexity plus
coalgebraic trace under one cost vector. Continuity of Type-2 computable maps is an
old impossibility boundary, productivity/causality are old admissibility conditions,
and guarded trace is old composition machinery. Unless one natural cost class is
representation-robust and makes a famous reactive-computation question strictly
sharper, OCCC is an occupied product. The gossip story is intuitive but may merely
rename online response time.

### 2.3 Obligation-Carrying Computation (ObCC)

**Founding definition sketch - SPECULATION.** An ObCC output is a finite triple
((v,o,p)): a presently usable value (v), a finite residual obligation (o) that
specifies future correctness/work still owed, and machine-checkable potential or
evidence (p) that bounds how the obligation can be discharged. Composition does
not erase debt: obligations combine by a fixed algebra, and guarded feedback is
legal only when a well-founded or amortized potential decreases. The class is
graded by immediate work, exported debt, verification cost, and worst-case future
discharge cost.

- **First-class resource - SPECULATION:** exported computational debt and the
  potential/evidence that makes future discharge compositional.
- **Candidate CANNOT shape - CONJECTURE:** no sound finite certificate algebra can
  export less than a task-dependent debt lower bound while guaranteeing all legal
  continuations; cycles with nondecreasing potential cannot be admitted as completed
  outputs.
- **Crisp famous question - HEURISTIC:** *when is a partial or asynchronous result
  genuinely complete enough to compose, rather than merely shifting hidden work to
  its consumer?* This sharpens the long-standing modular-verification and amortized-
  analysis problem, though it is not yet one canonical famous open problem.
- **Occupation risk - EMPIRICAL / CHECKED PRIMARY STATEMENTS:** extreme. Proof-
  carrying code attaches machine-checkable safety proofs; Hoare/dependent type
  systems internalize verification conditions; session types specify future
  protocols; resource-aware session types explicitly let messages and processes
  carry amortized potential for future communication. See
  [Necula 1996](https://www.usenix.org/legacy/publications/library/proceedings/osdi96/full_papers/necula/html/node2.html)
  and [Das-Hoffmann-Pfenning 2017](https://arxiv.org/abs/1712.08310).
- **Gossip one-liner - SPECULATION:** *Every answer comes with a receipt for the work
  it has secretly pushed into the future.*

**Steelman - HEURISTIC.** Existing type systems usually verify a chosen program.
ObCC would instead classify *problems by legal debt-bearing outputs* and seek a
representation-robust minimal-debt theorem. A natural duality between exported
potential and an adversarial continuation could create a real CAN/CANNOT pair, and
the hidden-work story directly attacks a genuine weakness of ordinary output
contracts.

**Hostile expert attack - HEURISTIC.** Resource-aware session types already make
messages carry potential, enforce protocol composition, and prove cost soundness.
Calling the typed value an output type rather than a program type does not make a new
class. The proposed CANNOT is either the soundness theorem of the potential method or
a definition-level rejection of unpaid cycles. The absence of a single famous
question with a nontrivial automatic construction demotes this candidate unless a
Gate 0 finds a problem-level theorem not expressible as an effect/session type.

### 2.4 Reduction-Producing Computation (RPC)

**Founding definition sketch - SPECULATION.** An RPC instance is a finitely
presented source problem family (P), target family (Q), and resource contract.
The output is not a solution of (P(x)), but a machine-checkable uniform reduction
consisting of an instance transformer, solution translator, correctness proof, and
resource-distortion certificate. Reductions compose as the primitive execution
law; the charged resources are construction cost, proof size/checking, oracle calls,
adaptivity, and accumulated distortion.

- **First-class resource - SPECULATION:** the cost and distortion of transforming
  questions and transporting solutions, with proof provenance.
- **Candidate CANNOT shape - CONJECTURE:** a monotone invariant of problem families
  forbids any admitted low-distortion reduction; composition cannot cross the
  invariant even when instance computation is free.
- **Crisp famous question - HEURISTIC / FAILURE:** “which problems are really the
  same?” is already made precise by Turing, many-one, truth-table, polynomial-time,
  parameterized, approximation-preserving, and Weihrauch reductions. RPC does not
  make a previously mushy famous question crisp.
- **Occupation risk - EMPIRICAL / CHECKED PRIMARY STATEMENTS:** fatal unless a new
  reduction object appears. Weihrauch degrees explicitly order computational
  problems using uniform pre/post-processors; complexity theory and proof complexity
  already resource-bound reductions and their certificates.
- **Gossip one-liner - SPECULATION:** *The answer to one problem is a verified road
  that turns every instance into another problem.*

**Steelman - HEURISTIC.** Treating reductions as delivered computations could make
problem-space search and proof provenance explicit, and distortion accounting could
prevent informal reduction chains from laundering resources. A complete calculus of
automatically generated reductions would be useful.

**Hostile expert attack - HEURISTIC.** Reductions have been first-class mathematical
objects for most of computability and complexity theory. Verification adds proof-
carrying code; resource vectors add standard bounded reducibilities. The CANNOT is
any monotonicity argument used to prove nonreducibility. This candidate fails
property 4 and is `OCCUPIED-PRODUCT` at the concept level.

### 2.5 Quotient-Native Computation (QNC)

**Founding definition sketch - SPECULATION.** Fix a uniformly presented finite group
(G_n) acting on finite encodings (X_n). A QNC problem maps input orbits to output
orbits, and legal algorithms are equivariant transducers: relabelling an input may
only relabel the output. A result is an orbit-level object equipped with a finite
observer algebra; choosing a representative, adding an order, naming an atom, or
installing a gauge is a separate *symmetry-breaking resource* and must be declared.
Composition occurs directly on orbit-valued interfaces rather than by silently
canonicalizing after every step.

- **First-class resource - SPECULATION:** symmetry breaking—the advice, ordering,
  names, or canonicalization work required to pass from a quotient result to a
  representative—plus observer complexity on the quotient.
- **Candidate CANNOT shape - CONJECTURE, WITH ELEMENTARY CORE:** for any nontrivial
  orbit, there is no deterministic equivariant section of the quotient map; hence a
  representative-valued continuation requires an explicit symmetry-breaking
  resource. A nontrivial version must lower-bound that resource for a named finite
  family and show orbit-level composition avoids it.
- **Crisp famous question - HEURISTIC:** *is canonicalization actually necessary to
  compute with unlabeled objects?* This makes the graph-isomorphism-versus-
  canonization and “compute modulo symmetry without choosing names” question a
  founding output-type distinction.
- **Occupation risk - HEURISTIC / HIGH:** group-action algorithms, graph
  canonization, nominal sets and nominal computation, orbit-finite automata,
  quotient types, species, equivariant algorithms, and gauge-invariant computation
  occupy large pieces. The elementary no-section theorem is known mathematics and
  can be too trivial to carry a model.
- **Gossip one-liner - SPECULATION:** *Computers keep wasting work naming things that
  have no real names; this model computes without choosing a label.*

**Steelman - HEURISTIC.** Unlike a baseline race, QNC changes what counts as a legal
answer and exposes a hidden free operation—symmetry breaking. The CAN could be a
representation-robust compiler from a finite presentation to an orbit-native
pipeline that preserves products, quotients, and one feedback operator without
canonical representatives. The CANNOT has a clean structural form: natural choice
is impossible on nontrivial orbits, so any representative-dependent interface pays
a named resource. A successful theorem would explain precisely when canonical forms
are an artifact of interfaces rather than computation.

**Hostile expert attack - HEURISTIC.** Outputting an “orbit” by returning the input
representative and promising not to inspect labels is trivial. Invariant theory and
nominal techniques already compute modulo actions; canonical graph forms are
invariant strings, not equivariant sections, so the elementary no-section argument
may miss the famous question. QNC survives only if Gate 0 freezes a useful pipeline
where orbit-native composition is automatic, materially smaller than a complete
invariant, and has a quantitative lower bound on symmetry-breaking—not merely the
sentence “there is no natural choice.”

### 2.6 Context-Switching Strategy Computation (CSSC)

**Founding definition sketch - SPECULATION.** A CSSC problem asks for a finite
strategy, not a terminal value. The environment may move among a finite,
predeclared set of modes/specifications; the output strategy must maintain declared
safety/liveness guarantees while explicitly paying for assumption changes,
controller-state migration, and mode switches. Composition connects games at their
ports; feedback is play. The class is graded by memory, switches, regret or violated
obligations, and synthesis cost.

- **First-class resource - SPECULATION:** assumption/context switches and the state
  migration needed to keep a strategy valid through them.
- **Candidate CANNOT shape - CONJECTURE:** below a switch/memory budget, an explicit
  environment strategy forces either a specification violation or a quantified loss;
  no finite strategy uniformly handles an unrestricted assumption lattice.
- **Crisp famous question - HEURISTIC / WEAK:** *can a reactive controller remain
  correct when its environment assumptions change?* Reactive synthesis already
  makes the static version precise; switching adds a resource but does not obviously
  reveal a previously undefined question.
- **Occupation risk - EMPIRICAL / CHECKED PRIMARY STATEMENTS:** extreme. Game
  semantics makes strategies compositional; reactive synthesis constructs
  controllers from game specifications and studies weakest/permissive environment
  assumptions; online learning already proves lower bounds with switching costs.
  See [Abramsky-Jagadeesan-Malacaria 2000](https://doi.org/10.1006/inco.2000.2930),
  [Chatterjee-Henzinger-Jobstmann 2008](https://arxiv.org/abs/0805.4167), and
  [Cesa-Bianchi-Dekel-Shamir 2013](https://arxiv.org/abs/1302.4387).
- **Gossip one-liner - SPECULATION:** *The answer is a plan that keeps working even
  when the rules change—and every rule change has a price.*

**Steelman - HEURISTIC.** Standard complexity treats the environment as input or an
adversary; CSSC could classify problems by the cost of *reinterpreting* an ongoing
strategy across mode changes. A compositional theorem joining synthesis and
switching lower bounds might be structurally useful.

**Hostile expert attack - HEURISTIC.** This is reactive synthesis or online learning
with a switching-cost coordinate. Strategy-as-output, game composition, adversarial
context, and assumption repair are all established. Any CANNOT will be a game
lower bound, and any CAN a synthesis algorithm. The resource may be useful, but it
does not define a genuinely new class at current scope.

### 2.7 Counterfactual-Ready Computation (CRC)

**Founding definition sketch - SPECULATION.** A CRC problem's output is a finite
state (Z(x)) that must answer a predeclared finite family of future updates,
interventions, and queries without rerunning the original computation. The problem
contract freezes which interventions may arrive, whether they are adaptive, and how
many rounds occur. Cost has an offline coordinate for producing (Z) and online
coordinates for update, query, retained space, and recovery; sequential updates are
composition and adaptive queries supply feedback.

- **First-class resource - SPECULATION:** counterfactual breadth—the range of future
  changes a result remains ready for—jointly with offline size and online update/
  query cost.
- **Candidate CANNOT shape - CONJECTURE:** an information-transfer or communication
  witness forces a space/update/query tradeoff for any state supporting all declared
  interventions.
- **Crisp famous question - HEURISTIC:** *how much of a computation must be retained
  so that tomorrow's change does not force a restart?* Dynamic data structures and
  incremental computation already make important versions precise.
- **Occupation risk - EMPIRICAL / CHECKED PRIMARY STATEMENTS:** effectively fatal at
  this abstraction. Dynamic data structures and cell-probe lower bounds jointly
  price updates, queries, and memory; self-adjusting computation automatically
  tracks dependencies and propagates changes with correctness/cost theorems;
  knowledge compilation classifies representations by succinctness and supported
  queries/transformations. See
  [Patrascu-Demaine 2006](https://doi.org/10.1137/S0097539705447256),
  [Acar-Blume-Donham 2014](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0956796813000431), and
  [Darwiche-Marquis 2002](https://arxiv.org/abs/1106.1819).
- **Gossip one-liner - SPECULATION:** *Compute once, keep exactly enough structure
  to survive every change you promised to handle.*

**Steelman - HEURISTIC.** CRC reframes a “result” as future-ready state and could
unify preprocessing, provenance, incremental computation, and dynamic lower bounds
under one output type. The narrative is useful and the CAN/CANNOT asymmetry is real:
automatic dependency tracking versus information-transfer tradeoffs.

**Hostile expert attack - HEURISTIC.** That unification is precisely a survey-level
wrapper around mature models. The family of future updates is a data-structure
operation set; (Z) is the data structure; offline/online cost is standard; adaptive
feedback is standard. No strict theorem delta or new famous question remains.

### 2.8 Refinement-Stream Computation (RSC)

**Founding definition sketch - SPECULATION.** An RSC problem returns a finite-coded,
potentially infinite stream ((y_t,c_t)) of approximants and certificates. A declared
preorder makes information monotone; any retraction consumes a separate mind-change
budget. A finite observer supplies a tolerance and must receive a certified adequate
approximant within the corresponding latency bound. Composition transports
certificates and moduli, and guarded feedback may consume earlier approximants.

- **First-class resource - SPECULATION:** certified refinement latency and allowed
  retractions as properties of the output type.
- **Candidate CANNOT shape - CONJECTURE:** discontinuous targets or targets above a
  chosen effective Borel/Weihrauch level admit no uniformly certified refinement
  stream with the registered finite latency/mind-change bound.
- **Crisp famous question - HEURISTIC:** *when does “getting closer forever” count as
  solving a problem?* This sharpens approximation versus delivered finite
  certification, but the project already killed mind-change/limit computation as a
  resource dial.
- **Occupation risk - HEURISTIC / EXTREME:** domain theory, exact real arithmetic,
  Type-2 computability, computable analysis, anytime algorithms, Gold/Ershov
  mind-change hierarchies, and Weihrauch discontinuity already own the components.
- **Gossip one-liner - SPECULATION:** *An answer may never stop improving, but every
  promised accuracy must arrive on time with a certificate.*

**Steelman - HEURISTIC.** The output-type framing is better than the dead D1 power
dial: it distinguishes an uncertified limit from a stream whose every finite use is
delivered on schedule, and it makes certificate transport through composition
load-bearing.

**Hostile expert attack - HEURISTIC.** The repair does not escape the graveyard.
TTE already demands finite output prefixes/precision in finite time; computable
analysis tracks moduli; mind-change hierarchies track revisions; proof-carrying
outputs track certificates. Joining them is an occupied product, and the CANNOT is
the inherited continuity/limit hierarchy. RSC is not a live Gate-0 candidate.

## NARRATIVE

**Honest one-sentence story - HEURISTIC / SEARCH UNIT ONLY.** *Eight
problem/output-first definitions exposed two Gate-0 hypotheses, but none yet
changed what computation can honestly claim to do.*

**Obviousness and triviality attack - HEURISTIC.** Candidate generation is not the
moonshot. Until one candidate has an automatic CAN and a non-definitional CANNOT, it is
philosophy with new labels and does not survive "isn't that obvious?" or "that's
trivial."
