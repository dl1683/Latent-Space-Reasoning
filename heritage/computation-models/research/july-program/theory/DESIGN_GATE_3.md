## 1. What Round 2 settled

Both independent tracks rejected the generic closure metatheorem as the founding
object while retaining its nontrivial operational core as a permanent admission
gate. Both also selected Pareto claim shapes and death-gated candidates. The live
disagreement was the positive object:

- **ICSM:** make causal arrival, public commitment, lag, observation, work, and
  retraction jointly visible for process-valued tasks;
- **EIT:** make reliable information transport across physical cuts visible under
  independently sourced embedding restrictions and full resource accounting.

**HEURISTIC - adjudication.** ICSM goes first because a causally admissible closure
cannot synthesize instance information that has not arrived. This attacks the
PFPWO-style absorption pattern at its root. The statement is deliberately narrow:
an oracle, predictor carrying instance advice, or noncausal arrival contract could
absorb the distinction, and existing online/sequential theories may already contain
the entire resource product. EIT remains live because its task and falsifiers are
more concrete, but it cannot clear unless its bounded-layer class is a real regime
and its cut theory is not merely the occupied VLSI/interconnect tradition.

PFTC is not killed. It remains an unregistered speculative backup and receives no
Batch-3 slot.

## 2. Permanent closure/admission calculus

**PROGRAM LAW - PERMANENT.** Before theorem work, every separation proposal must
freeze:

1. a task relation and observation/output quotient;
2. the input-arrival, interface, error, and uniformity contracts;
3. a typed set of free sequential, parallel, encoding, decoding, randomization,
   state, feedback, reuse, approximation, and discard operations;
4. an ordered resource algebra with explicit composition and error accumulation;
5. an independently justified non-closure axiom; and
6. the cost of any compiler that adds or simulates the proposed primitive.

Candidate and comparator receive the same declared closure. If a uniform,
task-preserving compiler places the candidate construction inside that closure with
overhead inside the claimed Pareto region, issue
`SYMMETRIC-CLOSURE-ABSORPTION` and stop theorem work. If the proposal excludes the
compiler only by changing the task, output, information, error, or resource
contract, the comparison is not cleared.

This law is a mandatory design/evidence gate, not a candidate founding model. Its
abstract algebra is occupied by resource-theory and axiomatic-complexity traditions;
without a nontrivial compiler, minimal-generator, monotone-converse, or natural iff
result, closure inclusion is definitional.

### 2.1 CCC-A - finalized floor statement, not a flagship claim

**CONJECTURE / UNREVIEWED FLOOR LEMMA.** Fix typed processes, a predeclared free
closure \(\operatorname{Cl}_{\mathcal F}(C)\), a task observation relation, an
error-composition law, and an ordered resource monoid. Suppose a device realization
factors as

\[
  d=e_{\mathrm{out}}\circ p\circ e_{\mathrm{in}}.
\]

If one uniform compiler places \(e_{\mathrm{in}}\), \(p\),
\(e_{\mathrm{out}}\), and their well-typed composition in
\(\operatorname{Cl}_{\mathcal F}(C)\), preserves the registered task observation
within its error budget, and incurs no larger charged resource vector than the
claimed device point, then the closure realizes every such registered task point at
no larger vector. No strict Pareto separation at that point follows from the device
factorization.

The substantive burden is the uniform cost/error compiler and the independent
choice of \(\mathcal F\), not set inclusion. If iteration 9 cannot retain anything
beyond generated-closure membership, CCC-A remains a definition-level audit lemma
and may not be advertised as a new CANNOT theorem.

## 4. ICSM Gate 0 - iterations 1-5

### 4.1 Minimal candidate, frozen against proliferation

**SPECULATION.** An Irrevocable Causal Stream Machine is one ordinary uniform
probabilistic machine with:

1. an input stream under an explicit computable, task-independent arrival envelope;
2. private work tapes charged in ordinary time and space;
3. a typed public claim channel whose emissions remain in the observable record;
4. explicit abstention and retraction events rather than silent overwriting; and
5. a finite resource vector containing ordinary work, space, observed input bits,
   lag/lookahead, public output bits, retractions, and error.

A task is a causal stream relation. It must forbid deferred-work tricks such as
emitting an unevaluated program, every possible answer, or a pointer unless the
stakeholder relation accepts that object. Any asymptotic claim must survive
bounded-distortion rechunking and computable time reparameterization.

This is a Gate-0 sketch, not a selected model definition.

### 4.2 Pre-registered stakeholder task probes

The task probes are frozen before the source and occupation search. They may be
killed or weakened out of candidacy, but not swapped for more favorable examples.

#### ICSM-T1 - stable live captioning

Acoustic frames arrive causally. A uniform system emits time-aligned caption tokens
to a live display. Every visible replacement, deletion, or semantic correction is a
charged retraction; abstention is explicit. The score jointly records work, observed
frames, display lag, public output length, retractions, and transcript error. The
source gate must find independent evidence that a stakeholder values both low lag
and display stability/correction cost, not merely final transcript accuracy. The
occupation comparator includes incremental ASR, simultaneous translation,
streaming transducers, prefix-to-prefix models, and revision/stability metrics.

#### ICSM-T2 - time-critical public warning

A declared multichannel sensor stream arrives under a computable envelope. A
uniform system may issue, update, downgrade, or cancel a public alert. The score
jointly records ordinary work, observations, decision lag or lead time, alert and
retraction events, false/missed alert loss, and final error. The source gate must
find independent evidence that the stakeholder faces both timing and revision/
false-alarm consequences. The occupation comparator includes quickest change
detection, sequential hypothesis testing, online learning/decision, event-triggered
control, and recourse.

For each task, Gate 0 requires at least one independent task-domain source and one
independent technical source supporting the exact timing/output stakes. Failure to
source the public-process contract is evidence for `OUTPUT-FIAT`; exact absorption
into the strongest standard task is evidence for `OCCUPIED-PRODUCT`.

### 4.3 ICSM kill tokens - adopted verbatim

- **`OCCUPIED-PRODUCT`** (translation is exact).
- **`OUTPUT-FIAT`** (the gap is only append-only syntax).
- **`ARRIVAL-ADVICE`** (schedule encodes instance or answer).
- **`DEFINITIONAL-HIERARCHY`** (an indistinguishable-prefix tautology).
- **`NO-ROBUSTNESS`** (chunking erases the result).

Any one kills ICSM for Batch 3. Synonyms do not dilute these tokens, and no
definition may be multiplied to avoid one.

### 4.4 ICSM Gate-0 survival obligations

ICSM survives Gate 0 only if all of the following are present:

1. two source-backed stakeholder task cards with end-to-end output relations;
2. an occupation matrix against interactive/persistent machines, streaming,
   Type-2/infinite-word computation, online algorithms and communication, online
   learning and sequential inference, recourse, delay games, and dynamic algorithms;
3. no exact product translation covering the task and proposed joint obligation;
4. a computable task-independent arrival envelope with no advice or free oracle;
5. a semantic output rule not reducible to append-only syntax;
6. a proof-ready **CONJECTURAL** joint inequality coupling at least two of lag,
   observation, work, and retractions, rather than only an indistinguishable-prefix
   fact;
7. a same-task uniform-strategy route approaching that inequality; and
8. survival under bounded-distortion rechunking, computable time recoding, and a
   semantically equivalent output representation.

Items 6-7 are theorem-shape obligations only. Gate 0 does not prove them.

## 5. EIT Gate 0 - iterations 6-9

### 5.1 Frozen candidate and task

EIT retains the Round-1 **Post-Fabrication Permutation Transport** task. A uniform
fabric is built before an arbitrary permutation \(\sigma\) on \(N\) named ports is
known. After \(\sigma\) arrives and its acquisition, storage, compilation, and
distribution are charged, \(Q\) batches of \(N\) independent \(b\)-bit words must
be explicitly materialized at their named destination ports by deadline \(\delta\)
with block error at most \(\epsilon\). Pointers, labels, retained inputs, remote
references, or latent fields are not outputs unless a sourced stakeholder accepts
them.

The provisional lower-bound class C2D has constant or explicitly bounded routing
layers, a source-backed component/channel density at a declared feature scale,
bounded signal speed and reliable per-mode rate, uniform pre-instance fabrication,
and arbitrary digital, analog, stochastic, coding, optical, and scheduling
operations that fit the geometry and pay the common resource vector. Candidate
escape classes - 2.5D, 3D wire, free-space/guided wave, and hybrid - are separate,
fully charged classes rather than one favored super-class.

### 5.2 EIT gate tokens - adopted verbatim

- **EIT-CLEAR-TO-PROVE**: issue only when EIT-0, the stakeholder, C2D, the resource
  composition laws, the infinity-leak audit, and occupation matrix are frozen and no
  registered blocker remains.
- **EIT-SEPARATION-REVIEWED**: issue only after fresh independent reviewers have
  attacked both cannot and can proofs, including the physical accounting.
- **ABSORBED**: a fair closure simulates the proposed construction at no worse vector.
- **OCCUPIED-KILL**: the full task/model/theorem pair is already known or is a direct
  corollary without a new computational object.
- **TASK-KILL**: no independent stakeholder needs the registered task and interface.
- **RESTRICTION-KILL**: the competitor restriction is justified only by protecting
  the candidate advantage.
- **LEAK-KILL**: unbounded precision, wavelength or feature shrinkage, advice,
  fabrication, state, time, energy, or interface work enters uncharged.
- **NO-REGION-KILL**: honest accounting leaves no nonempty separating region.
- **FRAMEWORK-KILL**: only the audit or closure framework survives; invariant #1 is
  not met, so the home-run direction is stopped and the result is retained as floor.
- **VOID**: the machines did not solve the same task under the same interface.

`EIT-SEPARATION-REVIEWED` cannot be issued in Gate 0. **NO-BREADTH** is a required
stakeholder-source finding, not an additional token: if two independent sources do
not support post-fabrication permutation breadth and explicit delivery, issue the
verbatim EIT token **TASK-KILL**.

### 5.3 EIT Gate-0 survival obligations

EIT survives Gate 0 only if:

1. two independent sources support the registered task breadth, timing, and explicit
   payload delivery for a real stakeholder;
2. a real packaging/fabrication regime independently supplies the layer and density
   bounds before the theorem target; otherwise issue `RESTRICTION-KILL`;
3. the restriction passes the question loop's non-fiat tests: pre-stated, symmetric,
   composition-closed, robust to constant-factor technology changes, inhabited by a
   real implementation population, and paired with every escape cost;
4. the full task/model/frontier is not occupied by VLSI area-time and bisection,
   communication complexity, interconnect and routing theory, network coding, or
   electromagnetic channel/degrees-of-freedom results;
5. a neutral cut obligation remains valid under coding, broadcast, side information,
   retained state, and matched waves/wires, without claiming that the textbook cut
   principle itself is new;
6. the resource algebra and infinity-leak ledger are frozen; and
7. `EIT-CLEAR-TO-PROVE` can be issued with no other EIT blocker.

No cut lemma, planar lower bound, or positive construction is proved in Gate 0.
