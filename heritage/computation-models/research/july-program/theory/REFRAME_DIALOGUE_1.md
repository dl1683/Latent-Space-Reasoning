## Round-1 answer

**RECOMMENDATION - CONJECTURE.** Re-found the positive program on **Embedded
Information-Transport Computation (EIT)**: uniform physical computers that move
newly supplied information to newly named destinations under an explicit
time-energy-area-volume-precision-interface account. Use a small **Costed Closure
Calculus (CCC)** as the negative theorem and mandatory kill gate. Do **not** make the
abstract triple of task, resource vector, and competitor closure the founding object.

The supervisor has the right negative lesson: if the comparison class may absorb
the mechanism at the comparison boundary without paying for it, substrate separation
is impossible by construction. That lesson alone is not yet a new model. General
closure, convertibility, monotone, and resource-vector formalisms are occupied. The
program earns a re-foundation only if one independently motivated restriction
supports a nonempty, reviewed positive region. The strongest wager is not **wave
beats wire**. It is that information crossing a geometric cut has a cost set by the
number and quality of independent channels through that cut, and different embedding
classes may expose different feasible frontiers.

Two batches must produce a source-backed task, a legitimate restricted class, a
neutral cut lemma, a matching construction, and a nonempty Pareto region - or a
death certificate. There is no framework-only fallback.

- **CONJECTURE**: a precise claim with identified falsifiers.

# Part I - Independent reframes derived before considering the supervisor's lean

These four candidates begin from different moves: change the physical primitive;
change the timing of the competitor restriction; change the claim type to
probabilistic or interactive semantics; or target task-relative resource inequalities.

## Candidate A - Embedded Information-Transport Computation (EIT)

### Founding move

**CONJECTURE.** Make an embedded computer's atomic act a costed transfer of
distinguishable information across a physical cut. A machine specifies an embedding
and fabrication-time geometry; admissible communication modes; a schedule and
switching rule; a noise and decoding model; an exact input/output interface; and a
resource vector. The initial vector is area A, volume V, completion time T, energy E,
fabrication or reconfiguration depth D, precision P, interface traffic I, and
persistent-state bits B.

The primitive is mode-neutral. Wires, free-space modes, guided waves, acoustic modes,
and mixed systems are admissible. Wave propagation is not free or special. Each
mechanism pays for launch, propagation, control, detection, error, and occupancy
under the same interface convention.

The first task should be **post-fabrication permutation transport**: after a uniform
fabric is built, an arbitrary destination permutation arrives, followed by batches
of independent words; every word must be materialized at its named output. Routing
is the task rather than a hidden subroutine.

### Candidate theorem pair

**CONJECTURE - CANNOT.** In a bounded-layer, bounded-density two-dimensional
embedding with bounded signal speed and bounded per-crossing-mode information
capacity, some post-fabrication permutations require a lower frontier in
(A,T,E,I,B), from a cut-demand theorem plus a bound on channels crossing the cut.

**CONJECTURE - CAN.** A separately costed three-dimensional or free-space-mode
construction realizes the same task outside that two-dimensional frontier after
fabrication, detection, control, precision, and output materialization are charged.

The correct result is a Pareto region, not a scalar speedup.

### Why the graveyard points here

- **DERIVED.** PFPWO failed because Fourier structure was assigned to the substrate
  while the direct baseline fused the same structure at the task boundary. EIT makes
  the transported information and destination relation common inputs and charges
  channel realization on both sides.
- **DERIVED.** BLSD-CT failed because schedule compilation was hidden. EIT places
  fabrication, control, switching, and reconfiguration in the resource vector.
- **DERIVED.** D1's finite-deadline lesson becomes an interface rule: the answer
  must be explicit at required ports by the deadline. A pointer, latent field, or
  retained state is not an answer unless the task requests it.
- **HEURISTIC.** The parity and causal-reach work suggests that geometric cuts are a
  more stable comparison boundary than named substrates.

### Pros, cons, occupation, fast kill

- **HEURISTIC - PRO.** This changes the computational object and offers a plausible
  cannot theorem plus a concrete can construction.
- **HEURISTIC - PRO.** Packaging and geometric embedding can motivate the restriction
  independently of the favored mechanism.
- **OPEN - CON.** A bounded-layer class is rigged if the stakeholder can use 3D
  wires, optical interposers, serialization, or large off-package memory.
- **OPEN - CON.** A neutral cut bound may constrain waves and wires equally after
  aperture, wavelength, noise, mode count, detector cost, and control are charged.
- **HEURISTIC - OCCUPATION HIGH.** VLSI complexity, bisection and area-time bounds,
  communication complexity, network coding, interconnect theory, and electromagnetic
  degrees-of-freedom results overlap. Novelty cannot rest on using a cut.
- **CONJECTURE - KILL.** Kill EIT if the same task/model/frontier is known; if the
  positive device violates the neutral cut bound; if the restriction is justified
  only by excluding the favored mechanism; or if honest cost leaves no open region.

## Candidate B - Staged Fabrication / Online Computation (SFOC)

### Founding move and theorem route

**CONJECTURE.** Treat the timing of information arrival as the independently
justified restriction. Computation is divided into fabrication before the instance
is known; bounded reconfiguration after a public task descriptor is known; online
work after private or adversarial instance data arrives; and explicit output
materialization. No instance-dependent geometry, state, advice, certificate, or
calibration may be installed before its information becomes available. Every later
bit written into the machine is charged.

**CONJECTURE - CANNOT.** For a registered online task, a machine with bounded
post-instance writable state, observations, or communication must pay a lower bound
in competitive ratio, rounds, probes, or written bits.

**CONJECTURE - CAN.** A uniform physical fabric achieves a better frontier because
its prebuilt connectivity or dynamics can be activated after the instance arrives
without encoding that instance during fabrication.

### Pros, cons, occupation, fast kill

- **HEURISTIC - PRO.** The stage contract attacks boundary laundering and unbounded
  advice directly. Built before the input exists is mechanism-independent.
- **HEURISTIC - PRO.** It combines naturally with EIT and exposes schedule
  compilation as a cost.
- **OPEN - CON.** Fixed dynamics may merely rename task-family advice as fabrication.
- **OPEN - CON.** Digital competitors also receive preprocessing, randomized data
  structures, batching, and amortization; the gap may vanish.
- **HEURISTIC - OCCUPATION VERY HIGH.** Online algorithms, competitive analysis,
  cell-probe lower bounds, preprocessing/query tradeoffs, advice complexity,
  nonuniform circuits, streaming, and communication complexity occupy the space.
- **CONJECTURE - KILL.** Kill SFOC as primary if the restriction amounts to denying
  the baseline the preprocessing granted to the device; if fixed state is uncharged
  advice; or if the theorem is exactly an occupied online or cell-probe result with
  no new physical-cost interaction.

## Candidate C - Probabilistic Interactive Stream Computation (PISC)

### Founding move and theorem route

**CONJECTURE.** Reopen exactly the D1 cells the reviewer left uncovered: randomized
answers, interactive protocols, and infinite-output semantics. A machine emits a
time-indexed observation stream and may exchange messages with a verifier or
environment. Correctness quantifies over prefixes, stopping rules, confidence,
convergence modulus, and adversarial interaction.

Candidate claims include distributional approximation with error shrinking under an
observation budget; anytime computation with certified prefixes; interactive
verification; and infinite objects represented by convergent names.

**CONJECTURE - CANNOT.** Under explicit observation and interaction budgets, no
machine in a stated closure class can certify the target or reach the required error
without a lower-bounded number of samples, rounds, or bits.

**CONJECTURE - CAN.** A candidate process supplies an honestly costed stochastic or
interactive resource improving an accuracy-time-energy frontier.

### Pros, cons, occupation, fast kill

- **DERIVED - PRO.** The finite deterministic deadline lemma did not close these
  cells. This is a real logical opening in the record.
- **HEURISTIC - PRO.** The claim type changes rather than merely swapping substrate.
- **OPEN - CON.** Randomness and interaction are standard resources; a physical
  source is not new unless its cost or access contract is new.
- **OPEN - CON.** Infinite outputs can hide infinity in convergence rates, moduli,
  oracle names, or observation time. Any finite transcript remains reproducible.
- **HEURISTIC - OCCUPATION EXTREME.** BPP, randomized query complexity, interactive
  proofs, streaming, Type-2 computability, represented spaces, computable analysis,
  stochastic processes, and statistical decision theory occupy the obvious forms.
- **CONJECTURE - KILL.** Kill PISC as primary if no resource remains after standard
  models receive the same oracle or physical source; if convergence lacks a charged
  computable modulus; or if novelty is only an indefinitely improving trace.

## Candidate D - Task-Relative Resource Inequalities (TRRI)

### Founding move and theorem route

**CONJECTURE.** Replace the substrate-supremacy claim by a mechanism-neutral resource
inequality for a task family, with architecture classes occupying different feasible
regions. Candidate inequalities couple information displaced across cuts; time and
path length; energy and reliability; area or volume and independent modes; precision
and distinguishable states; and interface traffic or persistent state.

**CONJECTURE - CANNOT.** Every realization of a registered task satisfying common
physical assumptions obeys a resource inequality.

**CONJECTURE - CAN.** A construction matches or approaches the inequality in a
previously unattained regime, proving that the region is feasible and useful.

### Pros, cons, occupation, fast kill

- **HEURISTIC - PRO.** Mechanism neutrality reduces asymmetric-baseline errors.
  Matching upper and lower bounds are useful even without a new complexity class.
- **HEURISTIC - PRO.** The graveyard becomes evidence about invalid resource
  projections rather than disconnected autopsies.
- **OPEN - CON.** This may be physics-aware accounting, not a model of computation.
  A resource vector without an order, task, and feasible-set theorem says little.
- **HEURISTIC - OCCUPATION HIGH.** Communication lower bounds, pebbling, time-space
  and area-time tradeoffs, thermodynamic computing, energy complexity, resource
  theories, and circuit/layout complexity overlap.
- **CONJECTURE - KILL.** Kill TRRI as founding direction if it yields only an audit
  checklist, known bounds rewritten as vectors, or no construction in a region a
  fair competitor cannot enter.

# Part II - Adjudication, including the supervisor's Closure-Class / Pareto lean

## Candidate E - Closure-Class / Pareto Framework (CCPF)

### Strongest exact version

Let M be a set of typed processes with serial and parallel composition. Let R map a
process to a partially ordered resource monoid. Let C be a competitor class and
Gamma a declared set of operations such as composition, wiring, randomization,
advice, preprocessing, state retention, postselection, approximation, interface
translation, and access to a physical primitive. Define Cl_Gamma(C) as the least
class containing C and closed under Gamma. Let a task be an interface and loss
predicate, and let F(T,C,Gamma) be the nondominated resource vectors of processes in
Cl_Gamma(C) that meet that predicate.

This makes the supervisor's negative idea precise enough to attack.

**CONJECTURE - CCC-A, costed absorption schema.** Suppose a proposed device factors
as e_out composed with p composed with e_in. If Cl_Gamma(C) contains the encoders,
decoders, their serial composition, and p at no larger charged resource vector than
the claimed device, then every task realized by the device is realized within the
same upper resource set by Cl_Gamma(C). No strict separation exists in those
coordinates.

**CONJECTURE - CCC-L, laundering schema.** If the task quotient identifies an
explicit answer with a certificate, pointer, latent state, boundary field, or
retained history, then a claimed saving in the omitted materialization coordinate is
not a separation for the explicit-answer task. Restoring the quotient map restores
its cost.

**HEURISTIC.** EFRS and PFPWO are candidate instances of CCC-A; D1 and the BLSD
schedule obstruction are candidate instances of CCC-L. Neither implication is
recorded as PROVED here.

The positive half would seek a task T and independently motivated C,Gamma for which
one architecture has a resource vector outside F(T,C,Gamma), with robustness under
nearby closure choices.

### What is genuinely strong

- **DERIVED.** It states the graveyard's common failure as mathematics rather than
  accumulating substrate-specific warnings.
- **HEURISTIC.** Closure sensitivity forces every claimed separation to expose the
  exact operation whose addition absorbs it.
- **HEURISTIC.** A costed, typed version is more useful than the informal maxim that
  a baseline should be fair.
- **HEURISTIC.** CCC-A can become a reusable pre-theorem kill gate for every future
  task card.

### Where it is too weak or occupied

**HEURISTIC - OCCUPATION BLOCKER.** Abstract resource theories already define free
objects and operations, composition, convertibility preorders, and monotones. See
[Coecke, Fritz, and Spekkens](https://arxiv.org/abs/1409.5531) and the general
monotone construction of [Gonda and Spekkens](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.07085).
Closure and equivalence questions are themselves known to become difficult or
undecidable in quantum resource-theory settings; see
[Scandi and Surace](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.09341). Therefore the words closure,
resource vector, and Pareto frontier do not supply novelty.

**DERIVED.** The uncosted absorption theorem is nearly definitional: if C is closed
under exactly the device operation, then the device is in the closure. Teeth require
one of three nontrivial additions: a minimal generating set, a cost-preserving
simulation bound, or a theorem classifying which independently motivated closures
do and do not absorb a task.

**OPEN.** Quantifying over arbitrary tasks and closure generators makes the theorem
either false or tautological. Properties X and operations Y must be fixed before a
proof can be assessed.

**OPEN.** A Pareto vector is not yet a cost model. It needs component definitions,
composition laws, scaling, precision and failure conventions, and a decision rule
for incomparable vectors.

**OPEN.** A closure can silently encode the conclusion. Restricting 3D or wave modes
must come from a stakeholder, fabrication regime, or established architecture class,
not from a desire to preserve separation.

### Occupation checks for the other candidates

- **HEURISTIC.** Electromagnetic channel mode counting is not open territory;
  [Miller](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.05427) gives a general waves-and-communications
  modes framework. EIT must use such physics honestly, not claim invention of modes.
- **HEURISTIC.** Type-2 and represented-space semantics already cover computation on
  infinite objects; [Pauly](https://arxiv.org/abs/0907.3230) is one entry point.
  PISC needs a new costed access contract, not merely infinite output.
- **HEURISTIC.** Online advice is a mature resource with lower-bound machinery; see
  [Boyar et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1408.7033). SFOC must distinguish fabrication
  from advice non-circularly.

This is a bounded occupation check, not a literature-completeness claim.

**RECOMMENDATION - CONJECTURE.** Candidate A wins narrowly because it is the only

whether a neutral information-cut model supports any honest, task-relevant region
between independently justified embedding classes.

# Part III - One primary direction and its first-gate design

## Primary direction: EIT with a Costed Closure Calculus

### Founding object

An EIT machine is a tuple of:

1. fabrication class and geometric embedding;
2. information-arrival stages;
3. admissible channel family and composition rules;
4. noise, failure, precision, and decoding contract;
5. explicit port-level task interface;
6. schedule, control, reconfiguration, and persistent-state contract; and
7. a partially ordered cost vector R = (A,V,T,E,D,P,I,B).

**CONJECTURE.** The object is new enough to investigate only if its cross-mechanism
closure theorem and its first frontier theorem are not already equivalent to a known
resource theory plus a known VLSI bound. This is an occupation obligation, not a
presumption of novelty.

## Registered task target EIT-0

### Post-Fabrication Permutation Transport

For parameters N, b, Q, error epsilon, and deadline delta:

1. A uniform transport fabric is fabricated before the instance is known.
2. A permutation sigma on N named ports arrives after fabrication. Its acquisition,
   storage, compilation, and distribution are charged.
3. Q batches follow. Each batch contains N independent b-bit words at named input
   ports. No compressibility or application structure is promised.
4. By the deadline, each word must be explicitly recoverable with block error at
   most epsilon at output port sigma(i). A pointer, label, shared latent field,
   retained input, or remote memory reference does not satisfy the task.
5. All encoding, modulation, switching, propagation, detection, decoding, control,
   state retention, and I/O crossing are charged.

**OPEN - STAKEHOLDER.** The provisional stakeholder is an on-package shuffle or
permutation interconnect under layer, area, latency, and energy constraints. Batch A
must replace this phrase with two independent sources showing that arbitrary or
adversarial permutations and explicit payload delivery are actually required. If it
cannot, the task is killed as an invented benchmark.

### VOID rule

**CONJECTURE.** If either class fails the same task interface, has an inconsistent
error convention, or receives different information at fabrication time, the
comparison is VOID rather than a win or loss.

## Independently justified competitor classes

The primary lower-bound class C2D is provisionally:

- constant or explicitly bounded routing layers;
- bounded component and channel density under a declared feature scale;
- bounded signal speed and per-mode reliable information rate;
- uniform pre-instance fabrication;
- arbitrary digital, analog, stochastic, coding, and scheduling operations that fit
  the geometry and pay the common cost vector; and
- no ban on optics or waves merely because they are optics or waves.

The positive candidates C3D, Cwave, and Chybrid must each receive separate frontiers.
They are not pooled into a favored super-class. Their extra volume, aperture, modes,
crossings, detectors, and controls are charged.

**CONJECTURE - RESTRICTION TEST.** C2D is legitimate only if a real fabrication or
packaging regime supplies the layer and density bounds before the theorem target is
chosen. If the restriction is instead chosen because C3D escapes it, issue
RESTRICTION-KILL.

## Registered theorem targets

### Target 1 - CCC-A: Costed Absorption

**CONJECTURE.** For typed processes and a componentwise or explicitly ordered cost
monoid, if a competitor closure contains the device primitive, interface maps, and
composition with no larger charged vector, it contains every task realization built
from those pieces with no larger vector. Therefore a strict Pareto separation must
identify a missing closure generator or a cost inequality.

**So what.** This converts symmetric-closure absorption into a reusable theorem and
forces every positive claim to name the exact non-absorbable resource.

**Kill condition.** If this is merely the definition of generated closure and no
nontrivial cost or minimal-generator statement survives, demote it to a definition;
do not advertise it as the cannot theorem.

### Target 2 - EIT-CUT: Neutral Information-Cut Lemma

**CONJECTURE.** If K independent b-bit payloads whose sources and destinations lie on
opposite sides of a cut must be materialized by time T with error at most epsilon,
then the reliable information carried across the cut is at least a stated
K,b,epsilon quantity. Any architecture must supply enough time-integrated reliable
cross-cut channel capacity. The statement must be valid for wires, waves, coding,
randomization, interaction, and mixed mechanisms under the same error model.

**So what.** The lower bound attaches to demanded information movement, not a chosen
substrate or algorithm.

**Kill condition.** If coding, broadcast, side information, retained state, or a
different task quotient invalidates the bound under the registered interface, stop.

### Target 3 - EIT-CANNOT-2D

**CONJECTURE.** Combine EIT-CUT with a source-backed bound on reliable channels
crossing a bounded-layer 2D cut to derive an explicit frontier inequality for EIT-0.
No asymptotic exponent is registered until dimensional assumptions and resource
units are frozen.

**So what.** This is the independently motivated cannot theorem.

**Kill condition.** A fair C2D counterembedding, an uncharged off-plane escape, or a
lower bound already identical in task and model kills novelty or correctness.

### Target 4 - EIT-CAN-3D

**CONJECTURE.** Give a uniform, explicit architecture and schedule in C3D, Cwave, or
Chybrid that completes EIT-0 and calculate every component of R, including the cost
of sigma, launch/detection, control, error correction, fabrication, and outputs.

**So what.** This establishes that a region beyond the C2D frontier is feasible, not
just that planar layouts are constrained.

**Kill condition.** If the construction pre-encodes sigma, omits a resource, relies
on growing precision or wavelength without cost, or has no nonempty asymptotic or
finite-parameter region, issue NO-REGION-KILL or LEAK-KILL.

### Target 5 - EIT-ROBUST

**CONJECTURE.** State the minimal closure additions and geometric relaxations that
absorb the separation. The result must survive at least one stronger neighboring
class than the initially chosen C2D, or be reported as a knife-edge conditional.

**So what.** Closure sensitivity becomes a theorem about the result rather than a
post-hoc fairness argument.

Registered outputs:

Batch-A exit is exactly one of EIT-CLEAR-TO-PROVE, OCCUPIED-KILL, TASK-KILL,

### Batch B - Theorem pair or death certificate

Only after EIT-CLEAR-TO-PROVE:

1. Prove EIT-CUT and submit it to a fresh adversarial proof reviewer.
2. Derive EIT-CANNOT-2D from an exact geometric channel lemma.
3. Specify one matching positive architecture, not a family name, and account for
   every resource component.
4. Exhibit a symbolic asymptotic region and at least one plausible finite-parameter
   witness. CPU-only computation may check algebra or constants; no GPU is needed or
   authorized.
5. Run closure perturbations: allow extra layers, 3D wires, coding, randomization,
   batching, state, and hybrid modes one at a time. Record the absorption boundary.
6. Send every would-be PROVED statement to fresh independent review. Until then it
   remains CONJECTURE or DERIVED.

Batch-B exit is exactly EIT-SEPARATION-REVIEWED, NO-REGION-KILL, ABSORBED,
OCCUPIED-KILL, LEAK-KILL, or FRAMEWORK-KILL.

## Evidence within two batches that kills the direction

Any one of the following is sufficient:

1. **Occupation death.** A prior framework plus prior theorem covers the same task,
   staged interface, embedding class, resource order, and matched construction. A
   new name or combined exposition is not enough.
2. **Task death.** Two independent sources do not support post-fabrication arbitrary
   permutation payload delivery as a real task, or realistic traffic structure lets
   a direct competitor fuse or compress the task exactly as in PFPWO.
3. **Restriction death.** The lower bound needs a planar restriction no target user
   actually faces, or the fair baseline naturally includes 2.5D or 3D channels that
   close the region.
4. **Counterembedding death.** A C2D construction with coding, serialization,
   randomized routing, state, or batching violates the claimed lower frontier under
   the frozen assumptions.
5. **Physical death.** The positive construction only wins by uncharged aperture,
   modes, shrinking wavelength or feature size, precision, detector energy, control,
   calibration, fabrication, state, or output work.
6. **No-region death.** Common accounting makes the proposed positive class obey the
   same cut frontier, or all finite and asymptotic candidate regions are empty.
7. **Novelty death.** CCC-A reduces to generated-closure membership and EIT-CUT to a
   textbook cut bound, with no new cost-preserving classification theorem.
8. **Mission death.** After two batches the only durable result is BLSD-like audit
   methodology. Retain it as the floor, issue FRAMEWORK-KILL, and do not claim the
   computation-model mission has been reframed successfully.

**CONJECTURE.** Evidence that merely makes the proof difficult is not a kill. Evidence
that invalidates the task, restriction, accounting, novelty, or nonempty region is.

# Part IV - Explicit disagreements with the supervisor for Round 2

## Disagreement 1 - The triple is not yet the founding object

**HEURISTIC.** A task-resource-closure triple is an excellent claim-audit record. It
is not yet a model on the scale demanded by invariant #1. Without operational machine
semantics and a positive feasible region, it is a vocabulary for comparisons. Round
2 should not call it the new model unless a closure-sensitive theorem is both
non-definitional and joined to a genuine can theorem.

## Disagreement 2 - The generic no-go is at risk of tautology

**DERIVED.** Closed under operation p implies that compositions using p remain in the
closure. Quantifying this over all tasks does not add teeth. The nontrivial theorem
must characterize cost preservation, a minimal generator set, or a natural closure
family that absorbs a non-obvious task class. I would demote an uncosted version to a
definition rather than advertise it as the cannot half.

## Disagreement 3 - Pareto is an answer format, not the founding cost model

**HEURISTIC.** Listing time, energy, area, and precision avoids premature
scalarization, but it does not say how resources compose or which trade is useful.
The founding decision remains the operational resource algebra and task interface.
Pareto frontiers come after those choices.

## Disagreement 4 - Occupation is a current blocker, not a later check

**HEURISTIC.** General resource theories already have the abstract shape the
supervisor proposes. The program must identify its unoccupied theorem-level delta in
Batch A. Application to physical-computing claims may still be valuable, but domain
application alone does not satisfy invariant #1.

## Disagreement 5 - Planar VLSI versus 3D wave is not analogous to quantum yet

**HEURISTIC.** Classical polynomial time is a broad, independently entrenched
competitor principle. Bounded-layer planar fabrication is a contingent architecture
regime, and waves are not the unique escape from it. 3D wires and hybrid packaging
may receive the same benefit. The honest theorem must compare embedding or channel
