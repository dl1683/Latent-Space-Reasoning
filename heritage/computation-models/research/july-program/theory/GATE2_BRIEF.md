## 2. Three task cards to adjudicate

### 2.1 Spatial stochastic state preparation

**SPECULATION — UNREGISTERED TASK CARD.**

- **Input:** a finite local energy model, geometry, temperature, and tolerance.
- **Output:** one physical configuration installed at named output sites and within a
  stakeholder-justified metric of the target.
- **Symmetry requirement:** both competitors physically instantiate the same output.
- **Charged resources:** programming, area, geometry, mixing, correlation,
  calibration, verification, precision, energy, and final materialization.
- **Mandatory baselines:** local digital stochastic fabrics, local randomized
  circuits/distributed processes, cluster and tempering algorithms, and matched
  parallelism/I/O.

### 2.2 Persistent distribution tracking

**SPECULATION — UNREGISTERED TASK CARD.**

- **Input:** a finite bounded-rate parameter path $\theta_t$.
- **Output:** a finite sample/action stream with cumulative downstream loss and lag.
- **Symmetry requirement:** both competitors retain warm implicit state.
- **Charged resources:** updates, transport/adaptation work, latency, state,
  calibration, communication, precision, energy, and readout.
- **Mandatory baselines:** online MCMC, sequential Monte Carlo, filtering, direct
  policies, dynamic-regret/transport formulations, and specialized streaming
  hardware.

### 2.3 Bounded nonlocal transition

**SPECULATION — UNREGISTERED TASK CARD.**

- **Input:** a specified multimodal mixture or Ising family.
- **Candidate operation:** a physically realized cluster flip, replica swap, or
  collective transition with bounded uniform control, communication, area, strength,
  precision, and noise.
- **Output:** a finite sample or mode-sensitive decision.
- **Symmetry requirement:** the baseline implements the identical transition with
  matched hardware before comparison with weaker generic samplers.
- **Mandatory question:** does a physical upper bound and digital/implementation
  lower-bound route survive after the global coupling and control fabric are fully
  charged?

## 3. Stage-A controls and verdict tokens

**HEURISTIC — REVIEW-INHERITED CONTROLS.** Each task card must face, in order:
implicit-state symmetry, oracle translation, direct-task substitution, algorithm
substitution, matched parallelism/hardware, matched physical locations, and matched
I/O/materialization. The gate must identify the allegedly mispriced primitive only
after these controls.

- **TASK-MISMATCH-CLEAR:** one finite useful task retains a stated asymptotic or
  Pareto mismatch after all symmetric baselines, and the smallest forced model
  distinction is identifiable.
- **ABSORBED:** the mismatch disappears under implicit state, oracle translation,
  direct-task substitution, matched hardware, or an established model with
  polynomial translation.
- **VOID:** the stakeholder task, physical boundary, or output metric changes during
  adjudication, making the registered comparison non-truth-apt.

These are **HEURISTIC PRECOMMIT TOKENS**, not current verdicts.

## 4. One bounded Lambda definition-falsification unit

**HEURISTIC — UNREGISTERED, BOUNDED FALSIFICATION AGENDA.** Lambda is not a founding
model proposal at this gate. One self-contained unit may test a proof-carrying
stochastic condition object against the following ten conjunctive conditions:

1. **Explicit object.** Define a finite transducer syntax, task metric, readout, and
   numeric condition/certificate object without reference to digital simulation
   cost.
2. **Uniform certificate provenance.** A charged uniform compiler constructs the
   certificate; user-supplied instance proofs may not carry answers or samples as
   advice.
3. **Machine checkability.** A fixed polynomial-time verifier derives every
   regularity, tail, amplification, chart, and readout bound used.
4. **Representation theorem.** Polynomially conditioned, polynomial-time $C^2$
   coordinate changes distort the resource polynomially; ill-conditioned charts are
   charged.
5. **Honest composition.** Sequential and parallel laws preserve the typed error
   contract; multiplicative amplification is not hidden behind an additive length
   whose simulator cost is exponential.
6. **Noncontractive witness.** A named useful family has polynomial resource through a
   uniformly generated certificate, not a promise that it mixes.
7. **Necessity witness.** A named mode-sensitive metastable family has provably
   superpolynomial resource under the same definition.
8. **Same-semantics BPP embedding.** Robust switched stochastic modules realize BPP
   with polynomial resources and no new clock, oracle, or readout rule.
9. **Strict theorem delta.** The result is not obtained by applying BGP,
   Blanc–Bournez, standard SDE discretization, spectral-gap/log-Sobolev/mixing
   theory, or channel contraction separately and adding an interface.
10. **Task forcing.** A task that has already survived Stage A actually requires the
    condition object.

**HEURISTIC — FIREWALL VERDICT RULE.** Failure of conditions 1–7 or 9 is **KILL** for
this Lambda proposal. Conditions 8 and 10 are mission conditions: without them a
condition theorem may be useful but is not the program's model. Passing all ten is
only **CLEAR-TO-FORMALIZE**, not a proof, novelty finding, or model selection.
