## Part 2 — Candidate dossiers

### 2.1 Candidate A — FSTT-2C

#### Exact conjunction

**DEFINITION — HEURISTIC, TO BE FROZEN IN BATCH 4.** For a finite input \(x\) and
requested accuracy \(2^{-p}\), a constructor supplies:

1. a finitely encoded preparation law \(P_{x,p}\) on a finitely encoded initial
   state;
2. a declared horizon \(T(x,p)\), with finite Markov kernels
   \(K_{x,p,1},\ldots,K_{x,p,T}\);
3. a finite stochastic readout kernel \(R_{x,p}\) to strings of polynomially bounded
   length;
4. one charged uniform compiler producing finite-bit samplers
   \(P'_{x,p},K'_{x,p,t},R'_{x,p}\), their runtime/randomness bounds, and checkable
   per-component TV error bounds;
5. the noncircular composition law

   \[
   \operatorname{TV}(P K_1\cdots K_T R,
   P'K'_1\cdots K'_T R')
   \leq \epsilon_{\rm prep}+\sum_{t=1}^{T}\epsilon_t+
   \epsilon_{\rm read};
   \]

6. two genuinely different constructor lemmas using the same finite-output
   semantics:
   - **D-LSN:** a finite rational local stochastic network / probabilistic circuit,
     whose local kernels have exact or explicitly approximated finite samplers; and
   - **CTMJ:** a finite-state continuous-time Markov jump / kinetic-Ising network
     with rational bounded rates and polynomial total exit-rate–time product, whose
     event clock, uniformization, Poisson tail, preparation, calibration, and finite
     readout are derived by a charged compiler rather than promised as terminal-law
     correctness; and
7. an end-to-end cost vector charging description/construction, programming,
   certificate generation/checking, preparation, transitions, ordinary work,
   random bits, precision, state, time, calibration, readout, and output bits.

Here the TV bound is not the proposed novelty by itself. The candidate theorem is the
whole constructor-to-finite-output statement and its shared class consequence.

#### Why the ingredients are old but the assembly remains open in the record

| Ingredient | Record evidence — labelled scope | Missing conjunct |
|---|---|---|
| Deterministic input/evolution/output simulation | **PROVED (literature), REVIEW-VERIFIED:** BGP has representation, initialization, robust deterministic evolution, output margins, and explicit numerical cost. | BGP is deterministic and tied to its ODE representation; it is not a finite stochastic output theorem or a constructor for arbitrary physical descriptions. |
| Robust reachability/evaluation for dynamical systems | **PROVED (literature), REVIEW-VERIFIED:** Blanc–Bournez connect time/length/precision robustness to PTIME/PSPACE in their stated models. | No stochastic terminal law, preparation/readout kernel pair, or cross-substrate constructor. |
| Stochastic dynamics and computable measures | **PROVED (literature), REVIEW-VERIFIED:** BGR computes invariant measures under explicit regularity, noise, and mixing hypotheses. | Equilibrium/invariant measure rather than finite-horizon preparation-to-readout transduction; no two-constructor theorem. |
| Finite-kernel error composition | **PROVED BY REVIEW (elementary):** Markov contraction plus telescoping gives additive TV error; stochastic readout does not increase it. | Does not construct kernels or certify their physical provenance. |

**EMPIRICAL SOURCE-CLOSURE FINDING, REVIEW-VERIFIED.** Review 1 reports that none of
the ten examined primary sources contains all four of preparation, finite stochastic
output, a uniform family constructor, and noncircular composition across more than
one substrate. `research/NOVELTY_CHECK.md` section 9 preserves that gap as an open
problem and corrects the predecessor uniformity cells to partial.

**HEURISTIC — NO LITERATURE-WIDE NOVELTY CLAIM.** The ten-source absence is enough to

#### CAN + CANNOT pair

**CONJECTURE — CAN-1, decision completeness.** The D-LSN constructor uniformly
embeds every polynomial-size probabilistic circuit, so every BPP language has a
polynomial-cost FSTT-2C decision realization with finite readout.

**CONJECTURE — CAN-2, useful continuous-time capability.** The CTMJ constructor
produces inverse-polynomial-TV finite spin outputs for a uniformly described
bounded-degree high-temperature / Dobrushin-unique Ising family with rational
interactions, a generated mixing/rate certificate, charged preparation, and finite
readout.

**CONJECTURE — CANNOT, finite-stochastic ceiling.** Every polynomial-cost FSTT-2C
sampling family is in SampBPP, and every bounded-error decision family is in BPP.
Consequently, any claimed super-BPP classical capability must violate a named
premise: polynomial horizon, uniform finite sampler construction, finite
preparation/readout, polynomial total jump-rate–time product, polynomial certificate
generation, or polynomial total cost.

**HEURISTIC — ASYMMETRY RISK.** CAN-1 and the CANNOT may collapse to the standard
BPP machine characterization, while CAN-2 may collapse to uniformization plus a
standard high-temperature Glauber-mixing theorem.
The conjunction earns theorem work only if the two constructor lemmas create a
strict theorem delta beyond those separate facts. This is a Gate-0 question, not a
positive conclusion.

#### Tractability within two batches

**HEURISTIC — HIGH FOR GATE 0, MEDIUM FOR THEOREM.** The clocked constructor and TV
telescope are elementary. The continuous-time constructor requires explicit
uniformization, finite rate arithmetic, Poisson-event truncation, a generated
high-temperature mixing certificate, calibration, and readout accounting. One batch can
decide occupation, constructor honesty, and strict delta. A second batch is enough
for the narrow proof only if the continuous certificate is fixed rather than
invented during proof.

### 2.3 Candidate C — Proof-Carrying Stochastic Condition Object

#### Exact conjunction

**DEFINITION TARGET — HEURISTIC.** PCSCO is Lambda's ten-condition firewall read

1. finite transducer syntax, task metric, readout, and a numeric condition/certificate

2. a charged uniform certificate generator, with no user-supplied instance advice;
3. a fixed polynomial-time verifier deriving every regularity, tail, amplification,
   chart, and readout bound used by the simulator;
4. polynomial distortion under polynomially conditioned polynomial-time \(C^2\)

5. honest sequential and parallel composition laws for resources and typed error;
6. a named useful noncontractive family with a uniformly generated polynomial

7. a named mode-sensitive metastable family with provably superpolynomial resource
   under the same definition;
8. a same-semantics BPP embedding using robust switched stochastic modules;
9. a strict theorem delta beyond BGP, Blanc–Bournez, standard SDE discretization,
   spectral-gap/log-Sobolev/mixing theory, and channel contraction separately; and
10. a positive typed task that already survived Stage A and forces this object.

#### Old ingredients and unclaimed assembly status

**HEURISTIC — OLD-INGREDIENT ASSESSMENT FROM THE RECORDED LITERATURE MAP.**

#### CAN + CANNOT pair

**CONJECTURE — CAN.** A useful named noncontractive family and every BPP computation

**CONJECTURE — CANNOT.** Polynomial-PCSCO processes are weakly simulable in
