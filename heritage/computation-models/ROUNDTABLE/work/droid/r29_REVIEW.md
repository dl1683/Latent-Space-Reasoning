## The statement actually attacked

Fix length n, source space S, description bound p, a source M and ordinary target U. **H1:** equal input, initial state, advice, *joint* randomness and oracle access, including environment correlations; charged encoded state, scratch, sampling and precision, no uncharged hidden tables. **H2:** for each instruction a of each P of size at most p, ONE uniformly compiled macro C(P,a) works for EVERY reachable pre-state, not one per state. It includes program fetch/dispatch; uses at most f(n,S,p) target steps and h(n,S,p) peak live space; preserves its conditional full endpoint and public-trace law and an invariant encoded state, with no stronger oracle. Generation/setup costs at most g(n,S,p). **H3:** macro composition preserves all permitted observations/interventions, including private-state adaptive schedulers; interiors cannot disclose what source atomic steps hide. Timed observables must actually match or the claim is explicitly a rescaled *different* contract. **H4:** identical success contract and honest charges for setup, messages, precision, scratch, calls and restoration; the comparator admits the compiled program and its h-space execution. These are restatements, not replacement hypotheses; see source for exact wording.

**Conclusion, conditional:** Any source P with worst-case T steps has target Q with the same *joint transcript law*, time at most `g+fT`, peak space at most h and a charged description. In particular `OPT_U(n,h) <= g+f OPT_M(n,S,p)` with compatible program budget, promises and worst-case contracts. For f>0, a target time lower bound L yields `OPT_M >= max(0,(L-g)/f)`; a source lower bound does *not* transfer forward. A *multiplicative* no-super-polylog source speed advantage needs f polylog, g/T polylog (the source says "setup negligible"), feasible h and same metric. For literal equal-cost instructions with `f=1,g=0,h<=S`, strategy-class inclusion gives `OPT_U<=OPT_M` for any identical-score task, even online. Expected stopping-time bounds require expectations and do not become worst-case. Time lowering alone says nothing about probes, communication, energy or space. Source: Droid, *The translation wall, version 1* (2026).

**Weakest premise: H4** as a falsification condition, since "honest" and "target admits the compiled program" can conceal arbitrary differences in target budget and price vector. **Hardest substantive obligation: H2** (the single macro and its dispatch, exact joint conditional law and *intermediate* space). The H2 "public trace" clause and H3 "scheduler sees no interiors" overlap; they are not logically independent unless quiescent kernel equality is the H2 contract and scheduler compositionality is the H3 contract. Strictly, H4's demand that the target "admits the compiled program" *presupposes* the existence of an H2 compilation; no literally isolated H2 failure exists under that existential reading of H4. The H2 row below means "same admission *budget for any legal target program*, but no legal macro." H1's joint-law clause similarly overlaps kernel semantics if the environment is placed inside the H2 pre-state.

**Proof attack (CONJECTURE, author-family):** There is no clean all-four countermodel: couple initial joint configurations by H1; for a common conditional state/history H2 gives one legal macro with the same next endpoint and emission, while H3 gives the environment identical information to select the next instruction; induct to T. H4 admits the Q thus built and sums at most f per instruction plus one setup g. I tested two attempted attacks: replacing `exists one macro, forall states` with `forall states, exists macro` hardwires unknown state; allowing equal marginal random bits but not their correlation breaks full-history equality. Both violate literal premises, as round 23 already noted. The hand induction adds no fresh-independent certification. Juris Hartmanis and Richard Stearns, *On the Computational Complexity of Algorithms* (1965), supply the established machine-complexity context, not priority for this exact formulation.

**Tautology/triviality verdict:** The literal-instruction inclusion corollary is exactly "a subset of admissible strategies cannot beat its superset" under an identical score. The generalized `g+fT` lemma is the equally standard compiler induction, **not literally a subset argument** when instructions differ: it forces explicit *uniform conditional kernels, dispatch, live scratch, adversarial atomicity and setup*, which are real audit obligations. Its one nontrivial role here is detecting when a proposed "ordinary comparator" secretly lacks the candidate's access/trace/timing contract. No novel impossibility follows.

All have private scratch and boundary-only public replies, no mid-macro deadlines. Interpreter P is the same read-only indexed control tape on both sides; the fixed target interpreter pays **one target step per fetch/branch dispatch** even if the source also pays a fetch. Its fixed instruction semantics need no generated target code, so g=0 and P counts toward p. Branches use the same one-bit branch selector and prewritten addresses; arbitrary decoding on a bit-only tape would need a larger f. The *peak* spaces count scratch during execution, not just at endpoints.
