Lemma (Droid, elementary): connected graph + diagonals => the generated semigroup contains GL_n => any product-closed regular-image family containing the format needs m >= n^2 parameters. Consequence: the lemma's hypotheses hold for butterfly, Monarch, order-1 HSS and operator-pair-specific displacement rank <= 3; the r = 2 membership is checked at six t values only (not all t) and is therefore EMPIRICAL. ## PRIOR ART (targeted web search, 2026-10-03; not systematic)
Closest published framework: "Structured matrix factorization length" (arXiv 2606.07407, https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07407): for an affine variety X of structured matrices it defines the r-th X-factorization variety (Zariski closure of products of r members of X), the factorization length, and computes the DIMENSIONS of these varieties for Toeplitz, Hankel, bidiagonal, tridiagonal, skew-symmetric and companion matrices. Our Part 1 and Part 3 are the same kind of object (dimension of the k-fold product variety) for MONARCH and BUTTERFLY structure; the search found no Monarch or butterfly dimension formulas in that paper's listed cases, nor in Dao et al. 2022 (Monarch: expressive structured matrices; describes Monarch as permuted block matrices with rank-1 blocks) or Kaleidoscope (O(n) factor bound). So the Monarch exact law min{q^4, (k+1)q^3 - kq^2} (prime-power q proved by the affine-plane witness; q = 6 numerical) and the butterfly law min{n^2, n(k log2 n + 1)} (verified n <= 32) are, as far as this search shows, new instances inside a known framework, NOT a new framework. Novelty remains UNESTABLISHED until the full text of 2606.07407 and the Monarch/Kaleidoscope sources are read; also Ye-Lim 2016 (Toeplitz factorisation) and Pan (displacement growth) cover other cells.
