**1. Substantive Concerns Still Not Fully Addressed**

There are no remaining mechanism-level blockers. The EOS/completion framing, extractor-selection caveat, budget-vs-path-vs-verbosity distinction, and discovery-set overfitting risk are all now recognized.

There are, however, three remaining substantive cleanup items:

1. **Compute parity is still incomplete.**  
   The current sweep stops greedy at `2048`, but prefix `N=10 x 1024` costs roughly 10k generated-token budget per task. A `2048` greedy result can kill prefix if it wins, but a failure at `2048` does not prove prefix is Pareto-efficient. Add adaptive greedy continuation to `4096` and possibly `8192` or until completion/saturation, at least for tasks still truncated at `2048`.

2. **Response-level CIs are overconfident because of task clustering.**  
   The `100/100` EOS correctness lower bound assumes response-level independence. But responses are nested inside 25 tasks and repeated seeds. Report response-level stats, but also use task-level or cluster-bootstrap intervals. This matters for `P(correct|EOS)`, EOS-rate comparisons, and “any EOS seed implies plurality success.”

3. **The preregistration is internally stale.**

Close, but not fully calibrated.

Do not claim generality across small quantized models, do not claim exact `P(correct|EOS)=1.0` as a stable law, and do not claim prefix beats increasing budget until the sweep is done.
