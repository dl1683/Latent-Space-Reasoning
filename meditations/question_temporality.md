# Question Temporality?

## When Is A Question Too Early?

- What evidence does this question require that does not yet exist?
- What source does this question require that has not yet been read?
- What verifier does this question require that has not yet earned trust?
- What benchmark does this question require that has not yet been stabilized?
- What mechanism does this question require that is still only metaphor?
- What decision would this question pretend to control before it actually can?
- What would make waiting more intellectually honest than answering?

## When Is A Question Too Late?

- What question should have been asked before the claim was written?
- What question should have been asked before the GPU run?
- What question should have been asked before the README sentence?
- What question should have been asked before the metric became a headline?
- What question should have been asked before the artifact became authoritative?
- What question should have been asked before the dead end accumulated followers?
- What signal reveals that the question has arrived after the damage?

## When Has A Question Ripened?

- What new evidence makes the question sharper than when it was written?
- What new failure makes the question more necessary?
- What new source changes the question from vague to testable?
- What new artifact gives the question an object?
- What new disagreement gives the question a boundary?
- What new forecast gives the question a horizon?
- What new negative result makes the question more valuable?

## When Should A Question Sleep?

- What question should stop receiving live attention until more evidence arrives?
- What question should be left in the archive to consolidate with later sources?
- What question should not be answered during the same session in which it was born?
- What question needs distance before its flattering assumptions become visible?
- What question needs sleep because every immediate answer is too easy?
- What question needs offline recombination with other questions?
- What question would become worse if forced into action now?

## When Should A Question Be Replayed?

- What question should be replayed after a failure?
- What question should be replayed after a success?
- What question should be replayed after a new source?
- What question should be replayed after a benchmark changes?
- What question should be replayed after a term drifts?
- What question should be replayed after a future agent inherits the work?
- What replay would improve future action rather than produce a convincing recap?

## When Should A Question Decay?

- What question should lose retrieval priority with time?
- What question should decay unless refreshed by new evidence?
- What question should decay unless it changes a decision?
- What question should decay unless it survives a stricter metric?
- What question should decay unless it transfers across contexts?
- What question should decay unless it remains cheaper than the attention it consumes?
- What decay rule would prevent old uncertainty from competing with live uncertainty?

## When Should A Question Consolidate?

- What repeated questions should become one schema?
- What repeated failures should become one sharper question?
- What repeated overclaims should become one standing gate?
- What repeated source distinctions should become one evidence rule?
- What repeated metric splits should become one strict-metric question?
- What repeated replay failures should become one memory-governance question?
- What consolidation would preserve decision-relevant state instead of compressing everything into a story?

## When Should A Question Become Evidence Work?

- What question has a clear object?
- What question has a clear falsifier?
- What question has a cheap first probe?
- What question has a forecast horizon short enough to learn from?
- What question has enough evidence liquidity to avoid thin-market confidence?
- What question has a cost boundary that prevents runaway build momentum?
- What question would be dishonored by more meditation because it now requires contact with the world?

## When Is A Question Computationally Irreducible?

- What question cannot be answered without running the system?
- What question cannot be predicted faster than the process unfolds?
- What question can only be forecast distributionally rather than pointwise?
- What question has a Lyapunov horizon beyond which exact prediction is fake?
- What question is better handled by robustness than prediction?
- What question should become a cheap probe because reasoning alone cannot cross the boundary?
- What question should admit that simulation is the shortest explanation?

## When Does A Forecast Belong Beside A Question?

- What question should carry a probability rather than only a phrase?
- What question should carry a confidence interval rather than a claim?
- What question should carry a base rate before a story?
- What question should carry a forecast horizon before a decision?
- What question should carry a calibration record before trust?
- What question should carry a market-like dissent signal before consensus?
- What question should carry an explicit condition under which the forecast should update?

## When Does Prediction Distort The Question?

- What question changes the system by being asked?
- What question becomes Goodharted once it is measured?
- What question becomes strategic once agents know it is the target?
- What question becomes reflexive once public?
- What question becomes less predictive when used for control?
- What question should stay private until its measurement cannot be gamed?
- What question needs robustness analysis more than a point forecast?

## When Should The Meditation Stop For Now?

- What question now needs measurement instead of more questions?
- What question now needs pruning instead of more questions?
- What question now needs sleep instead of more questions?
- What question now needs an external source instead of more questions?
- What question now needs a failed prediction instead of more questions?
- What question now needs a future agent instead of more questions?
- What question would prove that stopping is discipline rather than avoidance?
