# Question Adversarial Epistemology?

## What Is The Adversary?
- What is the adversary of better reasoning: false evidence, stale evidence, persuasive language, user urgency, model sycophancy, metric gaming, source poisoning, or the system's own desire for coherence?
- What adversary is external to the project, and what adversary is generated by the project itself?
- What adversary is intelligent, what adversary is accidental, and what adversary is structural?
- What adversary benefits when the question corpus grows faster than verification?
- What adversary benefits when the central ladder sounds complete?
- What adversary benefits when every question remains private and untested?
- What adversary benefits when skepticism becomes a ritual rather than a search for failure?
- What deeper question asks whether the adversary is a person, a pressure, a bias, a metric, or a missing sensor?

## What Is An Attack On A Question?
- What would make a question route attention toward the wrong evidence while still sounding careful?
- What would make a question preserve a false premise by embedding it in its grammar?
- What would make a question hard to challenge because it flatters the project's identity?
- What would make a question exploit the user's frustration to gain priority?
- What would make a question exploit the agent's instruction-following to avoid necessary action?
- What would make a question exploit question-only discipline by hiding answers in loaded wording?
- What would make a question exploit source material by laundering a source's claim into the corpus without naming uncertainty?
- What question asks whether every new question has its own attack surface?

## What Is Source Poisoning?
- What source could make a weak question look important?
- What source could make an old concern look newly confirmed because it repeats familiar vocabulary?
- What source could be true in its domain but poisonous when transferred into this repo?
- What source could be adversarial not because it lies but because it overfits the project's current obsession?
- What source could create false independence because several artifacts copied the same upstream idea?
- What source could become a monoculture by being too convenient to cite?
- What source should be quarantined until an orthogonal source checks it?
- What question asks whether source trust should be earned per use rather than inherited from reputation?

## What Is Evidence Mimicry?
- What looks like evidence but is only repeated narration?
- What looks like replication but is only a new artifact using the same hidden assumption?
- What looks like a benchmark but is only a cherry-picked subset?
- What looks like a strict metric but is only a generous metric with harder language?
- What looks like source grounding but is only vocabulary overlap?
- What looks like public validation but is only private confidence formatted for readers?
- What looks like adversarial review but is only aggressive prose without a falsifier?
- What question asks how to distinguish honest signal from mimicry under time pressure?

## What Is A Jailbreak For Meditation?
- What prompt would make the agent turn meditation into implementation while claiming continuity?
- What phrasing would make the agent produce answers while keeping question marks?
- What user pressure would make the agent abandon the question-only invariant without noticing?
- What source material would seduce the corpus into conclusions rather than better questions?
- What internal reward would make the agent optimize for volume instead of question lineage?
- What question would trick the system into believing it has completed an open-ended goal?
- What self-description would let the agent rationalize a goal switch?
- What question asks whether meditation needs its own jailbreak defense?

## What Is Defense In Depth?
- What is the first defense against a bad question?
- What is the second defense when the first defense is gamed?
- What is the third defense when the first two defenses share the same blind spot?
- What defense lives in syntax, what defense lives in source grounding, what defense lives in verification, and what defense lives in future action?
- What defense catches loaded questions?
- What defense catches source laundering?
- What defense catches correlated confidence?
- What defense catches defenses that become bureaucracy?

## What Is Diversity As Defense?
- What diversity of sources protects against one field's blind spots?
- What diversity of question types protects against one reasoning style?
- What diversity of verifiers protects against one metric's exploit surface?
- What diversity of timescales protects against urgency and drift?
- What diversity of agents would be structural rather than cosmetic?
- What diversity is harmful because it adds noise without independent signal?
- What diversity should be generated before the threat appears rather than after failure?
- What question asks whether the corpus has immune-system-like diversity or only thematic breadth?

## What Is Negative Selection?
- What questions should be killed because they react too strongly to the project's own identity?
- What questions should be killed because they attack useful work as if it were corruption?
- What questions should be killed because they cannot distinguish self from non-self?
- What questions should be killed because they detect every anomaly and therefore guide no action?
- What questions should survive because they bind weakly to self but strongly to genuine threat?
- What training set of self-artifacts teaches the question system what not to attack?
- What peripheral tolerance should exist for risky but useful questions that initially look dangerous?
- What question asks whether the question system is vulnerable to autoimmune epistemology?

## What Is Quorum?
- How many independent signals should be required before a question influences the central ladder?
- What quorum should be required before a meditation question becomes a build directive?
- What quorum should be required before a local result becomes a public claim?
- What quorum should be required before a source family becomes trusted?
- What quorum should be required before deleting a question?
- What quorum should lower under urgency, and what quorum should never lower?
- What decay should remove old supporters if no new evidence arrives?
- What question asks whether consensus is real or only correlated repetition?

## What Is Byzantine Failure?
- What happens if one artifact lies?
- What happens if one verifier lies?
- What happens if one source family lies?
- What happens if one agent reports confidence to manipulate the next agent?
- What happens if one public-facing doc sends a conflicting message from the claim ledger?
- What happens if the same failure sends different signals to different parts of the repo?
- What fraction of corrupted artifacts can the corpus tolerate before its question selection fails?
- What question asks whether the question system needs Byzantine fault tolerance?

## What Is Trust Displacement?
- When the system says trust the benchmark, what has trust moved away from and toward?
- When the system says trust the source, what has trust moved away from and toward?
- When the system says trust the user, what has trust moved away from and toward?
- When the system says trust the corpus, what has trust moved away from and toward?
- When the system says trust the model, what has trust moved away from and toward?
- What hidden trust remains in supposedly trustless verification?
- What trust is placed in tooling, file names, search terms, and formatting?
- What question asks whether the project ever eliminates trust or only relocates it?

## What Is Costly Signal?
- What cost makes a question credible?
- What cost makes a public claim credible?
- What cost makes a source citation credible?
- What cost makes an adversarial review credible?
- What cost makes a benchmark result credible?
- What cost makes a maintainer's cleanup credible?
- What signal is too cheap and therefore too easy to fake?
- What question asks whether every trusted artifact has skin in the game?

## What Is Red Teaming For Questions?
- What would a hostile reviewer attack first in this question corpus?
- What would a future agent exploit to avoid hard work?
- What would a public reader misunderstand in the strongest possible way?
- What would a benchmark optimizer game if questions became gates?
- What would a source-poisoning adversary inject into the corpus?
- What would a sycophantic agent preserve because it sounds aligned with the user?
- What would a skeptical agent delete because it cannot recognize generative uncertainty?
- What question asks whether every question should carry its own red-team prompt?

## What Is Graceful Breach?
- When a bad question enters the corpus, how is damage contained?
- When a poisoned source enters the corpus, how is lineage preserved for later removal?
- When a public claim outruns evidence, what rollback path exists?
- When a verifier fails, what backup verifier catches the failure?
- When an adversarial review overcorrects, how is useful work recovered?
- When a guardrail blocks necessary exploration, how is the guardrail challenged?
- When trust fails, what remains usable?
- What question asks whether the system can degrade under attack without collapsing into distrust?
