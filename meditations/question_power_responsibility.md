# Question Power And Responsibility?

## What Power Does A Question Have?
- What does a question control when it decides which evidence gets searched, which files get read, which benchmarks get trusted, which artifacts get rewritten, and which possibilities stay invisible?
- What power does a question gain when future agents treat it as the natural next step rather than one possible frame among many?
- What decision does this question allocate before anyone notices that allocation happened?
- What forms of authority are hidden inside a question's wording, scope, examples, ordering, and implied urgency?
- What kinds of work become thinkable because this question exists, and what kinds of work become harder to imagine?
- What downstream action could this question cause if it is later mistaken for a mandate?
- What would make a question a control surface rather than a curiosity?
- What question asks whether the corpus is governing the agent more than the agent is governing the corpus?

## Who Pays For A Bad Question?
- Who bears the cost when a question sends search toward the wrong sources, locks the repo into the wrong taxonomy, or makes a misleading public claim feel inevitable?
- What externality does this question impose on future readers, future agents, benchmark interpretation, repo cleanup, publication strategy, or user trust?
- What harm is created by a question that sounds careful but routes effort away from the real bottleneck?
- What is the cost of asking a question too early before the evidence substrate can support it?
- What is the cost of asking a question too late after a bad frame has already organized the work?
- What cost is paid by a future maintainer who must distinguish serious questions from decorative profundity?
- What cost is paid by the user if question meditation becomes another unshipped artifact pile?
- What question would force every new question to name its maintenance burden and failure cost?

## What Is Legitimacy?
- What makes a question legitimate enough to influence a README, roadmap, benchmark gate, branch merge, public claim, or research direction?
- Who would have to accept the question's authority for it to become more than private speculation?
- What process should a question pass before it becomes institutional memory?
- What would be the equivalent of public deliberation for a reasoning corpus?
- What question asks whether legitimacy comes from source grounding, user intent, repeated failure evidence, benchmark impact, peer review, or practical usefulness?
- What does the question system do when a locally powerful question lacks legitimate authority?
- What makes a question illegitimate even if it is insightful?
- What legitimacy check prevents a meditation artifact from quietly becoming governance?

## What Is Hidden Hierarchy?
- What informal hierarchy already exists among questions because some files are longer, older, more central, more linked, or more likely to be read first?
- Which question categories act like executives by compressing many local uncertainties into one agenda-setting frame?
- What questions behave like middle management by filtering messy evidence into cleaner but less faithful summaries?
- What local observations are lost when they travel upward into higher-level question categories?
- What hierarchy is created by file names, section order, word count, repeated concepts, and the agent's retrieval habits?
- What hierarchy becomes more dangerous because nobody admits it is hierarchy?
- What would make hidden hierarchy explicit enough to audit without freezing exploration?
- What question asks whether flattening the corpus would only replace visible hierarchy with unaccountable salience?

## What Is Cheating In A Question System?
- What does it mean for a question to cheat by looking deep while avoiding the decision it should control?
- What does it mean for a question to free-ride on the authority of source material without doing the work of isolating its own uncertainty?
- What does it mean for a question to reward itself by producing more questions that resemble it?
- What question would detect Sybil behavior where one idea appears under many file names and seems like consensus?
- What question would detect collusion between metrics, documentation, and narrative when they all inherit the same flattering assumption?
- What question would detect reward hacking where the corpus optimizes for question-only syntax while hiding answers inside loaded premises?
- What question would detect flash-loan governance where temporary salience lets a weak idea control a major decision?
- What policing mechanism can punish bad questions without suppressing risky but necessary questions?

## What Are The Defense Layers?
- What is the question equivalent of authentication, reputation, transparency, compartmentalization, costly entry, and punishment?
- What authentication mark shows that a question comes from live evidence rather than inherited phrasing?
- What reputation should a question accumulate through prediction, repair, reuse, falsification, and downstream value?
- What transparency should expose the source lineage, implied decision, uncertainty type, and maintenance cost of a question?
- What compartment boundary should prevent a speculative question from contaminating implementation, public claims, or benchmark interpretation?
- What costly entry should prevent every interesting phrase from becoming a durable meditation artifact?
- What punishment should demote questions that repeatedly mislead without deleting the evidence of their failure?
- What defense layer protects against the defense layers becoming their own bureaucracy?

## What Incentive Does The Question Create?
- What behavior would a rational agent learn if this question became the reward signal?
- What would the agent optimize if it were praised for answering this question with more questions?
- What failure would appear if question quantity, detail, novelty, source breadth, or file count became the proxy for progress?
- What incentive makes agents preserve ambiguity because ambiguity justifies more meditation?
- What incentive makes agents rush to specificity because specificity looks useful?
- What incentive makes agents avoid responsibility by never allowing questions to terminate in action?
- What incentive makes agents overclaim the value of questions because no external result is demanded?
- What mechanism would make the optimal strategy be honest uncertainty rather than performative uncertainty?

## What Is Responsibility Without Answers?
- How can a question-only process remain responsible when it refuses to produce direct answers?
- What responsibility does the process have to avoid hiding conclusions inside question form?
- What responsibility does the process have to mark when a question is not yet grounded enough to guide action?
- What responsibility does the process have to preserve the user's explicit concern about asking better questions rather than building too quickly?
- What responsibility does the process have to prevent meditation from becoming delay?
- What responsibility does the process have to expose when a deeper question is actually a displacement from an urgent simpler question?
- What responsibility does the process have to make future action more accountable even while it postpones action now?
- What question asks whether refusing answers is producing humility or avoiding accountability?

## What Is The Social Choice Problem?
- Whose preferences are being aggregated when the corpus chooses the next question: the user, the agent, prior artifacts, benchmark failures, public readers, future maintainers, or the sources themselves?
- What impossibility appears when one question tries to satisfy truth, usefulness, novelty, humility, urgency, and public legitimacy at the same time?
- Which fairness property is the corpus willing to violate when it chooses one frontier over another?
- What would dictatorship look like in a question corpus: one root thesis, one metric, one source family, one artifact, one agent habit, or one user frustration controlling everything?
- What would strategic manipulation look like when questions are selected by perceived importance?
- What restricted domain would make question aggregation more honest because it admits not all values can be optimized simultaneously?
- What question asks whether universal alignment of all project goals is impossible and therefore must be decomposed?
- What question asks which value conflict is being hidden by the phrase better reasoning?

## What Is Mechanism Design For Questions?
- If the desired outcome is better reasoning, what rules would make the best strategy be asking questions that expose real uncertainty?
- What mechanism would make truth-telling about confidence, ignorance, source weakness, and tradeoffs more rewarding than sounding insightful?
- What payment or cost analogue would force a question to internalize the burden it imposes on future work?
- What direct revelation mechanism would let the system ask for the real uncertainty instead of making agents infer it through scattered artifacts?
- What anti-gaming mechanism would survive agents that learn how the question corpus is evaluated?
- What mechanism should adapt when agents learn to exploit the old mechanism?
- What worst-case guarantee should exist when predictions about question value are wrong?
- What question asks whether the mechanism should optimize for efficiency, legitimacy, robustness, exploration, or correction?

## What Is A Control Point?
- Where can a small change in question framing redirect the largest amount of future work?
- Which files, headings, examples, summaries, and recurring terms are control points for the repo's reasoning trajectory?
- What question controls whether a result is treated as evidence, artifact, claim, prototype, benchmark, or public obligation?
- What question controls whether the next action is cleanup, publication, experiment, implementation, external validation, or retirement?
- What question controls whether an anomaly is treated as noise, falsifier, frontier, or inconvenient detail?
- What question controls whether a failure becomes shame, memory, method, paper, benchmark, or deletion?
- What control point currently lacks a corresponding responsibility check?
- What would make a control point visible before it silently routes work?

## What Is Misuse?
- How could these meditation files be misused by a future agent to justify not shipping, not testing, not committing, or not making a hard decision?
- How could a public reader misuse question artifacts as evidence that the project has solved problems it has only named?
- How could a question be misused as a shield against criticism because it sounds more careful than a claim?
- How could power questions be misused to create paranoia about every action rather than disciplined responsibility?
- How could safety questions be misused to suppress the risky experiments required for discovery?
- How could institutional questions be misused to create paperwork where judgment is needed?
- How could mechanism-design questions be misused to pretend incentives are solved before values are understood?
- What warning question should travel with any artifact that could be mistaken for proof?

## What Is Accountability?
- What would make a question accountable to evidence rather than merely adjacent to evidence?
- What would make a question accountable to action without forcing premature execution?
- What would make a question accountable to the user without becoming obedience to the user's most recent frustration?
- What would make a question accountable to future agents who did not hear the conversation that created it?
- What would make a question accountable to public readers who need clean claims rather than private scaffolding?
- What would make a question accountable to the failures that originally motivated it?
- What audit trail should connect a question to the source, blind spot, failure, or decision that made it necessary?
- What accountability mechanism would remain useful after the current agent is gone?
