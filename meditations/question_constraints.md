# Question Constraints?

## What Constraint Makes A Question Useful?
- What constraint turns this question from an atmosphere into a decision surface?
- What constraint would make the next question smaller, harder, and more consequential?
- What constraint would make the question answerable only by changing what the system attends to?
- What constraint would force the question to name the variable, domain, metric, and failure condition it depends on?
- What constraint would make a vague moonshot become a finite experiment rather than a mood?
- What constraint would expose whether the question is asking for understanding, performance, evidence, governance, or deployment?
- What constraint would stop the question from expanding until it becomes impossible to falsify?
- What constraint would show whether the question is a search operator or just a container for excitement?
- What constraint would preserve novelty while removing empty freedom?
- What constraint would make the question earn more attention than the other possible questions?

## What Is The Search-Space Constraint?
- What is the combinatorial space this question opens?
- What parts of that space are obviously unproductive and should be pruned before any build begins?
- What constraint would reduce the space enough that the remaining questions can be explored with human attention, repo evidence, and bounded compute?
- What constraint would create gradients inside the search space so the system can tell when a question is getting warmer?
- What constraint would prevent the system from treating every adjacent idea as equally promising?
- What constraint would turn free association into structured recombination?
- What constraint would make the question arc-consistent so every remaining path can still lead to a viable insight?
- What constraint would reveal that the remaining space is too small for novelty?
- What constraint would reveal that the remaining space is too large for disciplined search?
- What question asks whether the constraint itself is pruning truth or pruning only noise?

## What Is The Mechanism Constraint?
- What mechanism must be real before this question deserves a build?
- What mechanism would make the question portable across domains rather than metaphorical across domains?
- What mechanism would explain why a constraint improves reasoning instead of merely limiting behavior?
- What mechanism would distinguish a useful bottleneck from an arbitrary rule?
- What mechanism would show that the question is about repair, memory, attention, energy, scale, verification, or institutional coordination?
- What mechanism would survive if the original threshold, benchmark, or dataset changed?
- What mechanism would be visible in both a successful case and a failed case?
- What mechanism would predict when the constraint becomes over-restriction?
- What mechanism would predict when extra freedom becomes paralysis?
- What mechanism would make this question worth carrying into the next meditation pass?

## What Is The Evidence Constraint?
- What evidence already exists on disk that this question has not interrogated carefully enough?
- What evidence would make the question sharper without making it premature?
- What evidence would separate a science demo from deployment proof?
- What evidence would show that a reasoning improvement survives outside the protected conditions that made it look good?
- What evidence would show that a build crossed from TRL-style concept validation into relevant-environment demonstration?
- What evidence would expose whether a success depends on a hidden ecosystem, customer, data, hardware, or governance assumption?
- What evidence would force the question to name the scale at which the claim currently fails?
- What evidence would demote the question from build-ready to study-only?
- What evidence would promote the question from study-only to cheap probe?
- What evidence would make the next question ask for a kill condition before a roadmap?

## What Is The Strict-Metric Constraint?
- What metric would make this question incapable of hiding behind vibes?
- What metric would distinguish a better reasoning paradigm from a better benchmark trick?
- What metric would show whether memory, replay, repair, compression, or diffusion adds value after cost is counted?
- What metric would detect whether the question improved decision quality rather than narrative quality?
- What metric would reveal that a constraint produced robustness instead of only lower variance?
- What metric would reveal that a constraint produced conformity instead of creativity?
- What metric would make a negative result useful enough to keep?
- What metric would expose when a public claim is stronger than the evidence ledger permits?
- What metric would let the project compare a bounded probe against a full GPU run?
- What metric would make the next question accountable to the repository rather than to ambition?

## What Is The Wedge Constraint?
- What is the smallest wedge where this question could change a real decision?
- What is the beachhead customer equivalent inside a reasoning repo?
- What question identifies the first user, workflow, benchmark, or artifact that would actually care if the idea worked?
- What wedge would let the system learn without pretending to solve general intelligence?
- What wedge would force the project to choose between verification infrastructure, research cockpit, scoped artifact, durable memory test, geometry diagnostic, energy-aware policy, or stigmergic coordination?
- What wedge would prove value before asking for a broad architecture?
- What wedge would expose whether the problem is technical, economic, operational, institutional, or epistemic?
- What wedge would make scale a later consequence rather than an early assumption?
- What wedge would fail quickly enough to teach the next question?
- What wedge would prevent a breakthrough list from becoming a build queue without proof paths?

## What Is The Failure-Mode Constraint?
- What failure mode must be named before this question deserves another iteration?
- What is the valley-of-death equivalent for this reasoning paradigm?
- What scaling wall would appear only after a convincing prototype?
- What hidden ecosystem would have to exist before the idea could matter?
- What unit-economics equivalent would make a reasoning gain too expensive to deploy?
- What manufacturing-readiness equivalent would separate an elegant proof object from a usable system?
- What customer-readiness equivalent would show that no workflow is waiting for the improvement?
- What regulatory-readiness equivalent would show that governance, provenance, or auditability is behind the capability claim?
- What failure mode would be invisible if the project only studies successful examples?
- What question would force every promising idea to carry its most likely death pattern beside it?

## What Is The Energy Constraint?
- What is the scarce energy budget of this question?
- What part of the reasoning process spends the most energy moving information instead of transforming it?
- What is the token, context, attention, GPU, and human-review cost of keeping this question alive?
- What question asks whether the reasoning architecture is spending too much on communication and too little on computation?
- What question asks whether memory and compute are colocated enough to avoid wasteful retrieval?
- What question asks whether replay reduces future energy cost or only adds another maintenance burden?
- What question asks whether a cheap diagnostic can replace a costly repair attempt?
- What question asks whether the system has an operating point where more compute makes it less disciplined?
- What question asks whether the project is optimizing total capability or capability per watt, per token, per hour, and per verified claim?
- What energy constraint would make the next question respect the cost of being asked?

## What Is The Creativity Constraint?
- What constraint would make this question more creative rather than merely narrower?
- What constraint would force unexpected recombination between memory, diffusion, geometry, energy, verification, and institutional learning?
- What constraint would act like a sonnet form for reasoning research by forcing a turn at the right point?
- What constraint would act like a fugue subject by making every later question transform the same core theme?
- What constraint would act like a blues progression by making small deviations meaningful?
- What constraint would act like dropout by forcing the idea to work without one fragile support?
- What constraint would act like weight decay by removing oversized claims?
- What constraint would act like sparse coding by making every retained question earn its metabolic keep?
- What constraint would preserve enough freedom for novelty and enough restriction for navigability?
- What question asks whether the meditations themselves need a formal constraint grammar?

- What assumption changes when the question moves from one benchmark to a suite?
- What assumption changes when the question moves from one repo to an institution?
- What assumption changes when the question moves from one session to long-term memory?
- What assumption changes when the question moves from prototype to daily workflow?
- What hidden gradient, oxygen-transfer, ecosystem, or unit-economics equivalent appears only at scale?
- What question would force the project to test whether local repair degrades global coordination?
- What question would force the project to test whether a memory rule survives accumulation?
- What question would force the project to test whether a verification rule survives adversarial use?
- What question would make scale a source of questions before it becomes a source of claims?

## What Is The Capital Constraint?
- What is the patient-capital equivalent for a reasoning moonshot?
- What would fund this question through the long middle between interesting demo and dependable system?
- What committed-customer equivalent would prove that the question matters outside the research loop?
- What government-bridge equivalent would support work that is true but not yet productizable?
- What structural support is missing if the question needs more time than ordinary execution cycles allow?
- What question asks whether the project is using commits, docs, benchmarks, and artifacts as capital discipline?
- What question asks whether a build is being asked to cross a valley without a bridge?
- What question asks whether excitement is being used as a substitute for runway?
- What question asks whether the question has enough institutional support to survive a boring middle?
- What question asks whether the repo can remember why the question mattered after the initial momentum fades?
