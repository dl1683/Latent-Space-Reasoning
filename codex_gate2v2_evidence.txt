## Gamma is mislabeled

The reported

\[
\Gamma=TV(AM,MA)-TV(AM,\hat{AM})-TV(MA,\hat{MA})
\]

is a lower bound on \(TV(\hat{AM},\hat{MA})\) by the triangle inequality. In this experiment, positive Gamma means the filler-derived positional predictions themselves remain separated. It is not a lower bound on the commutator remaining after subtracting position effects.

Therefore “positive in 14/24 contexts” does not support the controlled claim.

## Strongest rival explanation

The strongest rival is **position/recency followed by context-dependent nonlinear gain or saturation**.

Post-hoc diagnostics show:

- Mean per-context cosine between observed and filler-predicted order vectors: 0.9962.
- The predicted vector generally points in almost exactly the observed direction but is too large.
- Fitting one scalar gain per context reduces mean residual TV to 0.00759; only 6/24 contexts remain above 0.01.

That gain fit is outcome-fitted, so it is not itself decisive. But it supplies the cheapest prospective rival: predict the gain from baseline/context features on training contexts and test it on held-out contexts and independently frozen fillers.

## Theory: what noncommutativity would imply

Even after repair, one noncommuting pair implies only a noncommutative **surface-indexed transition action**. If closure and behavioral congruence are established, it descends to a transformation monoid \(S\) containing elements \(a,m\) with:

\[
am\ne ma.
\]

It does not imply a group, Lie algebra, dimensional lower bound, nonlinearity, semantic classes, or native mathematics. Every sequential transducer already has a transition monoid.

The next equations should be tested prospectively:

\[
K_\epsilon=I,\qquad K_{uv}=K_v\circ K_u,
\]

followed by candidate relations such as:

\[
a^2\approx a,\quad m^2\approx m,
\]

\[
ama\approx am,\quad mam\approx ma
\]

(absorption/left-regular-band behavior),

\[
am\approx m\quad\text{or}\quad am\approx a
\]

(overwrite/recency),

\[
ama\approx mam
\]

(braid-like behavior), plus conditional commutation and eventual periodicity of powers.

The path to native algebra is:

1. Define response equivalence from future behavior, not human semantic labels.
2. Establish closure/lumpability so each surface induces a well-defined action.
3. Discover a presentation \(S=\langle a,m,\ldots\mid R\rangle\) from short words.
4. Predict held-out longer words and held-out representatives using \(R\), beating last-token, length, n-gram, nonlinear-recency, and lookup baselines.
5. Show representative independence.
6. Realize the same relations causally at an internal relay state.

That response-defined quotient and its stable predictive relations—not fitted matrices, cosine, or TV themselves—would be the native object.
