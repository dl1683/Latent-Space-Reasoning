# Question Frontier?

## What Makes A Question Frontier-Worthy?

- What question could change the most downstream beliefs if sharpened?
- What question could kill the most overclaim if answered negatively?
- What question could turn an existing mystery into a clean result without new compute?
- What question could expose whether a claimed paradigm shift is only improved engineering?
- What question could become public without depending on hidden confidence?
- What question could make the next artifact more honest rather than more impressive?

## Which Question Has The Highest Falsification Yield?

- What question is most likely to retract a load-bearing claim if asked seriously?
- What question would be valuable even if the answer is no?
- What question has existing evidence on disk that has not been fully interrogated?
- What question would convert a weak headline into a stronger negative result?
- What question would catch an overclaim before an external reader does?
- What question would make the portfolio's self-correction discipline visible rather than accidental?
- What question would improve credibility by reducing ambition?

## Which Question Has The Lowest Compute Cost?

- What question can be answered by rereading existing artifacts?
- What question can be answered by rescoring existing outputs?
- What question can be answered by analyzing abandoned data?
- What question can be answered by comparing already-written claims against already-recorded evidence?
- What question requires only a cheap probe rather than a full run?
- What question would be irresponsible to send to the GPU before cheaper observations are exhausted?
- What question best honors the single-laptop constraint as a scientific discipline?

## Which Question Tests Structure Over Scale?

- What question asks whether better geometry is doing work rather than more compute?
- What question distinguishes a structural mechanism from a lucky threshold?
- What question asks whether information allocation changed before performance changed?
- What question asks whether a small intervention reveals hidden state rather than adds capability?
- What question asks whether representation quality predicts task behavior across held-out systems?
- What question would fail if the result only comes from brute-force sampling?
- What question would make scale unnecessary if the theory is right?

## Which Question Tests State Over Output?

- What question asks whether internal state quality improved before output quality did?
- What question asks whether blackboard structure, memory, or replay explains the gain?
- What question asks whether output polish hides state degradation?
- What question asks whether a system can preserve useful state across sessions, agents, or benchmarks?
- What question asks whether the next output is merely better or the next state is more repairable?
- What question would reveal whether final-answer evaluation is the wrong object?
- What question would force the system to inspect its state before celebrating its answer?

## Which Question Tests Cross-Project Unification?

- What question appears independently in LSR, UESD, pruning, memory, and legal reasoning?
- What question would show whether attention sinks and wrong attractors are the same basin problem?
- What question would show whether source trust and institutional trust are the same verification problem?
- What question would show whether replay in memory and replay in benchmarks are the same maintenance problem?
- What question would show whether orthogonal verification is the shared primitive behind science, law, and AI?
- What question would unify domains only if the mechanism matches rather than the metaphor?
- What question would break a tempting analogy before it becomes theory?

## Which Question Has The Sharpest Boundary?

- What question has a clear object?
- What question has a clear falsifier?
- What question has a clear cost boundary?
- What question has a clear evidence boundary?
- What question has a clear promotion boundary?
- What question has a clear public-claim boundary?
- What question is currently too boundaryless to deserve more attention?

## Which Question Is Too Dangerous To Leave Vague?

- What question could mislead future agents if it remains poetic?
- What question could support an inflated README sentence if it remains vague?
- What question could justify a GPU run without a spend gate if it remains vague?
- What question could turn a diagnostic artifact into a promoted claim if it remains vague?
- What question could make a negative result look like failure rather than progress if it remains vague?
- What question could let a falsified thesis survive as atmosphere?
- What question must be narrowed before it becomes a task?

## Which Question Should Be Deferred?

- What question needs evidence that does not yet exist?
- What question needs a benchmark that has not yet been trusted?
- What question needs a verifier that has not yet been built?
- What question needs a theory that would currently be mostly metaphor?
- What question needs more compute than its current value justifies?
- What question is interesting but not controlling any near decision?
- What question would be better preserved as a stepping stone than promoted as a frontier?

## Which Question Should Be Killed?

- What question has become a slogan?
- What question has become a loyalty test?
- What question has become a way to avoid a known falsifier?
- What question has become detached from any artifact, metric, or mechanism?
- What question has already been answered negatively but keeps returning under new wording?
- What question consumes attention because it is beautiful rather than because it is useful?
- What question would make the frontier cleaner by disappearing?

## Which Question Should Become A Public Method?

- What question demonstrates the falsify-and-pivot discipline?
- What question shows how a research operation catches its own overclaims?
- What question can be shown with evidence that improves under adversarial scrutiny?
- What question converts a local retraction into a reusable method?
- What question would make negative results legible as intellectual progress?
- What question would teach future readers how to distrust the work productively?
- What question would be more valuable as a methodology than as a result?

## Which Question Should The Next Frontier Pass Ask?

- What question should be compared against every other question before more are written?
- What question would rank the current frontier by falsification yield, compute cost, boundary clarity, and public value?
- What question would reveal whether the frontier is becoming a portfolio strategy rather than a meditation?
- What question would reveal whether a frontier question is ready to become evidence work?
- What question would reveal whether a frontier question should remain purely contemplative?
- What question would reveal whether the next pass should add questions, delete questions, or split questions?
- What question would make the question frontier itself easier to falsify?
