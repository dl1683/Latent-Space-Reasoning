# Question Taste And Discernment?

- What if taste is not preference but a fast prior over which questions are likely to compress reality without losing the important residue?
- What if taste is the learned ability to feel when a question has too many degrees of freedom?
- What if taste is the learned ability to feel when a question has become overconstrained?
- What if taste is a search heuristic trained by prior contact with truth, failure, elegance, embarrassment, and use?
- What if taste is the tacit memory of which constraints previously opened a productive search space?
- What if taste is dangerous exactly because it feels like perception rather than inference?
- What if taste must always ask what experience trained it?
