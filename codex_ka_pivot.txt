## 1. Strongest honest claim

A defensible claim is:

> In one model and one registered code-prompt family, denotationally null suffix text causes a reproducible change in the immediate answer distribution. One fixed surface exhibits a correction-like pattern that transfers from depths 2–3 to depth 4, while different surfaces—even within the same human semantic class—produce materially different magnitudes. Sequential suffix experiments show no content-dependent noncommutativity beyond positional and context-dependent gain.

The evidence also supports saying that the registered content contrast was 6.3 times the particular variable-mention contrast tested. It does not establish that “semantics dominates confounds” generally.

It does **not** support:

- a semantic operator \(K_A\) shared by an ASSERT class;
- proof relevance or EqWit sensitivity;
- a transformation-monoid representation;
- a causal internal relay state;
- a model-native coordinate system;
- cross-task, cross-model, or long-depth universality;
- literal transport of 72% of “shadow probability mass.”

That last interpretation is especially unsafe: the \(L\to C\) coefficient is the fitted matrix’s behavior at a pure-\(L\) simplex vertex, while the observed inputs occupy only a restricted region. It is an extrapolated coefficient, not directly observed token mass flowing between bins.

## 2. Is K native mathematics?

No—not with current evidence.

There are two distinct things here:

- **Model evidence:** the output distribution really changes, and a constrained rule predicts part of that change across depth.
- **Researcher construction:** C/L/R, the simplex, stochastic K, the selected shadow token, and the diagonal normal form are all imposed coordinates.

If \(K_a\) becomes \(\operatorname{diag}(1,a,1)\) after a coordinate change, that does not make the observation false. Classical mathematics can describe a genuine model regularity. But it means the mathematical machinery itself is ordinary linear/Markov algebra, not a new structure discovered from the latent world.

More importantly, approximate lumpability has not been demonstrated. The current “lumpability \(R^2\)” calculation regresses averaged post-\(L\) on baseline \(L\); it does not test whether two distinct underlying histories with the same projected state receive the same projected successor ([analyze_lumpability.py](</C:/Users/devan/OneDrive/Desktop/Projects/Latent-Space-Reasoning/experiments/analyze_lumpability.py:104>)). The formal framework correctly says that a projected operator exists only under representative independence; otherwise K is merely a fitted summary ([INTENSIONAL_DESCENT.md](</C:/Users/devan/OneDrive/Desktop/Projects/Latent-Space-Reasoning/theory/INTENSIONAL_DESCENT.md:342>)).

Surface CV of 0.33–0.51 further blocks the notation \(K_A\). At most, the evidence licenses \(K_s\) for a particular literal surface \(s\).

So the verdict is:

> K is a promising behavioral compression of one response phenomenon, not yet a well-defined quotient action and not a native latent-space primitive.

The README’s statement that lumpability demonstrates “well-defined transforms” and a native coordinate system should therefore be treated as an overclaim ([README.md](</C:/Users/devan/OneDrive/Desktop/Projects/Latent-Space-Reasoning/README.md:32>)).

## 4. Better mathematical framing

The exact object presently supported is a **surface-indexed transition correspondence**:

\[
\mathcal F_s(p)
=
\{\,r(T_s z):r(z)=p\,\}.
\]

It is set-valued because representative independence has not been established. The experiments suggest that this correspondence may be thin along the sampled natural-data manifold.

If the next test establishes approximate single-valuedness, the least inflated framing would be:

- a controlled, non-autonomous response system;
- a context-indexed operator field;
- or a family of calibration channels sharing an approximate correction direction but carrying surface- and position-dependent amplitudes.

Schematically:

\[
p_{t+1}=F_{s,t,h}(p_t),
\]

where surface \(s\), position \(t\), and history class \(h\) remain explicit.

“Depth-invariant correction direction with surface-specific magnitude” suggests a common low-rank response mode whose coupling coefficient depends on the textual realization. That is closer to a constitutive response law than to a monoid or group.

Calling it a category, bundle, or cocycle now would mostly rename the context dependence. Those structures become meaningful only if they predict new compatibility relations that then survive intervention.
