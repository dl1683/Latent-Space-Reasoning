## A. What native latent mathematics should look like

“Native” cannot mean “impossible to encode in \(\mathbb R^n\).” Any finite digital model can be encoded numerically. It should mean that the mathematical objects and laws are induced by the model’s legal computations and observable consequences, rather than by an arbitrary activation chart.

Make the complete response tree primary:

\[
\operatorname{Beh}(x)(w,c)=r_c(T_wx).
\]

Then define:

\[
x\equiv_h y
\iff
r_c(T_wx)=r_c(T_wy)
\quad
\forall c,\ |w|\le h,
\]

and the distinguishing-future language

\[
\operatorname{Sep}(x,y)
=
\{w:\exists c,\ r_c(T_wx)\neq r_c(T_wy)\}.
\]

Under the project’s word-order convention,

\[
\operatorname{Sep}(T_ax,T_ay)
=
\{w:wa\in\operatorname{Sep}(x,y)\}.
\]

That derivative is a native notion of direction: applying \(a\) changes which future experiments can distinguish two places.

A denizen would consequently invent:

- **Place:** equivalence under available futures, not activation proximity.
- **Direction:** how a move transforms the language of distinguishing futures.
- **Distance/cost:** the shortest or cheapest legal continuation that separates or reaches a state. This is generally asymmetric.
- **Map:** a compressed machine predicting the response tree of unexecuted continuations.
- **Law:** representative-independent relations among typed moves—conditional commutation, absorption, idempotence, refinement, or erasure—that predict unseen compositions.
- **Geometry:** the refinement tower
  \[
  Q_0 \leftarrow Q_1 \leftarrow Q_2 \leftarrow\cdots,
  \]
  together with reachability and causal dependency, rather than a manifold fitted around activation vectors.

This is related to automata and coalgebra, as the existing theory already acknowledges. The field-changing contribution would not be renaming those subjects. It would be discovering a small, causally realized presentation of a real model’s continuation process—or establishing that current architectures do not admit one. ## D. Intensional Descent: keep the kernel, replace the architecture

The framework is half right.

Keep:

- D1–D6’s refusal to assume vector, metric, or manifold structure.
- The full future-response map and \(Q_M\).
- External denotation \(E\) as a separately declared semantics.
- Causal realization and representative independence as promotion requirements.

Change:

1. **Replace the strict chain.**

   The current claim requires

   \[
   \Delta_{\mathcal P}\subsetneq{\equiv_M}\subsetneq{\equiv_E}.
   \]

   That assumes a model never behaviorally conflates denotationally different operations. An imperfect model can simultaneously over-distinguish equal programs and under-distinguish unequal programs.

   Instead compare two kernel relations:

   \[
   R_M=\ker(\alpha),\qquad R_E=\ker(E).
   \]

   Then study:

   - \(R_E\setminus R_M\): history or presentation sensitivity;
   - \(R_M\setminus R_E\): semantic blindness or competence failure;
   - \(R_M\cap R_E\): jointly erased distinctions.

   A factor \(I\to D\) should be an empirical success condition, not the starting ontology.

2. **Replace the one-object monoid with a graded process category.**

   Objects should include token position, execution phase, available history, and response interface. A suffix at two positions is then represented by two appropriately typed arrows. The positional-gain result is evidence for this typing, not merely a nuisance coefficient.

3. **Make equality of response trees primary.**

   \(d_\infty\), TV, Fisher geometry, and regression can remain experimental tools, but not foundational ontology. D7 explicitly imports manifolds, tangent spaces, Fisher metrics, and Finsler-type seminorms; that entire branch is R^n scaffolding and should be demoted from the native core. [AXIOMS.md](<C:/Users/devan/OneDrive/Desktop/Projects/Latent-Space-Reasoning/theory/AXIOMS.md:327>).

4. **Add the finite-access refinement tower.**

   Exact \(Q_M\) is an omniscient external construction. A denizen has \(Q_h\), discovered through bounded futures, plus proofs or invariants controlling unseen branches. The existing finite-access theorem already explains why finite agreement cannot certify identity. [AXIOMS.md](<C:/Users/devan/OneDrive/Desktop/Projects/Latent-Space-Reasoning/theory/AXIOMS.md:1279>).

5. **Require a finite predictive presentation.**

   A quotient existing definitionally is not a discovery. Promotion requires compression over raw history, prediction of unseen derivatives/compositions, presentation transfer, and causal realization.

So: **\(Q_M\) is the right semantic north star, but the present IDC factorization is too clean for the actual model and the metric/differential extensions re-enter the R^n tunnel.**

## E. Is Qwen3-1.7B the wrong substrate?

Native mathematics is not proven impossible in Qwen. But a compact stationary algebra at the tested interfaces now looks unlikely.

The likely structural hole is:

\[
\text{full state}=\text{growing, position-indexed KV history}
\quad\neq\quad
\text{single residual or CLR response slice}.
\]

Later attention can reread surface history that the coarse response projection appeared to forget. That predicts exactly the observed combination:

- apparent equivalence split by a later suffix;
- strong position dependence;
- padding-sensitive interaction;
- weak interchangeability at single residual ports;
- failure on tasks demanding explicit composition.

The project’s own port-compression note identifies this possibility, although it remains a conjecture rather than a proved result. [AXIOMS.md](<C:/Users/devan/OneDrive/Desktop/Projects/Latent-Space-Reasoning/theory/AXIOMS.md:2456>). Earlier Qwen results are consistent with distributed whole-sequence computation and repeated single-port failures, but do not prove impossibility. [STATE.md](<C:/Users/devan/OneDrive/Desktop/Projects/Latent-Space-Reasoning/STATE.md:864>).

A plain SSM is not the answer: fixed-state recurrence improves Markov closure, but a diagonal or linear recurrence is already well described by linear systems and spectral theory.

The better substrate is a **looped or recurrent transformer with a fixed explicit workspace**:

- tied update across reasoning steps;
- fixed typed slots or graph-structured state;
- raw-history access cut off after compilation;
- explicit read/write/query ports;
- permutation or renaming symmetries built into the interface;
- tasks requiring held-out composition rather than memorized endpoint prediction.

That architecture does not guarantee new mathematics. It creates the conditions under which stable operational identity, iteration, reachability, and composition can exist.
